# Recognition with a homogeneous relation among three good speeds

Let \(E\) be an actual locally nilpotent derivation of
\(B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,z]\), with \(E^2x_i=0\) for \(i\le3\)
and \(E^2z=q\in\mathbb C^*\). If the three good speeds
\(b_i=Ex_i\) satisfy a nonzero homogeneous polynomial relation,
then \(\ker E\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\). A polynomial slice is automatic:
\(u=Ez/q\) satisfies \(Eu=1\).

The proof separates rank-zero and rank-one speeds from a rank-two
conical speed image. In the remaining rank-one and conical cases,
the actual coordinate geometry prevents every generic divisor of a
smaller polynomial three-ring from being missed. The separate
actual-commutator critical-point argument then supplies generic degree
one. Neither the relative generation statement nor the universal
pencil alone is treated as recognition.

## 1. Actual setup and the two branches

Use the complete actual hypotheses of the
[invariant-plane lemma88](constant-acceleration-invariant-plane-pass88.md).
The [conical normal](constant-acceleration-conical-normal-pass89.md) or
[curve-speed normal](constant-acceleration-curve-speed-pass89.md) construction gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,z]=A[u],\qquad Eu=1,\quad Ez=qu,
\quad q\in\mathbb C^*,\\
Ex_{\rm tr}=b\in S^3,\qquad S=\ker(W|A)=\mathbb C[s_1,s_2],
\quad Wu=0,\quad (b_1,b_2,b_3)S=S,\\
x_{\rm tr}=b\sigma+VY+U_0c_0,\qquad
[b,V,U_0]\in\operatorname{GL}_3(S),\quad c_0\in S,\\
A=S[Y,c],\qquad R=S[Y],\qquad
h=u-\sigma\in A\cap\operatorname{Frac}(R),\qquad
z=c+\frac q2(u^2-h^2).
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
Both \(E\) and \(W\) are actual LNDs. The pencil gives a nilpotent
tensor difference of \(h\), and all hypotheses of
[pole-support89](constant-acceleration-pole-support-pass89.md) hold.

If \(Y\) is algebraic over \(\operatorname{Frac}(S)\), the predecessor
already recognizes \(A=S[c]\). Assume below that \(Y\) is
transcendental, so \(R\) is a polynomial three-ring. We treat two
speed possibilities separately.

* In the rank-one case, the actual polynomial curve factorization is
  \(b_i=f_i(p)\), with \(p\in S\setminus\mathbb C\) and
  \(f_i\in\mathbb C[t]\).
* In the rank-two conical case,
  \(\operatorname{rank}[b_{s_1},b_{s_2}]=2\) and
  \(\det[b,b_{s_1},b_{s_2}]=0\).

These are restrictions on the supplied actual frame, not alternatives
proved for arbitrary sliced actions.

## 2. Rank-one speeds: a polynomial in the three good variables cannot be invariant

First dispose of two retained recognized subcases. If the \(f_i\)
have a nonzero complex linear relation, the same constant linear
combination of \(x_1,x_2,x_3\) is an actual invariant ambient
coordinate. The regular-kernel theorem recorded in the
[source85 audit](rank-one-quasi-nice-source-scope-pass85.md) recognizes \(A\).
Regularity is supplied by \(A[u]=B\).
If instead \(\partial_zp=0\), the actual good-coordinate three-ring
is \(E\)-stable. Its nice LND restriction gives an invariant
constant linear form by Dasgupta--Gupta Theorem 3.1(i), and the
same recognition gate applies.[^nice]

In the remaining rank-one case the three \(f_i\) are linearly
independent over \(\mathbb C\) and \(\partial_zp\ne0\).
Suppose \(k\in\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]\cap\ker E\).
Over \(K=\mathbb C(x_1,x_2,x_3)\), the actual polynomial \(p(z)\)
is nonconstant in \(z\), hence transcendental over \(K\).
Thus the substitution \(K[t]\to K[z]\), \(t\mapsto p(z)\),
is injective. The invariant equation gives
\[
0=\sum_{i=1}^3 k_{x_i}f_i(p(z))
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{i=1}^3 k_{x_i}f_i(t)=0\text{ in }K[t]
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k_{x_i}=0\text{ for all }i.
\tag{2}
\]
The final step is legitimate over \(K\): a nonzero complex
three-by-three coefficient minor witnessing independence of the
\(f_i\) remains nonzero under this field extension. Characteristic
zero then gives \(k\in\mathbb C\).

## 3. A nonlinear conical speed image has the same omission property

In the rank-two conical case, the image closure of \(b\) has a
principal prime relation ideal generated by an irreducible ternary
polynomial \(F\). That polynomial can be chosen homogeneous:
the Jacobian of \(tb\) in \(s_1,s_2,t\) has rank two, so both
evaluation kernels for \(b\) and for \(tb\) are height-one primes.
The latter is homogeneous and is contained in the former; the
two are therefore equal. If \(F\) has degree one, it gives an
actual invariant constant linear coordinate and recognizes \(A\)
by the same retained gate. Assume in what follows that
\[
F\in\mathbb C[T_1,T_2,T_3]\text{ is irreducible homogeneous},
\qquad \deg F>1,\qquad F(b)=0.
\tag{3}
\]

Suppose a nonconstant \(k\in\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]\cap\ker E\)
exists. Put again \(K=\mathbb C(x_1,x_2,x_3)\). Its gradient
is a nonzero row over \(K\), and
\[
\nabla k\cdot b(z)=0,\qquad F(b(z))=0,
\qquad b(z)\in K[z]^3,\qquad (b_1,b_2,b_3)K[z]=K[z].
\tag{4}
\]
The first equation places this polynomial curve in a fixed
two-dimensional linear subspace of \(K^3\). The restriction of
\(F\) to that plane is not the zero polynomial. Otherwise its
linear defining equation would divide \(F\) after scalar extension,
which is impossible: an irreducible polynomial over the
algebraically closed field \(\mathbb C\) is absolutely irreducible,
and its degree is greater than one.

Over an algebraic closure \(\overline K\), the nonzero homogeneous
binary restriction factors into linear forms. The domain
\(\overline K[z]\) and (4) force one such factor to vanish on
the entire curve \(b(z)\). Thus that curve lies in a fixed line
through the origin. Write \(b(z)=v\,a(z)\) for a nonzero constant
vector \(v\in\overline K^3\) and \(a(z)\in\overline K[z]\).
The extended unit row in (4) makes \(a(z)\) a unit, hence a
nonzero constant. Consequently \(\partial_z b_i=0\) for every \(i\).

The restriction of \(E\) to the actual
\(\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]\) is therefore a nonzero nice LND.
Theorem 3.1(i) just cited produces a nonzero complex constant
linear relation among the \(b_i\). This contradicts the fact that
their prime relation ideal is generated by the nonlinear \(F\).
We have proved, in both remaining branches,
\[
\boxed{\quad \ker E\cap\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]
       =\ker E\cap\ker(\partial_z)=\mathbb C.\quad}
\tag{5}
\]
The conical proof does not assume a coordinate status for either
binary parameter, nor a quasi-translation in four variables.

## 4. Every generic divisor is met, and the slice difference descends

The generalized conclusion of pole-support89 says that if
\(p\in S\) is irreducible and the image of \(Y\) in
\(A_{S_{(p)}}/pA_{S_{(p)}}\) is algebraic over
\(\operatorname{Frac}(S/(p))\), then \(\partial_zp=0\).
The element \(p\) is a nonconstant \(E\)-invariant, so (5)
rules this out. Therefore that image of \(Y\) is transcendental.
Since \(pA\) is prime by the factorial-closure argument in
pole-support89, coefficient reduction in polynomials in \(Y\) gives
\[
(pA)\cap R=pR.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus the prime \(pA\) lies over the generic point of the vertical
divisor \(pR\).

For any other height-one prime of \(R=S[Y]\), its contraction to
\(S\) is zero. Indeed an irreducible polynomial in \(R\) dividing
a nonzero element of \(S\) would itself be an irreducible factor
from \(S\). Localize now at \(S\setminus\{0\}\).
As in pole-support89, the extension
\(K_0[Y]\subset A_{K_0}=K_0[v]\) is finite and integral.
Lying over and contraction therefore give a prime of \(A\)
above each such remaining height-one prime of \(R\).
Together with (6), this proves that every generic divisor of
\(\operatorname{Spec}R\) is met by \(\operatorname{Spec}A\).

This is enough for intersection descent, without global finiteness
or faithful flatness over \(R\). If \(g\in A\cap\operatorname{Frac}(R)\)
had a pole at a height-one prime \(\mathfrak p\), choose a prime
\(\mathfrak q\) of \(A\) above \(\mathfrak p\).
In the DVR \(R_{\mathfrak p}\), write \(g=\pi^{-m}v\) with
\(m\ge1\) and \(v\) a unit. The inclusion into \(A_{\mathfrak q}\)
would give \(\pi^{-1}=\pi^{m-1}g/v\), contradicting
\(\pi\in\mathfrak qA_{\mathfrak q}\).
Normality of \(R\), or just reduced-denominator factorization
in this polynomial UFD, yields
\[
\boxed{\quad A\cap\operatorname{Frac}(R)=R,\qquad h\in R=S[Y].\quad}
\tag{7}
\]

Equation (7) alone does not identify \(A=R[c]\) with \(R\).
It does imply that identification, and hence recognition, if
\(\operatorname{Frac}(A)=\operatorname{Frac}(R)\) is established
separately. Sections 5 and 6 supply that missing fact under the
actual hypotheses here. This intermediate argument does not assume
global finiteness, surjectivity at every higher-codimension point, or
faithful flatness of \(R\to A\).

## 5. The actual rank-one commutator finishes that branch

Return to the remaining rank-one case of Section 2. Write
\(\alpha=\partial_zp\ne0\), where \(p\) is its actual speed-curve
parameter, not a divisor variable from Section 4.
The full [curve-speed proof89](constant-acceleration-curve-speed-pass89.md)
proves that \(Q=[E,\partial_z]\) is an actual LND with
\(\ker(Q|A)=S\), \(Qu=0\), and \(\alpha\in S\).
Its original transverse coefficient column is
\(Q(x_{\rm tr})=-\alpha f'(p)\in S^3\).

Because \(Q\) kills \(S\), applying it to the actual inverse matrix
in (1) puts both \(Q\sigma\) and \(QY\) in \(S\). If \(QY\)
were zero, the reconstruction would give
\(-\alpha f'(p)=b\,Q\sigma\). Since \(\alpha\ne0\), this would
make \(f\) and \(f'\) proportional, contradicting their nonzero
cross product proved in the curve-speed note.
Consequently
\[
d:=QY\in S\setminus\{0\},\qquad
Q(Y/d)=1\text{ on }A_d,\qquad
A_d=S_d[Y],\qquad
\operatorname{Frac}(A)=\operatorname{Frac}(R).
\tag{8}
\]
The kernel after this localization is exactly \(S_d\).
The usual slice theorem for the actual LND \(Q\) then proves
the displayed polynomial presentation, since \(d\) is inverted.
It is not assumed that \(d\) is a global unit or that \(Q\)
already has a global slice.

Now (7) and (8) give \(A=R\). Equivalently, (8) puts \(c\)
in \(\operatorname{Frac}(S)[Y]\); a coefficient pole would make
\(\overline Y\) algebraic on that divisor fiber. The generalized
pole-support argument, using the tensor nilpotence of \(h\),
would force a nonconstant invariant in the three good variables,
contrary to Section 2. Thus no coefficient pole remains and
\[
\boxed{\quad A=S[Y]\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}
\quad\text{in the actual rank-one-speed branch.}\quad}
\tag{9}
\]
The rank-zero-speed case is also already recognized by its
nonzero constant good speed and original linear coordinate slice.
These statements concern the actual three-good-coordinate frame
with nonzero constant \(q\). They impose no ordinary spatial-degree
bound on its coefficients, and do not cover every higher-rank
constant-acceleration action.

## 6. The original commutator also closes the conical branch

The complete [relative degree-one proof89](constant-acceleration-relative-degree-one-pass89.md)
applies to both remaining branches of (1). Its hypotheses include the
actual nonzero LND \(Q=[E,\partial_z]\), with
\(\ker(Q|A)=S\) and \(Qu=0\), as proved in the curve-speed and
conical-good-direction predecessors. If \(J\) is the original
four-coordinate Jacobian of the velocity vector, the actual identities
are \(Q(x)=-Je_4\) and \(JQ(x)=0\).
These are identities of original polynomial coefficients, not
conclusions inferred from a transformed Jacobian.

Here is the decisive step, retaining its specialization gate.
Put \(K_0=\operatorname{Frac}(S)\). The actual LND slice theorem gives
\(A_{K_0}=K_0[v]=K_0[Y,c]\) and
\(Q=\lambda\partial_v\), where \(\lambda\in K_0^*\).
Since \(Y\) is nonconstant in \(v\), \(K_0[v]\) is integral over
\(K_0[Y]\). The pencil puts \(h\) in \(K_0(Y)\), so normality gives
\(h\in K_0[Y]\), even without the global conclusion (7).

If \(Y'(v_0)=0\) at a geometric point, generation
\(v=G(Y,c)\) implies \(c'(v_0)\ne0\) by differentiation.
The reconstruction (1) then makes the specialized original coefficient
column \(Q(x)\) equal to \(\mu e_4\), where
\(\mu=\lambda c'(v_0)\ne0\). All coefficients lie in
\(K_0[u,v]\), so this specialization is legitimate in
\(\overline K_0[u]\), with no denominator in \(v\).
The identity \(JQ(x)=0\) forces \(Je_4=0\) after specialization,
contradicting \(Q(x)=-Je_4=\mu e_4\).
In characteristic zero a polynomial of degree at least two has a
geometric critical point. Therefore
\[
\deg_vY=1,\qquad A_{K_0}=K_0[Y],\qquad
\operatorname{Frac}(A)=\operatorname{Frac}(R),\qquad
A=R=S[Y]\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}.
\tag{10}
\]
The last equality uses (7), not generic degree one alone.
No global slice for \(Q\), preservation of \(S\) by \(\partial_z\),
or generic change promoted to original coordinates is assumed.

## 7. Exhaustion under the homogeneous-speed relation and provenance

The preceding arguments prove the promised original-frame statement:
\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
E\in\operatorname{LND}\bigl(\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,z]\bigr),\qquad
E^2x_1=E^2x_2=E^2x_3=0,\quad E^2z\in\mathbb C^*,\\
0\ne F\in\mathbb C[T_1,T_2,T_3]\ \text{homogeneous},\quad
F(Ex_1,Ex_2,Ex_3)=0\\
\Longrightarrow\quad
\ker E\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}.
\end{gathered}}
\tag{11}
\]
To check exhaustion, the invariant-plane lemma puts the unit speed
row \(b\) in the actual polynomial plane \(S\), so its differential
rank is at most two. Rank zero supplies a nonzero constant good speed,
hence an original linear coordinate slice. Rank one is covered by
Sections 2 and 5, including their separated recognized cases.
At rank two the homogeneous relation is exactly the conical condition
\(\Omega=\det[b,b_{s_1},b_{s_2}]=0\), and Sections 3, 4, and 6 apply.

More intrinsically, the homogeneous-relation hypothesis is equivalent
to \(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C(tb_1,tb_2,tb_3)\le2\),
where \(t\) is independent. A homogeneous relation for \(b\) is one
for \(tb\). Conversely, homogeneous components of a relation for
\(tb\) give a nonzero homogeneous relation for \(b\), by comparing
powers of \(t\). At rank two, the Jacobian of \(tb\) has columns
\(tb_{s_1},tb_{s_2},b\), so the characteristic-zero differential-rank
criterion gives the stated equivalence with \(\Omega=0\).

The branch \(\Omega\ne0\), with projective speed image of dimension
two, is not fully covered by this theorem. Nor is the existence of
three original flow-linear companions proved for an arbitrary sliced
action. Thus (11) is not unrestricted constant-acceleration recognition
or a solution of general cancellation. No novelty claim is made.
