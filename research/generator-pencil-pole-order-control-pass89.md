# Exact pole orders in a family of universal generator pencils

This polynomial-ring family realizes the strict valuation gap of
the universal generator pencil. It shows that the resulting bound
on leading-coefficient multiplicity is sharp. The formulas hold
for arbitrary indicated integers; the accompanying test checks
only explicitly listed finite cases, not the general theorem.

## 1. A universal recovery formula

For integers d>j>=1 set
\[
R=\mathbb C[a,t,b]\subset A=\mathbb C[a,t,c],\qquad
b=a^dc,\qquad A=R[c],\qquad h=a^jc.
\tag{1}
\]
The inclusion is injective because a,t,a to the d times c are
algebraically independent. In Frac(R), c=b/a to the d and
h=b/a to the d-j. Thus h has a pole at a=0 and is not in R.
The a-adic valuation also proves it is not integral over R:
a monic equation would have a unique lowest-valuation term.

For an independent polynomial parameter tau let
\[
N=\left\lceil\frac dj\right\rceil,\qquad
G=\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(-\tau a^j)^k,\qquad
c=G(c+\tau h)+(-\tau)^N a^{jN-d}b.
\tag{2}
\]
The last exponent is nonnegative, so all coefficients on the
right belong to R[tau]. The finite geometric-series identity
G(1+tau a to the j)=1-(-tau a to the j) to the N proves (2)
directly. Consequently A[tau]=R[tau,c+tau h]. No inverse of a
has been used in this ring-generation proof.

## 2. The strict gap and the sharp leading multiplicity

For clarity, the elementary general valuation implication is as
follows. Suppose domains R subset A=R[c] have the universal pencil,
and let nu be any valuation of Frac(A) nonnegative on R. If
nu(h)<0, polynomial membership h in R[c] first implies nu(c)<0.
An inverse polynomial c=F(tau,c+tau h), with F in R[tau,T],
may be evaluated at tau=-c/h. If nu(c)>=nu(h), this parameter
has nonnegative value, so F(tau,0) has nonnegative value, a
contradiction. Hence nu(c)<nu(h)<0. This step is valid for
arbitrary ordered valuation groups and does not assume R to A
is integral or faithfully flat.

Suppose additionally that h is in Frac(R), and extend a normalized
height-one DVR valuation v_p of R to Frac(A), normalized so its
restriction is still v_p. For the algebraic extension case, the
extended values can be regarded as rational numbers. A nonzero
relation sum from i=0 to m of alpha_i c to the i=0, with alpha_m
nonzero and all alpha_i in R, forces
nu(c)>=-v_p(alpha_m). Otherwise its leading term has uniquely
lowest valuation. It follows that
\[
v_p(h)=-n<0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
v_p(\alpha_m)\ge n+1
\quad\text{for every nonzero relation of c over R}.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed the leading-coefficient order is an integer k, and the
two inequalities give -k<=nu(c)<-n, hence k>=n+1. In particular,
if R is Noetherian and normal and a relation has leading order
at most one at every height-one prime, then h has no height-one
pole. The intersection of those DVRs inside Frac(R) is R, so
h is in R. This conditional conclusion needs the stated normal
intersection property, not just an algebraic extension of fields.

In the family (1), the valuations are
\[
v_a(c)=-d,\qquad v_a(h)=-(d-j),\qquad
a^dc-b=0,\qquad v_a(a^d)=d.
\tag{4}
\]
For j=1, the pole order n=d-1 makes (3) an equality for this
linear relation. Moreover the kernel of the map
C[a,t,b,T] to A sending T to c is exactly (b-a to the d times T),
by direct elimination of b. Every nonzero relation is therefore
a multiple of that polynomial, and its T-leading coefficient is
divisible by a to the d. This is the exact minimum multiplicity
of a leading coefficient in this example.

The excluded j=0 boundary behaves differently. Then h=c, and
specialization tau=-1 would make the proposed generator zero.
For d>0 the proper inclusion R subset A is visible from the pole
of c=b/a to the d, so A cannot be generated by that zero element
over R. The universal pencil fails. At the squarefree boundary
d=j=1 instead, h=b is already in R and the inverse is simply
c=(c+tau h)-tau b; there is no pole.

These are embedded polynomial rings and relative generators, not
an original four-coordinate additive-action frame. They do not
establish that the actual remaining geometric problem supplies
a relation with squarefree leading coefficient. The general
valuation observation and this exact sharp family must be kept
separate from that missing actual-ring premise.
