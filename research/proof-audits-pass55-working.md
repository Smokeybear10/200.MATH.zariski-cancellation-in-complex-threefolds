## 2. Leading-image obstruction

shows that a minimal gauge of degree n at least two supplies a
nonzero class in the fractional colon quotient
(M_n : (f,g))/M_n. The speeds f,g are the original bad-coordinate
velocities. They are not automatically parameters or coprime.

using gcd(f,g). Under the additional parameter-speed and reduced
zero-first-jet hypotheses, the existing I3, I4 and I5 identities,
together with the quartic syzygy, exclude minimum gauges three,
four and five. The conditional lower bound is six, or twelve for
the maximum degree of every projected original four-generator list.

## 3. Radial sufficient criterion

[The radial descent note](radial-slice-descent-pass55.md) gives two
full-determinant divisibilities for a supplied invariant center.
If they hold, the actual radial derivation preserves the algebra,
and polynomial spectral projection extracts an actual affine
generic slice from a supplied slice of any finite degree.

The proof does not require a bound on an arbitrary chosen slice.
It does require division by the full determinant with multiplicity,
not its radical. Regularity also forces the chosen center to lie
in the actual invariant base, so rational centers cannot be
silently substituted.
