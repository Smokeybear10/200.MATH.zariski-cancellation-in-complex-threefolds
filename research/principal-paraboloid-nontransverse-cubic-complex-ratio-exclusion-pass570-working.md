## 5. Actual partial, exact multiplicity and the equality case

Use the ACTUAL partial T_W=partial_W of R, fixing K,Y,Z.
Extend it uniquely to the finite separable overfield F(x).

\[
\deg_xT_W(t),\deg_xT_W(s)\le6,\qquad
\deg_xT_W(x)\le7 .
\tag{23}
\]

degree bound from seven to six. The last cofactor does not.
The full scalar denominator is a coefficient-field unit.

For every c in F, finite separability gives
T_W(c)=c_t*T_W(t)+c_s*T_W(s); this does not require T_W to preserve F.

\[
\deg_x T_W f\le\deg_x f+6
\quad\text{for every }f\in F[x]\text{ with }T_Wf\ne0 .
\tag{24}
\]
All iterations on R are licensed by the actual coordinate partial.

Let f in ker_R Delta be nonconstant, and write its UNIQUE actual
source polynomial with positive top source weight M. Its top is
H to the ell times G, where ell>=1 and H does not divide G.
Since H involves W,
\[
\partial_{\mathsf W}^{\ell}(H^\ell G)
 \equiv\ell!\,G(H_{\mathsf W})^\ell\not\equiv0\pmod H .
\tag{25}
\]
Every differentiated lower source part has strictly lower weight.
The FULL kernel (21) makes the exact intrinsic degree of
T_W to the ell(f) equal to M-6ell. On the other hand f has
intrinsic degree zero and (24) applies at every iteration.
Thus
\[
\ell D\le M,\qquad M-6\ell\le6\ell,\qquad D\le12 .
\tag{26}
\]
Combined with (22), this forces D=12. At this weight the only
Y,W monomials are W squared and Y cubed, with both coefficients
nonzero. After scaling,

relation at this stage, not yet the contradiction.

The critical point is that (25),(26) apply to EVERY nonconstant
polynomial Delta-invariant, not just one. Since D=12,
\[
12\ell\le M\le12\ell,\qquad
G\in K^*,\qquad \text{top}(f)\in K^*H^\ell .
\tag{27}
\]

because all three source weights(4,3,6) are positive.

Now use the ACTUAL SOURCE-WEIGHT filtration
\[

 K[Y,Z,W]_{\text{source weight}\le N}.
\tag{28}
\]

\[
\dim_K\mathcal F_N\le\lfloor N/12\rfloor+1 .
\tag{29}

Choose two algebraically independent polynomial invariants f,g

\tag{30}

This is impossible for large n. Thus the actual profile (16)
cannot occur, proving (1).
