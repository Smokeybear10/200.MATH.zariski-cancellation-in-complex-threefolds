# Recognition of the full height-one initial-kernel exact1122 sector

The retained working proofs now recognize every supplied globally
sliced complex polynomial four-ring with exact time degrees
\((1,1,2,2)\) whose generated weighted initial algebra has
transcendence degree three. There is no remaining weight-three
or weight-four principal-kernel branch in this supplied sector.

This is a composition of restricted working theorems, not a solution
of unrestricted cancellation. The full initial ideal can instead
have height two, and an arbitrary action need not admit this time
profile at all. Neither remaining issue is removed below.

## 1. Statement with the full initial map

Let the four named generators be actual polynomial coordinates and let
\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[X,Y,Z,W]=A[u],\qquad E=\partial_u,\qquad A=\ker E,\\
(\deg_E X,\deg_E Y,\deg_E Z,\deg_E W)=(1,1,2,2),\\
a=EX,\quad b=EY,\quad e=E^2Z/2,\quad h=E^2W/2 .
\end{gathered}                                                     \tag{1}
\]
Here \(\deg_E f\) is the degree in the slice parameter. It is not
the ordinary degree of \(f\) in the four named coordinates.
Neither the slice nor any invariant is assumed to be an ambient
coordinate.

For an independent indeterminate \(\tau\), define the entire initial map
\[
\begin{gathered}
\Theta:\mathbb C[\mathsf X,\mathsf Y,\mathsf Z,\mathsf W]\longrightarrow A[\tau],\\
(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y,\mathsf Z,\mathsf W)
\longmapsto(a\tau,b\tau,e\tau^2,h\tau^2),\qquad I=\ker\Theta .
\end{gathered}                                                     \tag{2}
\]
The formal source weights are \((1,1,2,2)\).

**Working theorem, with the retained proof inputs below.**
If \(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{im}\Theta=3\),
equivalently \(\operatorname{ht}I=1\), then
\[
\boxed{A\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}.}                                    \tag{3}
\]
The given slice then belongs to a polynomial coordinate frame of
\(B\), as a consequence of \(B=A[u]\) and (3).

Height one is a condition on the full ideal, not on one chosen
vanishing relation. A height-two ideal may contain many irreducible
equations to which the principal-kernel proofs do not apply.
The generated initial algebra in (2) is not assumed to be the whole
associated graded algebra \(A[\tau]\).

## 2. The initial bound reduces the theorem to three weights

If \(a,b\) are proportional over \(\mathbb C\), a nonzero constant
linear combination of \(X,Y\) is an invariant ambient coordinate.
The slice gives a regular kernel and the retained invariant-coordinate
endpoint in [three-good156](three-good-all-degree-recognition-pass156-working.md),
Section 3, already proves (3). Thus assume they are not proportional.

The two initial forms \(a\tau,b\tau\) are then algebraically
independent. Indeed a polynomial relation separates into homogeneous
binary relations in \(a,b\). Every nonzero homogeneous binary
polynomial over \(\mathbb C\) is a product of linear factors, and
one vanishing factor would contradict nonproportionality.

Since the source of \(\Theta\) is a unique factorization domain
and \(I\) is a height-one homogeneous prime, write \(I=(F)\),
with \(F\) irreducible and weighted homogeneous.
The independence just proved forces \(F\) to involve a degree-two slot.
The complete [initial-bound157](two-linear-two-quadratic-initial-bound-pass157-working.md),
Sections 2--6, supplies
\[
2\le d:=\operatorname{wt}F\le4 .                                  \tag{4}
\]
Its proof uses actual coordinate partials, their intrinsic leading
locally nilpotent derivations, exact multiplicity along the full
prime relation, and invariant growth. It does not substitute a
formal partial for an actual one.

These are exactly the three weights treated in the following sections.

## 3. Weight two and weight three

At weight two,
\[
F=\alpha\mathsf Z+\beta\mathsf W+Q_2(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y),
\qquad(\alpha,\beta)\ne(0,0).
\]
The explicit actual shear in157, equation11, replaces one quadratic
slot by a coordinate killed by \(E^2\), and has a polynomial inverse.
There are then three actual coordinates killed by \(E^2\).
The all-last-degree theorem156 proves (3).

At weight three the
[cubic normal forms157](two-linear-cubic-normal-forms-pass157-working.md)
reduce \(F\), by constant invertible changes and quadratic shears,
to precisely one of
\[
\mathsf X\mathsf Z-\mathsf Y^3,\qquad
\mathsf X\mathsf Z+\mathsf Y\mathsf W .                             \tag{5}
\]
These are actual polynomial coordinate transformations with polynomial
inverses. Their formal weighted automorphisms transport the entire
ideal. Every new coordinate has a nonzero homogeneous formal
expression of weight one or two; it cannot belong to the ideal
generated by the weight-three polynomial \(F\). Thus the exact
time degrees remain \((1,1,2,2)\).

The [full cusp theorem174](cusp-initial-recognition-synthesis-pass174-working.md)
recognizes the first full kernel in (5). The
[full split theorem183](split-initial-recognition-synthesis-pass183-working.md)
recognizes the second. Their actual charts and coefficient fields
are those belonging to the transformed frame, not arbitrarily
transferred fields from an earlier chart.
Each conclusion is (3), with the explicitly retained dependencies
of its source synthesis.

## 4. All weight-four generators are now covered

Every weighted homogeneous polynomial of weight four has the form
\[
F=Q(\mathsf Z,\mathsf W)
  +P_2(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y)\mathsf Z
  +S_2(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y)\mathsf W
  +H_4(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y),                                      \tag{6}
\]
where \(Q\) is a binary quadratic with constant coefficients.
Its symmetric matrix has rank zero, one, or two.

For rank zero, the complete zero-quadratic-part corollary in
[synthesis194](weight-four-split-initial-recognition-synthesis-pass194-working.md),

the independent coprime row is excluded by185, the common-factor
row recognized by186, the absent-variable row recognized by190,

and exact-degree preservation are part of those retained inputs.

For positive rank, the
[new synthesis247](positive-bad-quadratic-recognition-synthesis-pass247-working.md)
recognizes (6). Its rank-one part combines234,236 and the new
repeated-root exclusions237--246 with the retained recognition

The complete decomposition (6) leaves no other quadratic rank.

Consequently every full irreducible principal weight-four initial
kernel in the actual setup (1)--(2) gives (3).
Together with Sections 2--3 this proves the working theorem.

## 5. Exact remaining branch inside a supplied exact1122 frame

The initial-bound157 proof gives
\(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{im}\Theta\le3\).
When \(a,b\) are not proportional, their initial forms are independent,
so the only possible initial-image transcendence degrees are two
and three. This note recognizes the latter.
Therefore a hypothetical nonpolynomial kernel in a supplied
exact1122 frame must have
\[
\operatorname{ht}I=2,\qquad
\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\operatorname{im}\Theta=2,\qquad
k_{\partial_Z},k_{\partial_W}\le2 .                               \tag{7}
\]
The exact shifts \(k_T\) are the all-ring \(E\)-degree shifts of
the actual partials. The last bound is157, Section 8:
every other triple of initials is dependent in this case, so the
cubic horizontal cofactor vanishes. No independent leading
derivations are asserted.

There is also a useful exact ratio-field description.
Set
\[
t=b/a,\qquad q=e/a^2,\qquad \rho=h/a^2,\qquad
C_0=\mathbb C(t,q,\rho).
\]
The fraction field of the initial image is
\(\mathbb C(a\tau,t,q,\rho)=C_0(a\tau)\).
Because \(a\tau\) is transcendental over \(\operatorname{Frac}A\),
(7) implies \(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}C_0=1\).
Also \(t\) is transcendental over \(\mathbb C\):
an algebraic element over the algebraically closed constant field
would be constant, contradicting nonproportionality.
It follows that
\[
[C_0:\mathbb C(t)]<\infty .                                       \tag{8}
\]
This does not identify \(C_0\) with a selected LND's entire constant
field, or make its rational elements polynomial in the original ring.

The [actual controls212](height-two-no-quadratic-relation-actual-control-pass212-working.md)
show why neither a weight-two initial relation nor a uniform bound
on all generators of \(I\) may be added to (7).
Its genuine frames have leaders \((1,t,t^3,t^n)\);
the least relation weight outside \((\mathsf X\mathsf Z-\mathsf Y^3)\)
is \(\lceil2n/3\rceil\). Nevertheless those frames are recognized
immediately by their coordinate slice. Thus initial-ideal complexity
is not itself a cancellation obstruction or a lower bound on
coordinate-recognition complexity.

## 6. The global entry gap is unchanged

The true-minimum corollary of156 says that a hypothetical
nonpolynomial-kernel sliced action has total minimum time degree
at least six. If its minimum equals six, its sorted degree tuple
must be \((1,1,2,2)\). Combining that statement with (3) shows only
that every such minimizing frame would have height-two initial
kernel as in (7).

It does not raise the lower bound to seven: the remaining height-two
case has not been recognized. It does not prove any upper bound
on the minimum, produce a minimizing frame, or identify ordinary
polynomial degree with degree along the flow.

The full target \(A[u]\simeq\mathbb C^{[4]}\Rightarrow
A\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\) therefore remains unsolved.
The corrected field and dimension are still a working interpretation
of the professor's email, not a confirmation from the professor.
