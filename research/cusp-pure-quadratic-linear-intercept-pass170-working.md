# A pure quadratic cusp graph with a linear coefficient intercept is recognized

Status: working proof pending independent review of this saved artifact.
This note treats the actual cusp chart when its whole plane relation
is a scalar times a quadratic graph and the graph's constant coefficient
is at most linear in the coefficient variable rho. That last condition
is an additional hypothesis. It is not imported from the cubic case167.
Under these assumptions there is an explicit invariant coordinate of
the original four-ring, and the retained regular-kernel theorem gives
\(A\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\). No claim about all quadratic graphs,
other cusp branches or unrestricted cancellation is made.

## 1. Actual assumptions and the extra intercept hypothesis

Retain the actual chart of
[the first cusp reductions159](cusp-generic-chart-first-reductions-pass159-working.md):
\(B=A[u]=\mathbb C[X,Y,Z,W]\), \(E=\partial_u\), exact E-time
degrees \((1,1,2,2)\), and full weighted initial kernel \((XZ-Y^3)\).
Let \(a=EX\ne0\), \(N=[u^3](\partial_Z|_A)\),
\(S=\ker_A N\), and \(L=\operatorname{Frac}S\).
Use \(t=\tau\). In particular
\[
\begin{gathered}
A_L=L[v],\quad Nv=1,\quad L[y,\vartheta]=L[v],\qquad
\operatorname{Frac}A=\mathbb C(y,z,w)=L(v),\\
Y=y+tX,\quad Z=z+(3t^2y+k)X+t^3X^2,
\quad W=w+\vartheta X+\rho X^2,\\
z=3ty^2+k_t y+k_\rho\vartheta+n,\qquad
k_\rho w+y^3+H_2(k;y,\vartheta)+H_1(n;y,\vartheta)=\ell .
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
Here \(k,n,\ell\in L\), \(L/\mathbb C(t,\rho)\) is finite
separable, and the extended coefficient partials commute and have
joint constants \(\mathbb C\). The notation \(H_j\) is the ordinary
homogeneous directional Taylor coefficient, including its factorials.
These are actual polynomial-inverse consequences, not a formal ansatz.

Work with \(r=k_\rho\ne0\), and suppose the fixed-axis selected
curve is \(\vartheta=P(y)\), where
\(P=p_2U^2+p_1U+p_0\in L[U]\) and \(p_2\ne0\).
Then \(L[y]=L[v]\), so \(y\) is an affine parameter over \(L\).
The full selected kernel is generated by \(G=V-P(U)\).
The selected-factor reduction159 permits
\(\Phi-m=\lambda G+\kappa G^2\), with \(\lambda\in L^*\)
and \(\kappa\in L\). We assume the pure branch
\[
\Phi-m=\lambda\{V-P(U)\},\qquad \kappa=0,
\qquad p_0=e\rho+d_0\quad(e,d_0\in K),
\qquad K:=\ker_L\partial_\rho .
\tag{2}
\]
The last membership is the additional linear-intercept hypothesis.
It is not a conclusion of the graph's degree or the unit-gradient
identity. No result from169 is required in this conditional proof.

One has \([K:\mathbb C(t)]<\infty\),
\(\ker_K\partial_t=\mathbb C\), and \(\rho\) is transcendental
over \(K\). For the first assertion, differentiate the monic minimal
polynomial of an element of \(K\) over \(\mathbb C(t,\rho)\).
Minimality makes all coefficient rho derivatives zero. Its coefficients
therefore lie in \(\mathbb C(t)\). Linear independence over that
field persists on adjoining \(\rho\), bounding the degree by the
given finite extension. The constant-field assertion follows from
the joint constants. A prime below is \(\partial_t\) on \(K\).

## 2. Literal coefficients force reciprocal-linear lambda

The potential in159 has quartic part
\(-U^3(r_tU+r_\rho V)/r\). Equation (2) has degree two, so
\(r\in\mathbb C^*\) and \(k=r\rho+k_0\), \(k_0\in K\).
Its \(V^2\) and \(UV\) coefficients then give
\(n_{\rho\rho}=n_{t\rho}=0\). Thus
\(n=s\rho+n_0\), with \(s\in\mathbb C\) and \(n_0\in K\).
The remaining literal coefficients of the same potential give
\[
\begin{gathered}
k_0'''=6s/r,\qquad
\lambda=-s^2/r-\ell_\rho,\\
p_2=\frac{s k_0''/(2r)-n_0''/2}{\lambda},\qquad
p_1=\frac{s n_0'/r+\ell_t}{\lambda} .
\end{gathered}
\tag{3}
\]
In particular the numerator defining \(p_2\) is a nonzero element
of \(K\). No condition on the free integration constant \(m\)
is imposed by these formulas.

Use \(y\) as the third separating parameter. The constant determinant
of159 is now \(-\lambda\mathcal S=-a^3/y_v\in L^*\), where
\[
\mathcal S=P_t+P_U P_\rho+(s/r)P_U
                  +(3U^2+k_0''U+n_0')/r\in L^* .
\tag{4}
\]
This is the same literal determinant calculation as in167, with the
cubic coefficient zero. Its coefficient of \(U^3\) is
\(2p_2(p_2)_\rho\). It vanishes, so \((p_2)_\rho=0\)
and (3) forces \(\lambda_\rho=0\).
Consequently \(\ell=(-\lambda-s^2/r)\rho+\ell_0\),
\(\ell_0\in K\), and
\(p_1=\gamma\rho+b\), where
\(\gamma=-\lambda'/\lambda\) and
\(b=(s n_0'/r+\ell_0')/\lambda\) belong to \(K\).

Using the extra hypothesis \(p_0=e\rho+d_0\), the coefficients
of \(U^2\) and \(U\) in (4) give
\[
\begin{gathered}
p_2'+2\gamma p_2+3/r=0,\qquad
\gamma'+\gamma^2=0,\\
b'+\gamma b+2p_2(e+s/r)+k_0''/r=0,\\
\chi:=1/\lambda=\alpha t+\beta,\qquad
\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb C,\quad (\alpha,\beta)\ne(0,0),
\qquad \gamma=\alpha/\chi .
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]
The second equation on the first line is the rho coefficient of
the \(U\) equation; it may be separated because rho is transcendental
over \(K\). It implies \(\chi''=0\), and exact constants give
the last line. Without (2)'s intercept condition this comparison
would contain the unknown rho dependence of \((p_0)_\rho\).

## 3. Actual field return forces the intercept combination constant

All coefficients of \(P,k,n,\ell\) now lie in \(K[\rho]\).
Thus (1) puts \(z,w\) in \(K(\rho)[y]\). The exact actual field
return gives
\(L(y)=\mathbb C(y,z,w)\subset K(\rho)(y)\subset L(y)\).
Intersecting with \(L\) proves \(L=K(\rho)\). This does not
presuppose that \(K\) is rational.

Put \(H=\chi(e+s/r)\in K\) and
\(w_*=w+(s/r^2)z\). Direct substitution in (1) gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
w_*=V_0-\rho/(r\chi),\qquad
z=Z_0+(r\gamma y+re+s)\rho,\\
V_0=-y^3/r+(-k_0''/(2r)+3st/r^2)y^2
              +(-n_0'/r+s k_0'/r^2)y+\ell_0/r+sn_0/r^2,\\
Z_0=(3t+rp_2)y^2+(k_0'+rb)y+n_0+rd_0,\\
z=f w_*+g,\qquad f=-r^2\alpha y-r^2H,\qquad
g=Z_0-fV_0\in K[y].
\end{gathered}
\tag{6}
\]
Assume \(H\notin\mathbb C\). Over \(k_*:=\mathbb C(y)\),
the element \(f\) is transcendental, and \(w_*\) is transcendental
over \(M:=K(y)\). Actual field return is
\(k_*(z,w_*)=M(w_*)\). The intervening field
\(k_*(f,g)(w_*)\) must be equal to both. Intersecting with
\(M\) proves \(M=k_*(f,g)\).

The elementary pencil-degree lemma166 now applies: the extension
degree \([M(w_*):k_*(w_*,z)]\) is the total degree of the full
irreducible relation between \(f,g\). It is one here, hence
\(g\in k_*+k_*f=\mathbb C(y)+H\mathbb C(y)\).
Since \(g\in K[y]\), each coefficient of \(g\) lies in
\(\mathbb C+\mathbb C H\). Indeed, a finite complex basis for
the span of those coefficients and \(1,H\) stays independent
over \(\mathbb C(y)\); clear denominators and compare powers
of the transcendental \(y\).

For the decisive coefficient define
\(\mathsf c=sk_0-rn_0\), \(\mathsf d=r\ell_0+sn_0\).
The coefficient of \(y\) in (6) is
\[
q_1=k_0'+rb+H\mathsf c'+\alpha\mathsf d,\qquad
q_1'=H'\mathsf c',\qquad
\mathsf c''=2rp_2/\chi\ne0,\quad
\mathsf d'=rb/\chi .
\tag{7}
\]
To verify the derivative, (5) cancels
\(k_0''+rb'+H\mathsf c''+\alpha\mathsf d'\).
The coefficient-span conclusion writes \(q_1=u_0+u_1H\)
with \(u_0,u_1\in\mathbb C\). Differentiation gives
\((\mathsf c'-u_1)H'=0\). The supposition that \(H\) is
nonconstant implies \(H'\ne0\), so \(\mathsf c'=u_1\)
and \(\mathsf c''=0\), contradicting (7). Therefore
\(H=h\in\mathbb C\). This uses the linear coefficient,
not the cubic-case argument whose nonzero third coefficient
is unavailable here.

## 4. All coefficients needed for the global construction are constant

Set \(\mathsf b=st^3-rk_0\), so \(\mathsf b'''=0\).
Define
\[
\begin{gathered}
q_3=\alpha\mathsf b''/2,\qquad
q_2=3t+rp_2+h\mathsf b''/2+\alpha\mathsf c',\\
q_1=k_0'+rb+h\mathsf c'+\alpha\mathsf d,\qquad
q_1,q_2,q_3\in\mathbb C,\\
g=P_*(y)+g_0,\quad
P_*(y)=-r\alpha y^4+(q_3-rh)y^3+q_2y^2+q_1y,\\
g_0=(1+sh)n_0+rd_0+rh\ell_0\in K .
\end{gathered}
\tag{8}
\]
The first coefficient is constant because \(\mathsf b'''=0\).
For the second, its derivative is
\(3+rp_2'+\alpha\mathsf c''=0\) by (5) and (7).
The third is constant by (7) with \(H'=0\).
Expanding (6) gives exactly the displayed expression for \(g\).

Now \(f\in\mathbb C(y)\). Actual field equality and (8) imply
\(K(y)(w_*)=\mathbb C(y,g_0)(w_*)\), so intersection first
with \(K(y)\) and then with \(K\) proves \(K=\mathbb C(g_0)\).
This consequence also proves \(g_0\notin\mathbb C\).
Only its membership in \(K\), not this rationality conclusion,
is needed for the next construction.

## 5. The original-ring correction and both inverse orders

We reproduce the needed identities from168 with the cubic coefficient
zero, rather than apply its cubic theorem outside its hypotheses.
Let \(F=XZ-Y^3\), \(\mathcal L=\beta X+\alpha Y+h\), and
\(I_*=Z+\mathcal L(r^2W+sZ-rF)\in B\).
Equations (1), (3), and (6) give the literal equality
\[
r^2W+sZ-rF=-r\rho/\chi+\mathsf b''y^2/2+\mathsf c'y+\mathsf d
          +X(\mathsf b'y+\mathsf c)+X^2\mathsf b .
\tag{9}
\]
In particular \(\mathcal L=\chi X+\alpha y+h\) in this chart.
All rho coefficients cancel in \(I_*\). Its constant coefficient,
by (8), is \(g_0+q_3y^3+q_2y^2+q_1y\).
Put
\[
\begin{array}{lll}
A_2=\alpha\mathsf b'+\chi\mathsf b''/2,&
A_1=3t^2+h\mathsf b'+\alpha\mathsf c+\chi\mathsf c',&
A_0=k_0+h\mathsf c+\chi\mathsf d,\\
B_1=\alpha\mathsf b+\chi\mathsf b',&
B_0=t^3+h\mathsf b+\chi\mathsf c,&C_0=\chi\mathsf b .
\end{array}
\tag{10}
\]
Direct collection in (9) gives
\(I_*=g_0+\mathcal Q(X,y)\), where
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathcal Q=q_3y^3+q_2y^2+q_1y
 +X(A_2y^2+A_1y+A_0)+X^2(B_1y+B_0)+X^3C_0,\\
A_2'=3q_3,\quad A_1'=2q_2,\quad A_0'=q_1,\qquad
B_1'=2A_2,\quad B_0'=A_1,\quad C_0'=B_1,\\
(\partial_t-X\partial_y)\mathcal Q=0,\qquad
Q(X,Y):=\mathcal Q(X,Y-tX)\in\mathbb C[X,Y],\quad\deg Q\le3 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{11}
\]
For the first recurrence use \(\mathsf b'''=0\);
the next two use \(\chi\mathsf c''=2rp_2\) and
\(\chi\mathsf d'=rb\). The last three follow by differentiating
(10). The \(q_i\) are constant by (8), so these recurrences give
the differential identity. Substitution holding \(X,Y\) fixed
then makes every coefficient of \(Q\) derivative zero in \(K\).
Its exact constant field proves the claimed complex polynomial
membership, with no integration or denominator-descent assumption.

Consequently \(I=I_*-Q=g_0\) belongs to \(A\cap L=S\).
There is an explicit global inverse:
\[
\begin{gathered}
J=W+(s/r^2-X/r)Z,\qquad
I=Z+r^2\mathcal L J+r\mathcal L Y^3-Q,\\
Z=I-r^2\mathcal L J-r\mathcal L Y^3+Q,\qquad
W=J-(s/r^2-X/r)Z,\\
B=\mathbb C[X,Y,I,J].
\end{gathered}
\tag{12}
\]
Both composition orders follow by substitution with \(X,Y\)
unchanged. Only \(r\in\mathbb C^*\) is inverted. Thus this is
an actual invariant coordinate, not merely an element of \(A_a\).
The invariant has original weight at most four; no minimal-weight
assertion is needed.

Finally the regular-kernel application already stated in168 applies
with these verified hypotheses. The nonzero LND \(E\) is
\(\mathbb C[I]\)-linear on the polynomial three-ring over
\(\mathbb C[I]\) in (12). Its kernel \(A\simeq B/(u)\)
is regular because \(Eu=1\) makes the hypersurface \(u=0\)
smooth. Bhatwadekar--Gupta--Lokhande Theorem3.5 therefore gives
\(A=\mathbb C[I]^{[2]}\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\).
This is the retained external theorem, not a new proof of it.

## Scope and dependencies

The additional hypothesis \(p_0\in K[\rho]_{\le1}\) is used
essentially in (5). This note neither proves it for all pure quadratic
charts nor excludes their possible quadratic rho intercepts. It does
not treat the extra-factor branch, constant or linear graphs, the
\(r=0\) charts, other initial configurations, or arbitrary coordinate
entry. There is no dependency on a future169 classification.

1. [Actual cusp chart159](cusp-generic-chart-first-reductions-pass159-working.md) supplies the actual projected ring, exact field, potential and determinant.
2. [Selected-factor reduction159](cusp-generic-selected-factor-reduction-pass159-working.md) supplies the possible quadratic remainder; its vanishing is assumed in (2).
3. [Pencil-degree proof166](cusp-quartic-actual-field-recognition-pass166-working.md), Section3, is used only as the elementary field-degree lemma.
4. [Cubic coefficient proof167](cusp-cubic-field-forcing-pass167-working.md) and [global coordinate construction168](cusp-cubic-global-coordinate-pass168-working.md) supply the mechanisms reconstructed here. Their cubic classification is not applied to this quadratic graph.
5. [Bhatwadekar--Gupta--Lokhande Theorem3.5](https://arxiv.org/html/1501.01438v1) is the retained regular-kernel endpoint, freshly inspected in the168 investigation. No new external theorem is imported.
