# Irredundancy does not repair flow-affine linear extraction

The eleven displayed generators in the actual control428 are an
IRREDUNDANT algebra-generating list. Nevertheless their complex linear
span, even after adjoining constants, contains no ambient coordinate.
Thus adding irredundancy to that proposed global-entry step does not
repair it. This supplement proves the deletion assertion explicitly;
the all-coefficient no-coordinate statement is the retained theorem428.

Irredundancy means that deleting any member of this specified list
destroys algebra generation. It does not mean minimum cardinality among
all generating sets, or minimum dimension among generating subspaces.
Here the same polynomial ring has four known flow-affine coordinate
generators. No cancellation or unrestricted coordinate-entry theorem
is claimed.

## 1. Actual setup and the retained no-coordinate control

In the actual polynomial ring and globally sliced action
\[
B=\mathbb C[u,a,b,c],\qquad E=\partial_u,
\tag{1}
\]
use the eleven polynomials
\[
\begin{aligned}
g_1&=au,&g_2&=(a^2+1)u,\\
g_3&=a^2,&g_4&=a^3,&g_5&=a^5+a,\\
g_6&=b^2,&g_7&=b^3,&g_8&=b^5+b,\\
g_9&=c^2,&g_{10}&=c^3,&g_{11}&=c^5+c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{2}
\]
Set \(V=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{g_1,\ldots,g_{11}\}\).
Their monomial supports prove linear independence. The direct identities
\[
\begin{aligned}
a&=g_5-g_3g_4,& b&=g_8-g_6g_7,&c&=g_{11}-g_9g_{10},\\
u&=g_2-(g_5-g_3g_4)g_1
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
prove \(\mathbb C[V]=B\), and every member of V is killed by E squared.

The complete, independently reviewed
[control428](flow-affine-generating-space-no-coordinate-control-pass428-working.md)
proves
\[
(\mathbb C+V)\cap\{\text{ambient polynomial coordinates of }B\}
=\varnothing.
\tag{4}
\]
Its proof treats every complex coefficient vector, not a finite sample.
Writing a combination as
\(A(a)u+B_0(a)+C_0(b)+D_0(c)+\kappa\), it uses a critical point
when A is zero and the exact Euler characteristic of a generic whole
fiber when A is nonzero. It retains double roots of A and repeated
critical values of the separated plane polynomials. That proof and its
primary Euler-characteristic input are retained here, not replaced by
the irredundancy argument.

## 2. A three-generator one-variable deletion lemma

Consider the actual line ring \(\mathbb C[T]\) and
\[
p_2=T^2,\qquad p_3=T^3,\qquad p_5=T^5+T.
\tag{5}
\]
They generate it, since \(T=p_5-p_2p_3\). Each of its three
two-element sublists generates a proper subalgebra.

First, every polynomial expression in \(T^2,T^3\) has derivative
zero at T=0, by the chain rule. The derivative of T there is one.
Thus T does not belong to \(\mathbb C[T^2,T^3]\).

Second, choose a complex q with \(q^4=-1\). Then q is nonzero,
q and -q are distinct, and
\[
p_2(q)=p_2(-q),\qquad p_5(q)=p_5(-q)=0.
\tag{6}
\]
Every polynomial in \(p_2,p_5\) takes the same value at these
two points, whereas T does not. Hence these two do not generate T.

Third, let \(\omega\ne1\) be a primitive cube root of unity and
choose \(q\in\mathbb C\) with \(q^4=\omega\). These choices give
q nonzero and \(q\ne\omega q\). Their cubes agree. The other
coordinate also agrees because
\[
\begin{aligned}
p_5(\omega q)-p_5(q)
&=(\omega^2-1)q^5+(\omega-1)q\\
&=q\bigl((\omega^2-1)\omega+\omega-1\bigr)
=q(\omega^3-1)=0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
Thus \(p_3,p_5\) cannot generate T. This proves all three deletion
claims, including the pair whose map is injective but has zero
derivative at the origin. Point collisions alone were not used for
that first pair.

## 3. Every deletion from the eleven-generator list fails

For \(1\le i\le11\), write
\[
C_i=\mathbb C[g_j:j\ne i]\subset B.
\tag{8}
\]
If \(C_i=B\), every surjective specialization of B would have
its target generated by the images of those ten remaining functions.
We exhibit a specialization contradicting this for every i.

To delete \(g_1=au\), set \(a=\mathrm i\), \(b=c=0\), and keep u
free. This is a surjection \(B\to\mathbb C[u]\). The remaining
flow-dependent generator becomes
\((\mathrm i^2+1)u=0\), and every other remaining generator becomes
a complex constant. Their images cannot generate u. Thus \(C_1\ne B\).

To delete \(g_2=(a^2+1)u\), instead set \(a=b=c=0\) and keep u
free. The image of g1 is zero, and all remaining functions are
constant. This proves \(C_2\ne B\).

For any deleted member of the a-triple \(g_3,g_4,g_5\), specialize
\(u=b=c=0\), keeping a free. For any deleted member of the b-triple
\(g_6,g_7,g_8\), specialize \(u=a=c=0\), keeping b free. For the
c-triple \(g_9,g_{10},g_{11}\), specialize \(u=a=b=0\), keeping c
free. In each case both velocity-bearing functions vanish, the other
two triples become constants, and the only remaining nonconstant
images are one of the pairs in Section 2. That section proves their
algebra is strictly smaller than the corresponding line ring.

Therefore every one of the eleven subalgebras in (8) is proper:
\[
\boxed{\quad C_i\ne B\quad\text{for every }1\le i\le11.\quad}
\tag{9}
\]
In particular no g_i is a polynomial in the other ten. Any proper
sublist omits some g_i and lies in C_i, so it too fails to generate B.

## 4. What this obstructs, and what it does not

Combining (4) and (9) gives the exact bounded statement:
\[
\boxed{\begin{gathered}
\{g_1,\ldots,g_{11}\}\text{ is an irredundant algebra-generating list},\\
E^2(V)=0,\qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}V=11,\\
(\mathbb C+V)\text{ contains no ambient coordinate}.
\end{gathered}}
\tag{10}
\]
The velocity ideal is nevertheless the unit ideal, since
\((Eg_1,Eg_2)=(a,a^2+1)=B\). Differentiating (3) also shows that
the eleven differentials generate the full cotangent module of B.
Thus these local algebraic generation tests and deletion-irredundancy
coexist with the linear-extraction obstruction.

No four elements of \(\mathbb C+V\) can generate B. Indeed, four
algebra generators of a polynomial four-ring are algebraically
independent: otherwise the kernel of the surjection from a polynomial
four-ring would be a nonzero prime of positive height, lowering the
transcendence degree. They would therefore form an actual polynomial
coordinate system, contradicting (4). This observation supplies no
method for constructing such four elements outside the selected space.

The list in (2) is NOT claimed to have minimum cardinality. The four
known coordinates u,a,b,c generate B and are flow-affine, and dimension
prevents generation by fewer than four elements. Nor is V claimed
minimal among complex subspaces whose algebras equal B. We do not
determine the smallest possible dimension of such a subspace contained
in V; (4) only prevents that dimension from being four or less.

Finally, (3) explicitly allows nonlinear recovery of an adapted frame.
Our deletion obstruction does not prohibit replacing generators by
nonlinear expressions before deleting others. It disproves the narrower
inference that a deletion-irredundant flow-affine generating list must
already contain a coordinate in its constant linear span. It gives no
universal elimination procedure, no new recognition class, and no
counterexample to cancellation.
