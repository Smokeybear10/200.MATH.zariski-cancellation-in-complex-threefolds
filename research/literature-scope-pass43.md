# Source scope for global repairs and projection exclusion

## Established inputs actually used

The retained Maubach manuscript, *The Commuting Derivations Conjecture*
(October 5, 2001), was directly reread for Proposition 3.2,
Proposition 3.4, the associated independence statements, and
Theorem 2.4. The root reconstruction uses a coefficient minor on
complex fibers, not a claim that the dimension-four commuting
derivations conjecture is established.
Source: https://www.math.ru.nl/~maubach/Papers/The_Commuting_Dervations.pdf

The same manuscript explicitly states the affirmative CP(3) result,
where its notation means canceling a variable from a polynomial
three-ring. This is the complex surface cancellation input. Its
reference is Fujita, *On Zariski problem*, Proceedings of the Japan
Academy Series A 55 (1979), 106-110, DOI 10.3792/pjaa.55.106.
The complete original Fujita proof was not retrieved and reconstructed
in this pass. Direct DOI/J-STAGE and Euclid attempts failed or returned
access HTML. One new failed-download file is correctly retained as
tmp/pdfs/fujita-zariski-1979-pass43-access.html, not represented as a PDF
or used as mathematical evidence.

Bhatwadekar-Gupta-Lokhande, arXiv:1501.01438v1, Theorem 3.5 and
its complete proof were directly reread at the retained text's
lines 502-511. The theorem assumes an actual fixed ambient coordinate
and regular original kernel. Original-slice regularity is used only
for the original E. No auxiliary kernel inherits that slice.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01438v1

Dasgupta-Gaifullin, arXiv:2306.00510v2, Theorem 2.5 and
Proposition 2.6 supply slice and local-slice equalities over a
characteristic-zero field. These support the centralizer lemma
and its triangular extension. The abstract root lemmas were
restricted to domains containing Q after an audit caught the
overbroad phrase "characteristic-zero domain".
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.00510v2

Dasgupta-Gupta, arXiv:1701.03635v1, Theorem 2.2 and Theorem 4.1
were read in full by the root and independent agents. They state
the exact relative-plane kernel theorem attributed to
Daigle-Freudenburg and the primitive two-quasi classification
attributed to Wang. The determinant proof establishes a real
relative plane before the first input and divides an invariant
coefficient gcd before the second. The complete original 1998
and 2005 papers were not newly read here.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.03635v1

The Stacks Project, Theorem 10.34.1 (00FV), supplies the standard
finite-type Nullstellensatz consequence for coefficient ideals.
No equation ideal is asserted to be proper without checking it;
degree exhaustion is positive-only.
Source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FV

## Current universal-reduction comparison

Stéphane Lamy, *Tame polynomial automorphisms*, arXiv:2609.05655v1,
was submitted September 4, 2026. The root directly checked the
introduction and scope; the geometry agent directly read the
relevant three-variable reduction setup and four-variable orthogonal
subgroup section. The dimension-four subgroup preserves a fixed
nondegenerate quadratic form. It is not every polynomial
automorphism or every frame of a sliced action. The survey
explicitly leaves equality of the tame and full groups open in
dimension at least four.
Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05655v1

The geometry agent also read the relevant approximation passages
in Kanel-Belov, Yu and Elishev, arXiv:1712.01490v1, Definition 2.2
and Sections 3.1/3.3. The complete original Anick approximation
proof was not reconstructed. The all-N shear control in the
universal-frame note is proved directly and does not depend on
the approximation theorem.
Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.01490v1

The all-inverse-pair ideal was compared with the local pass-32 and
pass-39 constructions. The former minimizes another canonical
horizontal-action statistic; the latter assumes a supplied inverse
family. Neither was incorrectly credited as an unrestricted
automorphism search. No practicality or novelty is claimed for
the standard finite polynomial-equation encoding.

foliation article. Its title was checked immediately and it was
not used or saved as Kaliman's paper. The authoritative Kaliman
statement needed here is Maubach's exact Theorem 2.4. This is not
a claim to have newly reconstructed the full Kaliman proof.

A primary unipotent-action article was identified as a possible
generic-orbit lead but is not a proof input. The coefficient-minor
argument avoids assuming a generic torsor or an unproved base-change
descent. Secondary search results on open unipotent orbits are
likewise not evidence for the conditional theorem.

This is a bounded statement-level source audit, not a completed
novelty search across every bibliography, thesis, arXiv, MathSciNet,
zbMATH or all citations. No inaccessible source was treated as
read; no search failure was treated as absence of prior work.
There was no external contact, submission, public posting,
credential use or paid-source access.
