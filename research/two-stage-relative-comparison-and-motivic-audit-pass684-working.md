# A first-order relative comparison obstruction and a motivic source audit

Date: September 18, 2026.

Complex-threefold cancellation remains UNSOLVED and novelty UNKNOWN.
This note rules out a specific stable comparison that might otherwise

## 1. No comparison with this deformed first-kind family over the chosen base

Let

    B=C[a,V,b,k,S]/(V⁴+b³+a(1-V+2V²k)+a²S).

For any integer n>=2 and polynomial H in C[alpha,v,beta], define

    T_H=C[alpha,v,beta,W,U]/
        (alpha^n W+v⁴+beta³+alpha H(alpha,v,beta)).

For every ell>=0 there is NO C-algebra isomorphism

    phi:T_H[t1,...,tell] -> B[t1,...,tell]

such that phi(alpha)=d*a for a constant d!=0. No smoothness assumption
on T_H is needed. In particular the exclusion applies to smooth members
of this standard or deformed first-kind family with the indicated cusp.

This is a RELATIVE comparison obstruction. It does not rule out a
comparison moving a to an unrelated function, another presentation,
or a directly constructed whole action escaping the critical plane.

## 2. The conductor rigidifies the cusp coordinate, even with free variables

Both special fibers are copies of

    C[t³,t⁴][w1,...,w_(2+ell)].

Use V=t³ and b=-t⁴ for the target and the same signed parametrization for
v,beta in the source. The normalization is

    N=C[t,w1,...,w_(2+ell)],

and the conductor ideal in N is exactly t⁶N. To see the conductor
calculation, the semigroup generated by3,4 contains every integer >=6
and misses1,2,5. Thus every t-monomial of degree >=6 multiplies N back
into the cusp ring. If a nonzero polynomial has lowest t-degree0,3 or4,
multiply by t,t² or t, respectively: a surviving term then has a missing
degree. It cannot belong to the conductor. Coefficients in the polynomial
variables do not change this argument.

An isomorphism of special fibers extends to their normalizations and
preserves the conductor. Since the only units of N are nonzero complex
constants, it sends t to c*t with c!=0: equality of the principal ideals
generated by the sixth powers forces equality of their prime generators
up to a unit. This remains true if all added variables mix arbitrarily.
It follows that the reduction of phi must satisfy

    v -> c³V, beta -> c⁴b.

Consequently, before reduction there are F1,G1 in B[t1,...,tell] such that

    phi(v)=c³V+aF1, phi(beta)=c⁴b+aG1.                   (1)

The normalization assertion is also elementary here: t is integral over
the cusp ring, lies in its fraction field, and adjoining it gives the
integrally closed polynomial ring with the same fraction field.

## 3. The obstruction survives modulo the square of the base parameter

Apply phi to the defining relation of T_H and reduce modulo a². Since
n>=2, the W-term disappears. Using (1) gives

    0 = c¹²(V⁴+b³)
        +a(4c⁹V³ F1+3c⁸b² G1+d H(0,c³V,c⁴b)) mod a².

In B, V⁴+b³=-a(1-V+2V²k)-a²S. The ring is a domain and a is nonzero,
so division by a followed by reduction modulo a is legitimate. A necessary
condition in the special fiber is therefore

    d H(0,c³V,c⁴b)-c¹²(1-V+2V²k) belongs to (V³,b²).     (2)

After quotienting by that ideal, the special fiber becomes the explicit
ring

    C[k,S,t1,...,tell,V,b]/(V³,b²).

The relation V⁴+b³ is already zero there. In this polynomial extension of
C[V,b]/(V³,b²), the first summand of (2) is independent of k, whereas
the coefficient of k in the second is -2c¹²V². The element V² is nonzero.
Thus (2) is impossible. This proves the theorem for every H and ell, not
just a finite list of deformations or coordinate changes.

For H=1, the surviving linear V coefficient already contradicts (2).
For arbitrary H, that simpler argument is insufficient because H could
cancel the V term. The k coefficient supplies the uniform obstruction.

## 4. A separate published criterion does apply to the exact test family

For every R in671, let X=Spec R. Its map a:X->A¹ is flat: R is torsion-free
over the PID C[a]. Its open part is Gm times A²; its closed fiber is
Gamma_(nm,q) times A¹, where Gamma is the coprime cusp y^(nm)+b^q=0.

For any smooth affine Y, compare higher-Chow localization sequences for
X times Y -> A¹ times Y. Use dimension-indexed groups, with degree shifted
by the flat relative dimension2. The open vertical maps are isomorphisms
by homotopy invariance. The closed maps are isomorphisms by the cusp result
in [Hoyois--Krishna--Ostvaer, Proposition3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.1293v2)
and homotopy invariance. The five lemma then proves

    CH^j(Y,i) -> CH^j(X times Y,i) is an isomorphism

for every j and i>=0. Smoothness and the origin as basepoint allow
[Dubouloz--Pauli--Ostvaer, Theorem3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.08959)
to conclude that X becomes motivically A¹-contractible after finitely
many pointed P¹-smash suspensions. The same holds for X times A¹.

This argument uses the general criterion, not the named iterated-family
corollary whose displayed exponent range excludes our presentation.
Smash suspension is NOT adjoining polynomial variables. No unsuspended
contractibility, affine-space isomorphism, or cancellation result follows.
