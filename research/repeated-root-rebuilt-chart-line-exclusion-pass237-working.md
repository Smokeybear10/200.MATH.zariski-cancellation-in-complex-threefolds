# Rebuilding the repeated-root charts and excluding their constant-first lines

Under the supplied globally sliced exact1122 hypotheses, the two
remaining rank-one models admit an actual exchange of their quadratic
coordinate slots. Rebuilding the selected chart after that exchange
gives \(q=\rho^2-t^3\) or \(q=\rho^2-t^4\).
In both rebuilt charts the return polynomial is nonzero and the first
projected coordinate is nonconstant.

This is a restricted working exclusion. It does not put an arbitrary
cancellation instance into the supplied frame, exclude either entire
model, or solve the cancellation conjecture.

## 1. An actual coordinate exchange, not a change of generic parameters

Assume
\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[X,Y,Z_{\rm old},W_{\rm old}]=A[u],\quad E=\partial_u,\\
(\deg_E X,\deg_E Y,\deg_E Z_{\rm old},\deg_E W_{\rm old})=(1,1,2,2),
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{1}
\]
and assume the full leading-coefficient kernel is the principal ideal
generated by one of
\[
F_3=Z_{\rm old}^2-X^2W_{\rm old}-XY^3,\qquad
F_4=Z_{\rm old}^2-X^2W_{\rm old}-Y^4.                              \tag{2}
\]
The symbols in (2) name the weighted indeterminates corresponding to
the actual coordinates. A single leading relation is not enough.
These are the two surviving normal forms in
[frontier234](rank-one-bad-quadratic-four-model-frontier-pass234-working.md)
after [exclusion236](rank-one-distinct-root-residue-exclusion-pass236-working.md).

Set \(Z=W_{\rm old}\) and \(W=Z_{\rm old}\). This is an actual
polynomial automorphism, with the same involution as its inverse.
It preserves the exact degree profile. Up to multiplication by \(-1\),
the full kernels become
\[
\widehat F_3=X^2Z-W^2+XY^3,\qquad
\widehat F_4=X^2Z-W^2+Y^4.                                       \tag{3}
\]
The new \(Z\) occurs, with formal derivative \(X^2\).
Use the literal new \(\partial_Z\), not the old one, and rebuild
[universal184](weight-four-universal-chart-potentials-pass184-working.md):
\[
\begin{gathered}
a=EX,\quad b=EY,\quad e=E^2Z/2,\quad h=E^2W/2,\\
0\ne N=[u^3](\partial_Z|_A),\quad
L=\operatorname{Frac}(\ker_A N),\quad A_L=L[v],\quad Nv=1,\\
t=b/a,\quad \rho=h/a^2,\quad q=e/a^2,\quad
[L:\mathbb C(t,\rho)]<\infty,\\
t,\rho\text{ algebraically independent},\qquad
q=\rho^2-g(t),\quad g=t^3\text{ or }t^4 .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{4}
\]
The ratio identity follows by evaluating (3) at \((1,t,q,\rho)\).
There is no assertion that the old \(N,L,v\), projected entries or
return polynomial survive this exchange. They are reconstructed.

## 2. Polynomial potentials in the rebuilt chart

All coefficient partials extend uniquely from \(\mathbb C(t,\rho)\)
to \(L\), commute there, and fix \(v\). With \(X\) independent over
\(L(v)\), the actual projection gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
Y=y+tX,\qquad Z=z+\sigma X+qX^2,\qquad
W=w+\theta X+\rho X^2,\\
y,z,w,\sigma,\theta\in L[v],\quad L[y,z,w]=L[v],\quad
J(X)=J_0=-a^3\in L^* .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{5}
\]
Write \(H_j(c;U,V)=
\sum_{i=0}^j c_{t^i\rho^{j-i}}U^iV^{j-i}/(i!(j-i)!)\).
Retain arbitrary lower potentials \(k,n,\ell,m\in L\), and define
\[
\begin{gathered}
f=V^2-\frac{g''}{2}U^2+k_tU+k_\rho V+n,\qquad
\mathcal R=f_V=2V+k_\rho,\\
S=-\frac{g'''}6U^3+H_2(k)+H_1(n),\\
Q=-\frac{g''''}{24}U^4+H_3(k)+H_2(n)
       -\ell_tU-\ell_\rho V-m .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{6}
\]
Universal184 specializes to the polynomial identities
\[
\begin{gathered}
\sigma=-g'y+2\rho\theta+k,\qquad
z=2\rho w+f(y,\theta),\\
Rw=\ell-S(y,\theta),\qquad R=2\theta+k_\rho,\\
w^2+f_\rho(y,\theta)w+Q(y,\theta)=0 .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{7}
\]
No division by \(R\), coordinate derivative or original-ring
nonunit has been used in (7).

## 3. The identically zero return branch is impossible

Suppose \(R=0\). Then \(\theta=-k_\rho/2\in L\).
The third identity in (7) says \(S(y,\theta)=\ell\).
As a polynomial in \(y\), its cubic coefficient is
\(-g'''/6\), which is \(-1\) for \(g=t^3\) and \(-4t\ne0\)
for \(g=t^4\). Its other terms have degree at most two in \(y\).
Thus \(y\in L[v]\) is algebraic over \(L\), so \(y\in L\).

The last identity in (7) is now a monic quadratic equation for
\(w\) over \(L\), forcing \(w\in L\). The second gives \(z\in L\).
This contradicts \(L[y,z,w]=L[v]\). Therefore
\[
R\ne0.                                                           \tag{8}
\]
This does not say that \(R\) has no geometric roots.

## 4. Consequences of a constant first projection

Suppose now that \(y\in L\). By (8) and (7), \(\theta\) cannot
also belong to \(L\), since then \(w,z\in L\).
The third identity expresses \(w\) rationally in \(\theta\);
the second does the same for \(z\). Whole-line return yields
\[
L(\theta)=L(v).
\]
For a nonconstant polynomial \(\theta(v)\) over a characteristic-zero
field, the degree of this field extension is \(\deg_v\theta\).
Consequently \(\deg_v\theta=1\), and \(L[\theta]=L[v]\).

With \(y\in L\), the numerator \(\ell-S(y,\theta)\) has degree
at most two in \(\theta\). The denominator \(2\theta+k_\rho\)
has degree one. Polynomial return therefore forces
\[
\begin{gathered}
w=\lambda\theta+\omega,\qquad
z=\theta^2+\kappa\theta+\zeta,\qquad
\sigma=2\rho\theta-g'y+k,\\
\lambda,\omega,\kappa,\zeta\in L,\qquad \theta_v\in L^* .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{9}
\]
The leading coefficient of \(z\) is exactly one. No lower
coefficient potential has been suppressed.

## 5. An actual auxiliary derivation and its primitive part

Extend \(N\) to \(B=A[u]\) by \(Nu=0\). It commutes with \(E\),
and \(Na=0\). Define on the actual polynomial ring
\[
\mathcal P=aN-(NX)E .                                             \tag{10}
\]
Then \(\mathcal PX=0\), \([\mathcal P,E]=0\), and \(\mathcal P\)
maps \(B\) into \(B\). On \(B_a=A_a[X]\) it fixes \(X\) and
restricts to \(aN\) on \(A_a\), so it is locally nilpotent.
Its restriction to \(B\) is therefore locally nilpotent as well.
On \(B_L=L[v][X]\), it is \(a\partial_v\). In the branch (9),
put \(\tau=a\theta_v\in L^*\). The actual coordinate identities give
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathcal PX=\mathcal PY=0,\qquad
\mathcal PW=\tau(X+\lambda),\qquad \mathcal P^2W=0,\\
\mathcal PZ=\tau(2\theta+\kappa+2\rho X),\qquad
\mathcal P^2Z=2\tau^2\ne0,\qquad \mathcal P^3Z=0 .
\end{gathered}                                                    \tag{11}
\]

Let \(\Delta\) be a gcd in \(B\) of the coordinate components
of \(\mathcal P\). The image ideal \(I=(\mathcal P(B))\) is stable
under both \(\mathcal P\) and \(E\), the latter because they commute.
Writing \(I=\Delta I_0\), with gcd of \(I_0\) equal to one,
shows \(\Delta\mid\mathcal P\Delta\) and \(\Delta\mid E\Delta\):
apply the derivation to each generator of \(\Delta I_0\) and use
the gcd-one condition. A locally nilpotent derivation cannot take
a nonzero polynomial to a nonzero multiple of itself. This follows
from additivity of its degree on products. Hence
\[
\mathcal P\Delta=E\Delta=0,\qquad
D:=\mathcal P/\Delta\in\operatorname{LND}(B),\qquad [D,E]=0 .
\tag{12}
\]
The last local nilpotence also follows directly from
\(D^j=\mathcal P^j/\Delta^j\).
The derivation \(D\) is irreducible by its gcd construction.
In \(L[v][X]\), the common kernel of \(\mathcal P=a\partial_v\)
and \(E=a\partial_X\) is \(L\), so \(\Delta\in L^*\).
This is not a statement that \(\Delta\) is a unit in \(B\).

Set \(\delta=\tau/\Delta\in L^*\). Equations (11)--(12) give
\[
DX=DY=0,\quad DW=\delta(X+\lambda),\quad D^2W=0,\quad
D^2Z=2\delta^2\ne0,\quad D^3Z=0 .                                 \tag{13}
\]

## 6. The unit contradiction takes place in the original ring

Use the actual UFD \(S_0=\mathbb C[X,Y]\) and its relative
polynomial ring \(B=S_0[W,Z]\). By (13), \(D\) is an irreducible
\(S_0\)-locally nilpotent derivation with \(D^2W=0\).
[Dasgupta--Gupta, Lemma 2.3(i)](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.03635v1)
applies to the ordered relative variables \((W,Z)\) and gives
\[
DW\in S_0,\qquad DZ\in S_0[W].                                    \tag{14}
\]
Only the UFD assertion is used, not the separate PID slice assertion.
As \(DW\ne0\) and \(D\) kills \(S_0\),
\[
0=D^3Z=(DW)^2\partial_W^2(DZ).
\]
Characteristic zero implies \(DZ=A_1W+B_1\) for \(A_1,B_1\in S_0\).
Thus \(D^2Z=A_1DW\in S_0\).

The exact field and degree hypotheses imply
\[
S_0\cap\ker E=\mathbb C.                                         \tag{15}
\]
Indeed, if \(H(X,Y)\) has ordinary degree \(d>0\) and top homogeneous
part \(H_d\), then \(E^dH=d!a^dH_d(1,t)\ne0\).
The last nonvanishing uses the transcendence of \(t\) over
\(\mathbb C\). Thus no such \(H\) lies in \(\ker E\).

But \(D^2Z=2\delta^2\in L^*\) is fixed by \(E\).
Equations (13)--(15) therefore imply
\[
A_1DW=D^2Z\in\mathbb C^* .
\]
Both factors are actual elements of \(S_0\), so \(DW\) is an
actual unit of \(S_0\), hence a nonzero complex constant.
On the other hand, (13) in the faithful localization gives
\[
E(DW)=a\delta\ne0,                                                \tag{16}
\]
because \(E\) fixes \(L\) and \(EX=a\). This is a contradiction.
Consequently \(y\notin L\).

The argument does not promote an arbitrary element of \(L^*\)
to a unit of the original ring. The unit is forced by an explicit
product equal to a nonzero constant inside \(S_0\).

## 7. Exact scope

For each full kernel in (2), the actual exchanged chart satisfies
\(R\ne0\) and \(\deg_v y\ge1\). The proof of the constant-first
exclusion uses the form \(q=\rho^2-g(t)\), rather than a special
numerical value of the derivatives of \(g\). The preceding
zero-return exclusion additionally uses \(g'''\ne0\).

This note leaves nonconstant \(y\) untouched. It does not identify
the generic line coordinate with an original ambient coordinate,
settle the two full models, or give an arbitrary-frame entry theorem.
The written argument uses the retained actual-chart construction
and the cited published UFD result. Finite algebra controls and
separate AI reviews cannot replace those inputs or human expert review.
No proof-assistant certification, historical novelty, external contact,
submission or unrestricted cancellation solution is asserted.
