# A uniform conormal chart and cubic residue bounds

In the actual proper-image whole-double alternative, a constant linear
combination of the two base parameters has nonzero transverse differential
along the entire reduced fiber. In its formal transverse chart, the leading
canonical derivation has constant coefficients. This does not require the
original speed normal to be nonzero or an original affine-linear equation
to lie in the whole fiber ideal.

The chart bounds cubic residues more sharply than reduced constancy alone.
If the first base jet of h is nonzero, every cubic residue belongs to the
span of 1 and G. If that jet vanishes, the residue lies in the span of
1, G and Gc. These are necessary conditions, not an exclusion of the
proper-image alternative or a proof of cancellation.

## 1. Actual hypotheses

Use the complete actual setup of
[product-index112, Section 1](whole-double-product-index-pass112.md).
In particular R=S_m is the regular local invariant surface, with centered
polynomial base parameters s1,s2, and A is the actual localized smooth
threefold. Its canonical primitive LND N has full kernel R. Write
F_j=ker N^(j+1), and retain
\[
\begin{gathered}
\mathfrak q=\sqrt{\mathfrak mA},\qquad
A/\mathfrak mA=\mathbb C[c,G]/(G^2),\qquad
A/\mathfrak q=\mathbb C[c],\qquad
\bar N=\lambda G\partial_c,\quad\lambda\in\mathbb C^*,\\
F_2=R\{1,G,H\},\quad H\in\mathfrak mA,\quad
NG=h+\varepsilon br,\quad r=aG+bH,\\
a,b,h\in\mathfrak m,\quad \varepsilon\in R^*,\qquad
N(F_3)\subseteq\mathfrak mF_2,\qquad c\in F_4.
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
Here the same symbol G denotes the actual degree-two lift and its
nilpotent residue. The exact whole double and nonzero constant lambda
are the all-point results111. The last derivative containment is
product-index112, equation (7), using the actual all-divisor degree
modules, not only the displayed fiber algebra.

The all-point quotient-rank result retained in111 says that the two
base differentials span a one-dimensional nonzero space at every point
of the reduced line C=Spec(A/q). More precisely, the local description
in fixed-fiber111, Section 4, gives mA=(x,y squared) and q=(x,y)
locally along C. The residue of G is a generator of the nilradical.

## 2. Global conormal generators along the affine line

Since A is regular and C is a regular closed subscheme, this is a regular
immersion of codimension two. Its conormal module q/q squared is locally
free of rank two. The image M of mA in this conormal module is a rank-one
subbundle: locally in the preceding description it is generated by x.
The class of G complements M at every point. These statements concern
the reduced line, not an assumed equality of global ideals.

Every projective rank-one module over the PID C[c] is free. Choose a
generator of M. Because the two base images generate M, it is the image
of an actual element F=P(c)s1+Q(c)s2, for polynomials P,Q. Thus
\[
\begin{gathered}
F\in\mathfrak mA,\qquad
\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^2=\mathbb C[c]\{[F],[G]\},\\
\operatorname{gr}_{\mathfrak q}A
 \simeq\operatorname{Sym}_{\mathbb C[c]}(\mathfrak q/\mathfrak q^2)
 \simeq\mathbb C[c][[F],[G]],\\
\widehat A_{\mathfrak q}\simeq\mathbb C[c][[F,G]].
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
\]
The middle right-hand expression is a polynomial algebra in the two
conormal classes. The last expression is a formal power-series algebra.
The actual subalgebra C[c] splits the reduction to A/q. Consequently
substitution of the actual F,G gives a continuous homomorphism from
the power-series algebra to the q-adic completion. Its associated
graded map is the displayed isomorphism. Degree-by-degree lifting and
completeness give surjectivity; separatedness and the first nonzero
homogeneous coefficient give injectivity. This proves the last line.

The regular-immersion and conormal facts are standard; the precise
references are [Stacks0E9J](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0E9J),
[Stacks063M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/063M) and its
[symmetric-algebra map0632](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0632).
There is no assertion that q=(F,G) in the uncompleted A or that F is
N-invariant. Its coefficients may depend on c.

## 3. The volume factor is constant on the whole reduced line

The actual stable polynomial presentation B=A[u] supplies a nowhere-zero
three-form omega on A: evaluate the coefficients of its polynomial
four-volume at u=0, retaining du, and identify that result with
omega wedge du. The local argument is
[fixed-scheme92, Section 1](nonconical-fixed-scheme-structure-pass92.md).
The same construction is global, since det(Omega_A direct-sum A du)
is canonically det(Omega_A) after choosing du.

The forms dF,dG,dc are a differential basis along every point of C.
Their determinant relative to omega is therefore a unit of C[c], hence
a nonzero constant. In the formal chart, with a consistent orientation,
the actual canonical derivation consequently has the form
\[
\begin{gathered}
N=\nu(F,G,c)\operatorname{Jac}_{F,G,c}(s_1,s_2,\,\cdot\,),
\qquad \nu_0:=\nu|_C\in\mathbb C^*,\\
\operatorname{wt}F=2,\quad\operatorname{wt}G=1,
\quad\operatorname{wt}\mathbb C[c]=0,\\
s_i=w_i(c)F+t_i(c)G^2+O_{\rm wt}(3),\qquad
w_i,t_i\in\mathbb C[c].
\end{gathered}
\tag{3}
\]
There is no G-linear term: the base conormal image is M. The vector
w=(w1,w2) is unimodular over C[c], since the two base images generate
the line with basis F. Primes below denote differentiation in c.

These weights define the finite leading terms of the q-adic expansions;
the two filtrations are cofinal. The induced formal derivation raises
weight by at least one. Indeed its F, G and c coefficients have weights
at least three, two and one respectively. It extends continuously
because N preserves q. No assertion makes the completed derivation
locally nilpotent.

## 4. The base conormal vector is affine

Put d(c)=det(w,t). The leading term of Nc is 2 nu0 d(c) G. In the
whole double, F and G squared vanish, so comparison with (1) gives
d=lambda/(2 nu0), a nonzero constant. Let the first base jet of h be
ell1 s1+ell2 s2 modulo m squared, for constants ell1,ell2.
The term br in NG has weight at least five: b has weight at least two,
r=aG+bH has weight at least three, and H belongs to mA.

Comparing the weight-two coefficients in the canonical Jacobian and
the actual formula for NG gives
\[
\begin{gathered}
d=\det(w,t)=\lambda/(2\nu_0)\in\mathbb C^*,\\
(NG)_{[2]}=-\nu_0\{\det(w,w')F+\det(w,t')G^2\}
             =(\ell w)F+(\ell t)G^2,\\
\det(w,w')=-\ell w/\nu_0,\qquad
\det(w,t')=-\ell t/\nu_0,\qquad
\det(w',t)=\ell t/\nu_0,\\
w'=\frac{(\ell t)w-(\ell w)t}{\nu_0d}
    =\frac1{\nu_0}(\ell_2,-\ell_1)^t.
\end{gathered}
\tag{4}
\]
The third determinant identity differentiates the constant d. Expressing
w prime in the basis w,t gives the last line, including its sign.
Thus w(c)=w0+c w1 is affine. In particular w prime is zero exactly
when h belongs to m squared.

If w1=0, choose a constant row L nonzero on w0. If w1 is nonzero,
w0,w1 are linearly independent: dependence would make w vanish at a
complex point, contrary to unimodularity. A constant row L annihilating
w1 then has Lw0 nonzero. In both cases
\[
x=L(s_1,s_2)^t,\qquad Lw(c)=\kappa\in\mathbb C^*.
\tag{5}
\]
This is an actual invariant base coordinate with nonzero transverse
differential everywhere on C. It is not asserted to be an ambient
polynomial coordinate of A or B.

## 5. The constant leading weighted field

Complete L to an invertible constant two-by-two matrix with second row
J, and put f=J(s1,s2)^t. Replacing F by x in the formal chart is valid:
its conormal change has invertible coefficient kappa. With the same
weights for x,G and with the new constant volume factor denoted nu0,
one obtains
\[
\begin{gathered}
f=A(c)x+B G^2+O_{\rm wt}(3),\qquad
A(c)\text{ affine},\quad B\in\mathbb C^*,\\
N=\nu(x,G,c)(f_G\partial_c-f_c\partial_G),\qquad
\nu|_C=\nu_0\in\mathbb C^*,\\
N_* =\gamma x\partial_G+\lambda G\partial_c,
\qquad \gamma=-\nu_0A',\quad\lambda=2\nu_0B,\\
\gamma=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad h\in\mathfrak m^2.
\end{gathered}
\tag{6}
\]
For precision, before renaming the volume factor the coefficient of G
squared is det(L,J)d/kappa. It is therefore constant and nonzero. The
coefficient of x is Jw/kappa, which is affine. The Jacobian factor on
C changes by the constant kappa/det(L,J), so it stays constant and
keeps the same lambda in Nc. If w prime is nonzero, Jw prime cannot
vanish because Lw prime=0 and the two rows are independent. This proves
the final equivalence in (6).

Only the leading operator is claimed to have this simple form. Higher
terms of f and nu are not removed; there is no polynomial normal form,
original-normal-ideal equality or original partial-derivative stability.

## 6. Cubic residues

By product-index112, equation (8), the reduced residue of every V in F3
is constant. Subtract it and write its whole residue as G beta(c).
In the chart V=G beta(c)+O_wt(2), since its difference from this actual
polynomial belongs to mA. Write NV=A0+A1G+A2H. Equation (1) puts all
three coefficients in m. Only A0 contributes weight two; its first
base jet has the form u x+v f for constants u,v. Hence
\[
\begin{gathered}
\gamma\beta(c)x+\lambda\beta'(c)G^2
       =(u+vA(c))x+vB G^2,\\
\beta'=vB/\lambda=v/(2\nu_0),\qquad
\gamma\beta'=vA'=-v\gamma/\nu_0,\qquad
\frac{3\gamma v}{2\nu_0}=0.
\end{gathered}
\tag{7}
\]
The remainder of V contributes weight at least three after one
derivative. If gamma is nonzero then v=0 and beta is constant. If
gamma is zero, beta is at most affine. Since the actual elements 1,G
belong to F3, the image W3 of F3 in the whole fiber satisfies
\[
\boxed{\begin{array}{ll}
h\notin\mathfrak m^2:& W_3=\mathbb C\{1,G\},\\
h\in\mathfrak m^2:&
 \mathbb C\{1,G\}\subseteq W_3
 \subseteq\mathbb C\{1,G,Gc\}.
\end{array}}
\tag{8}
\]
The second line does not assert that Gc has an actual cubic lift.
Neither line gives a hidden quadratic component parameter. The actual
c still has degree exactly four by product-index112.

## 7. Scope and remaining gap

This argument replaces a previously unavailable constant leading chart
with an all-point conormal construction for the supplied whole-double
frame. It does not place an arbitrary cancellation input in that frame.
Even inside the frame, (8) is a restriction on a hypothetical proper
branch, not a contradiction or an actual realization of that branch.

The actual all-point rank, smoothness, canonical volume, whole-double
action, free degree modules and divisor valuations remain substantive
retained inputs. The cited Stacks facts justify the conormal step only.
Finite algebraic checks can verify the determinant and connection
identities, not those geometric hypotheses. Separate AI reconstruction
does not establish human expert review, formal verification or novelty.
