# Primary-source scope for intrinsic rank and integral realization

The comparison supports the stated hypotheses of the current deductions.
It does not establish novelty or an exhaustive absence of prior results.
The complex threefold cancellation implication remains unproved here.

## Statement comparison

Gupta's [ICM survey](https://ems.press/content/book-chapter-files/33171),
Sections 2-3, supplies the exact cancellation statement, the separation
from positive characteristic and the recognition-theorem context.
The original email's dimension and unspecified field are not silently
treated as confirmed. A current search returned no primary solution
adopted in this pass. This is a bounded search statement, not a universal
claim about all publications.

Petitjean's [second-kind Koras-Russell paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.06087)
was directly read, including Introduction and Section 2. It explicitly
gives a smooth contractible threefold family with all additive actions
invariant replicas of a primitive action. It does not establish that
these threefolds have polynomial cylinders. The statement is useful
only to exclude a deduction from contractibility alone.

The [Stacks geometric-regularity criterion](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/038U)
was directly read with proof. Characteristic zero makes the generic
coefficient field perfect. This justifies preservation of regularity,
not factoriality. The [Weil-divisor definitions and exact presentation](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BE0)
were also directly read; the class-group calculation reconstructs the
localization relations instead of presenting the machinery as new.

Sai Krishna's [affine-space characterization paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.05424),
Theorem 2.9 and Corollary 2.10, retains geometric factoriality when
passing from algebraically closed to arbitrary fields. They are recalled
statements there, not new proofs of those two inputs. An ordinary generic UFD does not satisfy
that stronger hypothesis automatically.

Kaliman's [final free-action paper](https://www.math.miami.edu/~kaliman/library/Ka.invent.2004.c%2B.pdf)
was directly read through Proposition 3.2's complete support argument,
Proposition 4.2's proof, and the retained-curve statement of Theorem 5.2.
The integer argument uses its quotient diagram with retained components.
No globally free-action theorem is applied to an action with a fixed line.
The new local lattice restriction is a deduction from that framework,
not a newly discovered topological method.

Freudenburg's [local-slice construction](https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/4752/ojm34_04_02.pdf),
Section 4.1, and [canonical factorization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.08786),
Sections 8.2.1-8.2.2, supply the exact established crossing-plinth action.
Those passages were independently read by the intrinsic-rank author.
The main audit reconstructed the ensuing boundary-field and divisor
calculations. Its rank-three action is not relabeled as a new example.

Alhajjar's [semi-rigid-domain paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.00445),
Propositions 3.2-3.3, was directly read by the intrinsic-rank author.
The rank-one formulation uses its exact hypotheses, and the current
argument does not infer stability of that invariant under a cylinder.

## Search and access boundaries

Broad web searches also returned AI research pages and nonprimary
summaries. They were not used as independent mathematical evidence or
as proof of the conjecture's current status. In particular, a page
repeating the same proposed leading-term route cannot independently
validate that route.

The exact statements from previous passes remain linked in the proofs.
The inaccessible full text of Freudenburg's 2009 slice article was not
newly obtained or represented as read. No paywall bypass, credentialed
search, expert correspondence or new external publication occurred.

No statement here establishes priority for the marked repair family,
the conditional isolation theorem, or the explicit class-group
specialization. These remain research deductions awaiting human review.
