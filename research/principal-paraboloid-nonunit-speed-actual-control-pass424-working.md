# An actual principal chart with nonunit speed and no slice for its selected top

This explicit globally sliced polynomial example satisfies the
principal1222 chart264 with nonconstant EX. Its literal selected
leading derivation has no global slice on the original invariant
ring, although the localized intrinsic line has a parameter v
with Nv=1. The example tests the distinction between original
polynomial coordinates and coefficient-field coordinates.

It belongs to the zero-coupling branch k=0, not the nonzero
constant-coupling theorem422. It is not a cancellation
counterexample, a counterexample to264, or evidence of a gap
in a proof that explicitly preserves the localization boundary.
No historical novelty is claimed for this control.

## 1. A genuine polynomial coordinate frame and its inverse

Start with
\[
B=\mathbb C[u,T,V,H],\qquad E=\partial_u,\qquad
A=\ker E=\mathbb C[T,V,H].
\tag{1}
\]
Set
\[
X=V+Tu,\qquad Y=T+uX,\qquad Z=H+u^2,
\qquad W=u+X^2Z-Y^2.
\tag{2}
\]
These are actual polynomial coordinates, with the explicit inverse
\[
\begin{aligned}
u&=W-X^2Z+Y^2,\\
T&=Y-uX,\\
V&=X-Tu,\\
H&=Z-u^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3}
\]
The right sides are composed in the indicated order, so every
inverse is polynomial in X,Y,Z,W. Substitution proves both inverse
orders without dividing by any nonconstant polynomial.

The complete time expansion of the last coordinate is
\[
W=V^2H-T^2+\bigl(1+2TV(H-1)\bigr)u+T^2(H-2)u^2.
\tag{4}
\]
The exact E-degrees are(1,2,2,2), with leaders
\[
(a,b,c,e)=(T,T,1,T^2(H-2)).
\tag{5}
\]
All are nonzero polynomials. In particular a=EX=T is a nonunit
of A and B, even though E has the original global slice u.

## 2. The full initial kernel really is the principal paraboloid

For an independent indeterminate zeta, the weighted initial map is
\[
(\mathsf X,\mathsf Y,\mathsf Z,\mathsf W)
\longmapsto
(T\zeta,T\zeta^2,\zeta^2,T^2(H-2)\zeta^2).
\tag{6}
\]
The relation mathsfX^2*mathsfZ-mathsfY^2 vanishes. Its source
polynomial is irreducible: viewed over C[mathsfX,mathsfY] as a
primitive linear polynomial in mathsfZ, its coefficients
mathsfX^2 and -mathsfY^2 are coprime, and it is irreducible over
the fraction field. Adjoining mathsfW preserves this property.

The fraction field of the initial image is C(T,H,zeta).
Indeed zeta=mathsfY/mathsfX after evaluation, T=mathsfX/zeta,
and H=2+mathsfW/(T^2*zeta^2). The converse inclusion is immediate.
It has transcendence degree three, so the source kernel is a
height-one prime. The displayed irreducible relation generates
that entire prime:
\[
I_{\rm initial}=(\mathsf X^2\mathsf Z-\mathsf Y^2).
\tag{7}
\]
This verifies the full-kernel hypothesis, not merely one relation.

## 3. The literal Z-partial and its exact leading derivation

Differentiate the ACTUAL inverse(3) by Z while fixing X,Y,W.
It gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\partial_Zu&=-X^2,\\
\partial_ZT&=X^3,\\
\partial_ZV&=-uX^3+TX^2,\\
\partial_ZH&=1+2uX^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]
Substituting X=V+Tu shows that the first, second and fourth
expressions have u-degree at most three, whereas the third
has u^4 coefficient -T^3. Since A is generated by T,V,H,
the selected horizontal shift is exactly four and
\[
\boxed{N=[u^4]\partial_Z|_A=-T^3\partial_V.}
\tag{9}
\]
The derivation rule bounds the shift on every element of A;
V attains degree four. The vertical value in(8) has degree two.
Thus the exact all-ring shift is also four.

The kernel and its field are
\[
\ker_A N=\mathbb C[T,H],\qquad L=\mathbb C(T,H).
\tag{10}
\]
This follows directly by differentiating polynomials in V,
using characteristic zero and the nonzero factor T^3.

There is no global slice for N on A: every N-value is divisible
by T^3. In fact N(A)=T^3 A, since partial_V is surjective on
polynomials in characteristic zero. Nevertheless
\[
v=-V/T^3\in L[V],\qquad Nv=1,\qquad
L[A]=L[V]=L[v].
\tag{11}
\]
The element v is NOT in A, since the prime T does not divide V.
It has a genuine pole at T=0. No ambient-coordinate conclusion
about this particular v follows from(11).

## 4. Complete fixed-X chart and both actual returns

The normalized leading ratios are
\[
t=b/a^2=1/T,\qquad s=e/a^2=H-2,
\qquad L=\mathbb C(t,s),\qquad a=1/t.
\tag{12}
\]
Using u=(X-V)/T and V=-v/t^3 in the full original rows gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
Y&=y+\sigma X+tX^2,&y&=1/t,&\sigma&=v/t^2,\\
Z&=z+2t\sigma X+t^2X^2,&z&=s+2+v^2/t^4,\\
W&=w+\theta X+sX^2,&w&=(v-1)/t^2,&\theta&=t-2v/t^3.
\end{aligned}
\tag{13}
\]
Thus the coupling is k=0. The first two complete potentials264
are realized by
\[
n=s,\qquad\ell=t,\qquad
z=2ty+\sigma^2+n,\qquad 2\sigma y+\theta=\ell.
\tag{14}
\]
Here m=k_s=0. The second identity is the undivided second
potential, with F(P,Q)=n_tP+n_sQ=Q. No formula requiring m
nonzero is applied.

The scalar determinant is exactly
\[
\boxed{\det\frac{\partial(Y,Z,W)}{\partial(t,s,v)}
=-1/t^4=-a^4,}
\tag{15}
\]
for the full rows(13), at every formal X. It is a unit of L,
not a unit of the original A: in A it is -T^4.

Whole line return is direct since w is affine in v over L:
\[
L[y,z,w]=L[v]=L[y,\sigma,\theta].
\tag{16}
\]
The stronger complex FIELD return also holds. Indeed
\[
t=1/y,\qquad
v=(w+y^2)/y^2,\qquad
s=z-(w+y^2)^2-2.
\tag{17}
\]
Conversely all three intercepts belong to C(t,s,v), so
\[
\operatorname{Frac}A=\mathbb C(t,s,v)=\mathbb C(y,z,w).
\tag{18}
\]
The exact localized ring statement is
\[
A_T=\mathbb C[y,z,w]_y.
\tag{19}
\]
To check it, recover T=y, V=-y(w+y^2), and
H=z-(w+y^2)^2, while w=-V/T-T^2 lies in A_T.
The element w is not in A. Therefore(18) is not an assertion
that the particular intercepts are polynomial coordinates of A.

## 5. The scope controls supplied by this example

All of the following coexist in one actual polynomial model:

- The global slice u and the full principal1222 initial kernel.
- Exact shift-four selected partial, both actual returns, and
  the complete calibrated scalar.
- A nonunit original speed EX, a selected intrinsic derivation
  with no global slice, and a localized parameter outside A.

Thus none of the following upgrades is licensed solely by those
facts: EX is a complex unit; the selected top has a global slice;
its chosen generic parameter belongs to A; or the original
intercepts form an unlocalized polynomial coordinate system.
This example verifies why264 and422 retain those distinctions.
It does not identify an instance where either note makes an
invalid upgrade.

Because k_s=0, the example neither realizes the nonzero-coupling
family422 nor proves it empty. Its nonvacuity conclusion concerns
the underlying principal chart, with a nonunit original speed.
