# The selected-divisor and three-good entry gaps

The local family exclusions do not yet provide a general reduction
for cancellation. This audit isolates one sufficient, still unproved
divisor assertion within the supplied dense three-good setting, then
separates it from the problem of obtaining that setting at all.
It records dependencies and scope, not a new classification theorem.

## 1. The exact supplied setting

Retain an actual polynomial coordinate system and an actual slice:
\[
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,W]=A[u],\qquad
E=\partial_u,\qquad E^2x_i=0,\qquad a_i=Ex_i.
\tag{1}
\]
Assume \(\operatorname{trdeg}_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C(a_1,a_2,a_3)=2\)
and that no nonzero homogeneous polynomial over \(\mathbb C\)
vanishes on the three speeds. The latter is the dense-cone
condition. There is no bound on the finite degree \(\deg_E W\).
The supplied frame, not a merely formal list of speeds, is essential.

The retained actual chart gives a nonzero intrinsic LND
\(N=[u^2](T|_A)\), where \(T=\partial_W\). Put \(S=\ker_A N\).
The intrinsic quotient theorem and its foundation audit supply
\(S\simeq\mathbb C^{[2]}\) and faithful flatness of the actual
inclusion \(S\subset A\). These are imported geometric results,
not consequences of generic-line generation alone.[^1]

For each prime element \(p\in S\), write
\[
D_p=S_{(p)},\qquad k_p=\operatorname{Frac}(S/pS),\qquad
A_{D_p}/pA_{D_p}=\ell_p[\xi_p],\qquad
1\le [\ell_p:k_p]\le\mu+1,
\quad
\mu=\min_{P\in\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]}\deg_E(W+P).
\tag{2}
\]
Here \(\ell_p\) is the relative algebraic constant field of the
full divisor fiber, finite separable over \(k_p\). The existence
of this polynomial-line presentation and the bound are the retained
147 result, using 145 for the fixed-good minimum. At each divisor
one may reselect a good coordinate whose speed is not divisible by
\(p\); this does not change \(N,S,\mu\).[^2]

The ring in (2) is the full localized invariant algebra. It is not
the smaller order generated by only two projected goods. In
particular, a degeneration of a cleared projected plane equation
is not automatically a degeneration of this full fiber.

## 2. A precise unrestricted selected-divisor assertion

The proposed sufficient lemma is: for every actual frame satisfying
(1) and the two speed hypotheses above, the selected intrinsic
derivation satisfies
\[
\ell_p=k_p\qquad\text{for every prime element }p\in S.
\tag{3}
\]

This is unproved. It imposes no last-coordinate slice \(W=u\),
triangular model for the embedded quotient, affine placement of
the projected goods, bound on \(\mu\), or integrality of a chosen
normalized intrinsic parameter. A proof must handle arbitrary
nonlinear projected placements allowed by the actual inverse.

Assertion (3) is a sufficient strategy, not a claimed necessary
condition for \(A\) to be polynomial. An actual selected-frame
counterexample would invalidate this strategy without disproving
cancellation. The degree inequality in (2) does not establish (3).

## 3. Why this assertion would be enough within the supplied setting

Divide \(N\) by its global invariant content in the UFD \(A\):
\[
N=c\delta,\qquad 0\ne c\in S,\qquad
\delta\text{ an irreducible LND of }A,\qquad
\ker\delta=S.
\tag{4}
\]
Here irreducible means that the image of \(\delta\) has no common
nonunit factor. Invariant content division and the retained plinth
argument apply to \(A\), whose factoriality and constant units
follow from \(A[u]\simeq\mathbb C^{[4]}\).[^3]

A prime \(p\in S\) remains prime in \(A\) by factorial closure.
The reduction \(\bar\delta\) on \(A/pA\) is nonzero, since otherwise
\(\delta(A)\subset pA\). Locally, the maximal \(p\)-content division
of \(N\) used in pass 147 differs from \(\delta\) by a unit in \(D_p\).
It therefore gives the same residue constant field. Localization
by \(S\setminus(p)\) commutes with the invariant kernel, because
these denominators are fixed and the residue ring is a domain.
Consequently
\[
\operatorname{Frac}\ker(\bar\delta:A/pA\longrightarrow A/pA)
=\ell_p.
\tag{5}
\]

Faithful flatness makes the primitive plinth principal:
\(\delta(A)\cap S=\alpha S\). The exact support test proved in pass 27
is \(p\mid\alpha\) if and only if the field degree in (5) is
greater than one. Thus (3) would force \(\alpha\in S^*\), give
a \(\delta\)-slice \(v\), and yield \(A=S[v]\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\).
This uses the actual base inclusion throughout.[^3]

There is also an exact published endpoint. Masuda's Theorem 3.5
assumes a factorial affine complex source, an irreducible LND,
an affine invariant ring, and a faithfully flat quotient. A
trivial \(\mathbb A^1\)-bundle is equivalent to: outside some
principal divisor all closed fibers are \(\mathbb A^1\), and
the invariant quotient of each prime boundary maps birationally
to that boundary.[^4]

All but this last birationality condition are already supplied
here. Indeed, a local slice \(z\) with
\(0\ne b=\delta z\in S\) gives \(A_b=S_b[z]\); if \(b\) is a
unit, recognition is immediate. Otherwise \(V(b)\) is the
required principal divisor. Equation (5) identifies its
birationality condition exactly with \(\ell_p=k_p\) at its prime
factors. Flatness and the shape \(\ell_p[\xi_p]\) alone do not
satisfy that extra condition.

This is a sufficient-chain audit using the displayed primary
statement and proof, not a new audit of every classification
dependency behind the retained quotient theorem.

## 4. What the family exclusions actually contribute

If the original bad coordinate is the given slice, then
\[
W=u
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
A\simeq B/uB
=\mathbb C[\bar x_1,\bar x_2,\bar x_3]
\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus the \(W=u\) line-family exclusions in passes 148 and 149 take place in
an already-polynomial invariant algebra. They are useful tests
of proposed realizations and restrictions on how a selected
intrinsic kernel can occur. They do not recognize a previously
unknown \(A\). Further extensions within \(W=u\) have the same
limitation. Putting an arbitrary given slice into a polynomial
coordinate slot is a conclusion to prove, not a free
normalization.[^5]

The all-degree projected exclusions in passes 148 and 149 are less restrictive
than \(W=u\), but still prescribe an embedded triangular quotient
model and particular affine or nonlinear projected placements.
Neither pass 139 nor pass 147 supplies a reduction to all these models.
Excluding another such placement therefore does not establish
(3) without an additional general reduction. The generic
critical-determinant condition and its already-retained origin
are recorded in pass 149, Section 3; they are not a new divisor
condition here.[^6]

Two retained controls prevent overly strong substitutions for (3).
First, an ordinary polynomial threefold can have a primitive
intrinsic LND with a nontrivial divisor constant extension, as
in pass 27, Section 4. Any proof of (3) must therefore use the selected
origin \(N=[u^2]\partial_W|_A\), not just factoriality,
contractibility, or an arbitrary additive action.[^3]
Second, the actual inverse-verified sharp family in pass 140 has
\(A\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\) and unbounded \(\mu=d-1\) as its
construction polynomial has degree \(d\ge2\). Forcing the
fixed-good minimum to be zero is false even in actual dense
frames. This is a retained scope boundary, not a new
counterexample or a failure of generic primitivity.[^7]

## 5. The two separate next questions

Within (1), the open question is whether the actual polynomial
inverse prevents every full divisor constant extension, as in
(3), or provides a different global descent mechanism that also
handles arbitrary nonlinear projected pairs. The generic
first-tail factor problem closed in pass 145 is not being reopened.

Before (1), no theorem in the retained argument puts an arbitrary
sliced LND on \(\mathbb C^{[4]}\) into three polynomial coordinate
slots annihilated by \(E^2\). Establishing (3) for the supplied
dense profile would not provide that entry theorem or handle
all other possible profiles. An unrestricted cancellation
argument must supply such an entry route with its remaining
cases, or use a different route.

These are independent logical gaps. The family calculations
are not evidence that either general gap is nearly resolved.
This note makes no new realization, classification, formal-proof,
or literature-completeness claim.
