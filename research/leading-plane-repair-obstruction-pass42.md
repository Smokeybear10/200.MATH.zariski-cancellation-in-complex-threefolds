# No correction within the displayed leading plane repairs the highest field

In the actual degree-two, rank-two-speed residual, the natural
slice-corrected highest field does descend to the sliced quotient.
It need not be locally nilpotent for any choice of slice. A genuine
polynomial example excludes every polynomial-coefficient correction
by the entire displayed leading module, not only one chosen correction.
All those corrected fields even have unit derivative ideal.

This is an obstruction to a specified repair procedure. It does not
obstruct a different coordinate frame, non-leading corrections, or
cancellation. The example already has an explicit polynomial quotient.

## 1. The general slice correction really descends

Use the complete assumptions of
[the degree-two residual](two-quadratic-degree-two-pass42.md).
Thus \(E\) is a sliced LND on \(B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]\),
\(E^3x=0\), the good partials have vanishing second adjoint,
and the two quadratic defects \(V_3,V_4\) are independent.
Write
\[
h=E^2x,
\quad H=h\cdot\partial_x,
\quad f=Ex_3,
\quad g=Ex_4.
\]
The actual highest-coefficient identity in the linked proof gives
\[
L:=[E,H]=-\frac12(fV_3+gV_4),
\qquad [E,L]=0.
\tag{1}
\]
The second equality uses \(Ef=Eg=0\) and \([E,V_i]=0\).
The identity is polynomial and holds without evaluating only on a
generic open set.

For any polynomial slice \(\sigma\) of \(E\), put
\[
\alpha_\sigma=H\sigma-\sigma L\sigma,
\qquad
Y_\sigma=H-\sigma L-\alpha_\sigma E.
\tag{2}
\]
One has
\[
E\alpha_\sigma=0,
\qquad [E,Y_\sigma]=0,
\qquad Y_\sigma\sigma=0.
\tag{3}
\]
Indeed \(E(H\sigma)=L\sigma\), while
\(E(\sigma L\sigma)=L\sigma\), because \(E\sigma=1\)
and \(E(L\sigma)=0\). This proves the first equality;
the other two follow directly from (1)--(2).

Consequently \(Y_\sigma\) is an actual polynomial derivation
preserving \(A=\ker E\) and killing the chosen slice in
\(B=A[\sigma]\). Its descent is not the missing step.
Local nilpotence remains a separate question.

## 2. A genuine independent-speed instance

Take actual polynomial coordinates \((u,v,a,c)\), set
\(g=uv-1\), and put
\[
E=u\partial_a+g\partial_c,
\qquad s=va-c,
\qquad r=ga-uc.
\tag{4}
\]
The exact polynomial inverse is
\[
a=us-r,
\qquad c=gs-vr.
\tag{5}
\]
Thus \(Es=1\), \(Er=Eu=Ev=0\), and
\(A=\mathbb C[u,v,r]\) is explicitly polynomial.

Use the actual physical frame
\[
x=(u+a^2/2,\ v+c^2/2,\ a,\ c).
\tag{6}
\]
The already verified brackets, also reconstructed below by the
standalone checker, are
\[
\begin{aligned}
W_1&=-\partial_a-v\partial_c,&
W_2&=-u\partial_c,\\
V_3&=2u(\partial_a+v\partial_c),&
V_4&=2ug\partial_c.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7}
\]
The defects have fraction-field rank two. The bad speeds
\((f,g)=(u,uv-1)\) are algebraically independent: their
Jacobian determinant in \((u,v)\) is \(u\ne0\).
The actual adjoint and shifted slice-filtration profiles are
\((1,1,2,2)\), as in the
[original full-frame construction](two-quadratic-defects-pass39.md).
No rank-one-speed exception is being used.

In the coordinates \((u,v,a,c)\), the highest field and its bracket
are
\[
H=u^2\partial_u+g^2\partial_v,
\qquad
L=-u^2\partial_a-(u^2v+ug^2)\partial_c.
\tag{8}
\]
In particular \([E,L]=0\), in agreement with (1).

## 3. Every leading-module correction still fails

Let the polynomial module of displayed leading fields be
\[
\mathcal M=B E+B W_1+B W_2+B V_3+B V_4.
\tag{9}
\]
This is a module of polynomial derivations; its arbitrary elements
are not asserted to be LNDs or to preserve \(A\).
Every one of its five generators kills \(u\) and \(v\).
Therefore every polynomial field \(X\in H+\mathcal M\)
satisfies the exact identities
\[
X(u)=u^2,
\qquad X(v)=g^2.
\tag{10}
\]
No bound or invariance assumption is placed on the polynomial
coefficients used to form the correction.

Equation (10) implies, by induction,
\[
X^n(u)=n!u^{n+1}\ne0\qquad(n\ge1).
\tag{11}
\]
Thus no field in \(H+\mathcal M\) is locally nilpotent on
\(B\). If such a field preserves \(A\), it is not locally
nilpotent there either, since \(u\in A\).

Moreover its derivative ideal is the unit ideal. The explicit identity
\[
u^2(v^2-2v^2g)+g^2(3+2g)=1
\tag{12}
\]
uses only \(g=uv-1\). Combining (10) and (12) puts one in
the ideal generated by the values of \(X\). Hence every such
field is nowhere zero and has coefficient gcd one in any polynomial
coordinate frame. Dividing a common polynomial factor cannot repair
the failure. A nonzero polynomial multiple also cannot be an LND:
its nonzero value on \(u\) is divisible by \(u\), contradicting
the elementary LND divisibility lemma.

For every polynomial \(E\)-slice \(\sigma\), (1)--(2) give
\(Y_\sigma\in H+\mathcal M\). Equations (3) prove that it
genuinely descends and kills its slice; (10)--(11) prove that its
restriction to \(A\) is not an LND. The conclusion quantifies
over all such slices, not only the one in (4).

## 4. The corrected field on the explicit quotient

For the particular slice \(s\) in (4), direct evaluation gives
\[
Hs=g^2a,
\qquad Ls=ug^2,
\qquad \alpha_s=-g^2r.
\]
Consequently the true slice-killing projection restricts to
\[
Y_s\big|_A
=u^2\partial_u+g^2\partial_v-ug^2r\partial_r.
\tag{13}
\]
It is a polynomial derivation with unit derivative ideal, but
its \(u\)-iterates are precisely (11). The original leading
restrictions have their one common intrinsic direction along
\(\partial_r\). They cannot change (10), regardless of their
polynomial multipliers.

This explains the obstruction to the proposed construction without
identifying an abstract invariant generator with an ambient coordinate.
The successful coordinates (5) already exist for the example.
Nothing here rules out constructing a different intrinsic LND by
using lower coefficients, a different ambient frame, or a correction
outside the module (9).

## Verification and inherited scope

The general descent identity (1)--(3) is proved algebraically.
The obstruction (10)--(11) is an all-coefficients and all-slices
deduction from annihilation of two actual invariant polynomials.
The example, its exact profiles and its polynomial inverse are
inherited from the pass-39 construction; they are not newly proposed
cancellation candidates. No new external classification is used.

The standalone
[four-method checker](leading_plane_repair_checks_pass42.py) imports only
`unittest` and SymPy. Its first run passed all four methods in 0.568 seconds,
with no failed assertions. It reconstructs the actual physical partials,
inverse, profiles and ranks; checks the full leading-module annihilation
and unit-ideal identity; and verifies the genuine slice correction and
quotient formula. Finite slice examples and iterates are controls only,
not machine proofs of the all-slices or all-coefficients quantifiers.

No general cancellation, novelty, expert-review, or formal
proof-assistant claim is made. The result excludes the displayed
repair procedure, not every route from the remaining residual to
an invariant coordinate.
