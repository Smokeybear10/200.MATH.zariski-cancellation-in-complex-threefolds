## Polynomial curve factorization

Michiel de Bondt, *Rational maps H for which K(tH) has transcendence
degree 2 over K*, version 4, Corollary 3.2 and Lemma 3.3,
[primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.06046v4), PDF pp. 20--21.

The root directly reread the retained text, including both complete
proofs. The actual subalgebra must first be proved to have fraction-field
transcendence degree one. The result then supplies a polynomial generator
of that field and the exact intersection with the ambient polynomial
ring. It does not say that the generator is an ambient coordinate.
The ordinary partial-derivative argument supplies that stronger
conclusion only in its separately proved cases.

## Plane LND invariants

Shigeru Kuroda, *The automorphism theorem and additive group actions
on the affine plane*, 2016, Theorem 1.1 and complete proof,
[primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.04070v1), PDF pp. 1--3.

The root directly read the statement and full proof through its end.
The theorem is applied to the restriction of the extracted primitive
field on an actual polynomial two-ring. That restriction is proved
locally nilpotent before application. The zero restriction is handled
separately. The characteristic-zero theorem is credited to Rentschler,
as the primary paper itself records.

## Invariants in a plane inside a larger polynomial ring

Shigeru Kuroda, *Initial forms of stable invariants for additive group
actions*, version 2 dated May 1, 2013, Proposition 3.2 and following
discussion, [primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1304.0313), PDF p. 8.

The root directly read the exact proposition, its characteristic-zero
setup, the subsequent intersection discussion and bibliography. It
states precisely the gate needed in the independent proof: a nonconstant
invariant in an actual coordinate plane implies an invariant coordinate
of that plane, unless the entire plane is already fixed. The ambient
LND is not required to preserve the plane.

The proposition credits Daigle--Freudenburg via Freudenburg's 2006
monograph, Theorem 5.37 and Corollary 5.40, and cites Gene Freudenburg,
*A note on the kernel of a locally nilpotent derivation*, Proceedings
of the AMS 124 (1996), 27--29,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03003-1).
The original publisher PDF was not retrieved by the root; its full
proof is not described as freshly reconstructed.

Versioned arXiv PDF and abstract requests initially returned cache
misses. The unversioned PDF was retrieved and explicitly displays
1304.0313v2 and the May 1, 2013 date. This is a successful same-source
fallback, not an inference from a search snippet.

A third-party search summary incorrectly dropped the two-variable
invariant hypothesis and suggested that every LND fixes a coordinate.
That summary was rejected. Only the precise primary proposition is
used. The apparent shortcut is neither a new theorem nor an
unrestricted cancellation solution.

## Recognition after an invariant coordinate

S. M. Bhatwadekar, N. Gupta and S. A. Lokhande,
*Some K-theoretic properties of the kernel of a locally nilpotent
derivation on k[X1,...,X4]*, version 1,
[primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01438v1),
Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.5, PDF pp. 10--11.

The root directly reread the local argument and final theorem with
their proofs in the retained text. The source requires a nonzero LND
fixing an actual coordinate and a regular kernel. The new proofs
establish the coordinate first. Regularity comes from the original
polynomial slice, not from the auxiliary extracted action or a
hypothetical local slice.
