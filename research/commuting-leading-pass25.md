# Commuting leading fields: the exact polynomial elimination gate

Date: 2026-09-13.

This bounded pass investigates the general setup
\(B=A[s]\cong\mathbb C^{[4]}\), not another restricted coefficient
family. It proves an all-degree criterion for a proposed degree-lowering
operation on two genuine ambient coordinate partials. It also proves
that arbitrary regular saturation and locally nilpotent recombination
inside one primitive intrinsic orbit direction cannot remove a missing
slice.

Neither result supplies the missing universal choice of coordinate
frame. General cancellation remains unresolved. These are elementary
proved repair gates, not a priority or novelty claim. They have not been
expert-reviewed or Lean-verified.

## 1. Established input and the new question

The authoritative [intrinsic-frame repair](intrinsic-frame-repair-pass24.md)
already extracts a nonzero tangent LND from an ambient coordinate partial.
The [earlier filtration note](filtration-obstruction-pass.md) already
proves that commuting ambient coordinate partials have commuting
intrinsic leading LNDs, and exhibits loss of generic rank. We do not
reclassify either fact as a new result.

The homogenization input is precisely the domain, finite-transcendence-
degree statement of Alhajjar, Proposition 2.2, attributed there to
Daigle: a derivation respects the filtration induced by an LND, and its
associated graded derivation is locally nilpotent when the original
derivation is. This statement does not assert that arbitrary lower
coefficients or coefficient-dependent row operations preserve local
nilpotence. [Primary statement, PDF p. 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.00445).

Here is the sharper proposed repair. Suppose two coordinate partials
have equal filtration degree and

\[
\widehat U=h\widehat T,
\qquad h\in A,
\]

with h invariant under their common intrinsic leading direction. The
derivation \(W=U-hT\) has smaller filtration degree. Does the leading
invariance of h suffice to make W another locally nilpotent direction?

The answer is no. The exact condition for this row operation is
invariance under the full ambient T, not its leading field.

## 2. An exact all-degree criterion for a coordinate pair

Let S be a domain containing \(\mathbb Q\), let
\(B=S[u,v]\), and let

\[
T=\partial_u,\qquad U=\partial_v,\qquad
W=U-hT,\qquad h\in S[u,v].
\]

All derivations in this section fix S. Then the following conditions
are equivalent:

1. W is locally nilpotent.
2. \(T(h)=0\).
3. W commutes with T.
4. There is a polynomial F in S[v] such that
   \((u+F(v),v)\) is an S-coordinate pair and W is its second
   coordinate partial.

In particular, when the operation is valid it preserves much more
than an LND: W has the coordinate slice v and a polynomially trivial
translation action.

**Proof.** The bracket is

\[
[T,W]=-T(h)T.
\tag{1}
\]

If \(T(h)=0\), integrate h in v to obtain F in S[v] with F'=h.
Then \(W(u+F)=0\) and W(v)=1. The inverse coordinate substitution
is \(u=(u+F)-F(v)\). This proves sufficiency and the coordinate
claim. Requiring h also to lie in ker U would be unnecessarily strong.

For necessity, localize S to its fraction field K. Local nilpotence
would survive this localization, since W fixes S. Suppose
\(m=\deg_u h\ge1\).

If \(m\ge2\), write \(\ell(v)\ne0\) for the leading u-coefficient
of h. Induction gives

\[
\deg_u W^j(u)=1+j(m-1),
\tag{2}
\]
\[
\operatorname{LC}_u W^j(u)
=(-1)^j\ell(v)^j
  \prod_{i=0}^{j-1}\bigl(1+i(m-1)\bigr)
\ne0.
\tag{3}
\]

Indeed \(\partial_v\) cannot increase u-degree, whereas
\(-h\partial_u\) increases the positive current u-degree by m-1.
The latter term therefore supplies the uncancellable top coefficient.
No iterate of u vanishes.

If m=1, write \(h=\alpha(v)u+\beta(v)\) with nonzero alpha in
K[v]. Express \(W^j(u)=a_j(v)u+b_j(v)\). Then

\[
a_0=1,\qquad a_{j+1}=a_j'-\alpha a_j.
\tag{4}
\]

Put \(e=\deg_v\alpha\). If e is positive, induction gives
\(\deg_v a_j=je\) and leading coefficient
\((-\operatorname{LC}_v\alpha)^j\): the derivative has smaller
degree than the product. If e=0, alpha is a nonzero constant in K,
and \(a_j=(-\alpha)^j\). Thus again no iterate vanishes. This
proves necessity in every polynomial degree. The affine term beta
does not affect the obstruction.

The same proof applies to any two actual coordinate partials in an
ambient polynomial ring, with S generated by the other coordinates.
It is not a criterion for arbitrary commuting LNDs lacking such a
joint coordinate presentation.

## 3. A leading-compatible elimination that fails this gate

Use the already recorded coordinate frame from Section 3 of
[the filtration-obstruction note](filtration-obstruction-pass.md).
On \(\mathbb C[a,b,c,s]\), set

\[
\xi=a-c(sa-s^2b),\qquad
\zeta=b-c(a-sb).
\]

Writing
\(N(s)=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}s&-s^2\\1&-s\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
we have \(N^2=0\) and

\[
\binom\xi\zeta=(I-cN)\binom ab,
\qquad
\binom ab=(I+cN)\binom\xi\zeta.
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently \((\xi,\zeta,c,s)\) is a genuine polynomial coordinate
system. Its partials in c and s, holding the other new coordinates
fixed, are

\[
T_c=\partial_c+(sa-s^2b)\partial_a+(a-sb)\partial_b,
\]
\[
T_s=\partial_s+
(ca-2csb+c^2sa-c^2s^2b)\partial_a
 +(-cb+c^2a-c^2sb)\partial_b.
\tag{6}
\]

Both are commuting coordinate LNDs. Relative to the original s-degree,

\[
\widehat T_c=-bs^2\partial_a,
\qquad
\widehat T_s=-c^2bs^2\partial_a=c^2\widehat T_c.
\tag{7}
\]

The polynomial \(h=c^2\) is invariant under both intrinsic leading
fields. Nevertheless the exact matching subtraction is

\[
W=T_s-c^2T_c
=\partial_s+(ca-2csb)\partial_a-cb\partial_b-c^2\partial_c.
\tag{8}
\]

It does reduce filtration degree from two to one. But

\[
W(s)=1,\qquad W(c)=-c^2,
\qquad W^j(c)=(-1)^j j!c^{j+1}\ne0,
\tag{9}
\]
\[
[T_c,W]=-2cT_c\ne0.
\tag{10}
\]

Thus a smaller degree and even a polynomial g satisfying W(g)=1
do not establish an LND or an additive-group action. Calling g a
slice in the LND sense would already presuppose the missing local
nilpotence. The gate identifies the exact failure:
\(T_c(c^2)=2c\), although the intrinsic top derivation kills c squared.

Within this same equal-degree row operation, an ambient T_c-invariant
coefficient cannot replace c squared while retaining that leading
cancellation: a multiplier of s-degree zero is its own leading
coefficient, which (7) forces to equal c squared. This statement is
only about this operation. It does not exclude changing both columns,
using other coordinates, or temporarily increasing filtration degree.

The underlying frame (5)-(7) is inherited. The assertion tested here
is the additional, coefficient-matched elimination rule (8), not a
new claim that leading rank can collapse.

## 4. Saturating a rank-one leading system cannot repair its primitive plinth

This section applies inherited UFD mechanics, not a new literature
theorem. Stampfli, subsection 4.1, Lemma 4.1.1(vi)-(ix), records the
invariant-multiplier criterion, the irreducible same-kernel description,
existence of an irreducible representative, and the LND divisibility
lemma, citing Freudenburg for their proofs. Subsection 4.2 also records
the change of plinth divisor under an invariant multiplier.
[Primary statements, printed pp. 6-7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.6246v2).
The direct argument below spells out their consequence for a proposed
rank-one repair sequence on the possibly unrecognized UFD A.

The following lemma does not require A to be polynomial. Let A be a
finitely generated characteristic-zero UFD, let K be its fraction
field, and let delta be a nonzero primitive LND on A. Here primitive
means that no nonunit of A divides every value of delta; it does
not mean the image values generate the unit ideal.

Then

\[
\operatorname{Der}(A)\cap K\delta=A\delta,
\qquad
\operatorname{LND}(A)\cap K\delta=(\ker\delta)\delta.
\tag{11}
\]

The second equality includes the zero derivation. For nonzero
\(h\in\ker\delta\), one moreover has

\[
\ker(h\delta)=\ker\delta,
\qquad
\operatorname{pl}(h\delta)=h\operatorname{pl}(\delta),
\tag{12}
\]

and h delta has a slice if and only if h is a unit and delta has a
slice.

**Proof.** Write a rational multiplier as f/g in lowest terms in
the UFD. If \((f/g)\delta\) takes every algebra generator into A,
then g divides all their delta-values. Primitivity forces g to be
a unit. This proves the first equality.

Now let \(L=h\delta\ne0\) be locally nilpotent. Since
\(L(h)=h\delta(h)\), the characteristic-zero LND factor lemma gives
L(h)=0 and therefore delta(h)=0. For completeness, the factor lemma
follows from the additive LND degree: if L(h) is nonzero and equals
hq, then its degree is both \(\deg_L h-1\) and
\(\deg_L h+\deg_L q\), a contradiction. Conversely an invariant
multiple is locally nilpotent because
\((h\delta)^j(a)=h^j\delta^j(a)\).

Equality of kernels follows from the domain property. Also
\((h\delta)^2(a)=h^2\delta^2(a)\), so the two derivations have
the same module of local slices. Taking their images proves (12).
Finally \(h\delta(g)=1\) forces h to be a unit. Since h is
delta-invariant, it then also gives \(\delta(hg)=1\). The converse
is obtained by dividing a delta-slice by the invariant unit h.

If several nonzero commuting LNDs have one-dimensional span over K,
one obtains such a delta by dividing any one of them by the gcd of
its generator-values. This division is valid: if L=g delta is an
LND, the same factor lemma gives L(g)=delta(g)=0, and then local
nilpotence of delta follows from \(L^j=g^j\delta^j\).
Every other member is consequently an invariant multiple of this
primitive delta.

This applies to the cancellation setup: A[s] being a polynomial
ring implies that A is a UFD. The lemma does not silently assume the
desired conclusion A is a polynomial ring.

**Consequence for entire repair sequences.** The rational line K delta
is closed under arbitrary polynomial linear combinations and Lie
brackets, as well as division by rational functions whenever the
result is regular. Any locally nilpotent derivation reached without
leaving that line is still an invariant multiple of delta. If the
primitive delta has no slice, none of these operations can produce
one. A nonunit multiplier may be removed, but a missing slice of the
primitive field itself cannot be removed by further saturation.

This also covers any added conjugates whose resulting intrinsic
derivations still lie in that same rational line. It says nothing
about conjugates that create a different intrinsic orbit direction.
No assertion about the unknown threefold having only one direction
is made.

## 5. What has and has not changed in the global problem

There is now an exact local acceptance criterion for a proposed
polynomial row elimination of two coordinate directions. Leading
invariance is insufficient; full ambient invariance under the
subtracted partial is necessary and sufficient. When it holds, the
operation has an explicit polynomial coordinate realization.

Changing the coordinate system also changes the other coordinate
partials. If one instead insists on keeping the entire original
commuting frame fixed while changing just U to U-hT, equation (1)
and its versions with the other partials force all those other
partials to kill h. More explicitly, in coordinates \((u,v,z_1,z_2)\),
leaving the partials in u, z_1, z_2 unchanged requires h to lie in
\(\mathbb C[v]\). The weaker requirement of preserving only the
pair needs just T(h)=0. If h also depends on the z variables, the
valid shear changes their partials to
\(\partial_{z_i}-F_{z_i}\partial_u\); these again commute with W.
These are different procedures, and neither requires h to be a
constant.

In the rank-one case, once the primitive direction is slice-free,
staying inside its rational span cannot repair the plinth. Therefore
the needed global result must guarantee either a suitable new
coordinate frame or a genuinely new intrinsic orbit direction, or
establish an intrinsic slice by another argument. None of the
following has been proved here:

- that a dependent leading frame always admits an admissible
  degree-lowering shear;
- that a finite sequence of admissible shears and other coordinate
  changes always terminates at a tangent LND with a slice;
- that arbitrary ambient conjugates necessarily supply such a slice;
- that a free or proper intrinsic action results from leading
  extraction.

The failure in (8) is especially relevant to properness: W has a
polynomial right inverse on s, but it does not define a Ga-action
at all. It cannot be used as evidence of a free or proper action.
The [horizontal-LND control](horizontal-lnd-pass24.md) is consistent
with this distinction: a regular connection with parameter derivative
one is also not automatically locally nilpotent.

The successful endpoint remains the actual tangent LND with an
actual LND slice specified in the authoritative intrinsic-frame
note. We have narrowed which elementary repairs are legitimate;
we have not supplied the missing universal existential argument.
