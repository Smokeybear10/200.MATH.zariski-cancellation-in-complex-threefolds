# A normal generator pencil still needs the actual-coordinate gate

The abstract universal pencil does not imply ring descent even
when both rings are polynomial three-rings and the invariant plane
has a polynomial, faithfully flat inclusion into the larger ring.
This control isolates an exact failed hypothesis: its proposed bad
coordinate has a zero fiber with a nonconstant unit.

## 1. Polynomial rings and a nonintegral slope

Let
\[
S=\mathbb C[a,t],\qquad A=S[c],\qquad
R=S[a^2c]\subset A,\qquad h=ac.
\tag{1}
\]
Writing b=a squared times c identifies R with C[a,t,b]: these
three elements are algebraically independent. Both R and A are
therefore smooth factorial normal polynomial three-rings with
only nonzero constant units, and their affine varieties are
contractible. The inclusion S to A is polynomial and faithfully
flat. Also A=R[c], and R has the form S[A_1,A_2] by taking
A_1=a squared times c and A_2=0.

Nevertheless h=b/a is not in R and is not integral over R.
For an elementary proof, use the ordinary a-adic valuation on
Frac(R)=C(a,t,b). It is nonnegative on nonzero elements of R,
whereas h has value -1. Equivalently its weights on a,c are
1,-2, respectively. In a monic equation for h, the highest power
would be the unique term of lowest valuation, which cannot cancel.
This proof does not infer integrality from membership in Frac(R).

For an independent polynomial parameter tau, put d=c+tau h.
There is an exact recovery identity
\[
c=(1-\tau a)d+\tau^2(a^2c),
\qquad A[\tau]=R[\tau,c+\tau h].
\tag{2}
\]
Thus this is a universal generator pencil with nonintegral slope,
not merely generation at selected parameter values. It is not a
polynomial automorphism fixing the embedded R and replacing c by d.

The tensor condition is equally explicit:
\[
A\otimes_R A
 =\mathbb C[a,t,X,Y]/\bigl(a^2(X-Y)\bigr),\qquad
\delta c=X-Y,\quad
\delta h=a(X-Y)\ne0,\quad (\delta h)^2=0.
\tag{3}
\]
Nonvanishing follows in the ambient polynomial domain by cancellation:
if a(X-Y) were in the ideal generated by a squared times (X-Y),
then 1 would belong to the ideal (a). The square vanishes by (3).
Consequently normality of R, smoothness and factoriality of A,
constant units, and the faithful flatness of S to A do not repair
descent from the tensor-nilpotence condition. Faithful flatness
of R to A is a different condition and does not hold here: the
proper maximal ideal (a,t,b-1) of R extends to the unit ideal.

## 2. An actual sliced cylinder, but not the required original frame

On the actual polynomial four-ring B=A[sigma], take E=partial_sigma
and define
\[
u=\sigma+ac,\qquad E(u)=1,\qquad
z=c+2a\sigma c+\sigma^2,\qquad
E(z)=2u,\quad E^2(z)=2,\quad E^3(z)=0.
\tag{4}
\]
Thus E is an actual sliced translation and B=A[u]=C to the fourth.
Substitution tau=2 sigma into (2) gives
\[
c=(1-2a\sigma)(z-\sigma^2)+4\sigma^2(a^2c),
\qquad B=R[\sigma,z].
\tag{5}
\]
All of these are actual polynomial identities and subalgebra
equalities. They still do not make z one of a polynomial
coordinate system.

Indeed put v=1+2a sigma. Direct multiplication gives
\[
(1+2a\sigma)(1-2a\sigma-4a^2c)-1=-4a^2z.
\tag{6}
\]
Thus v is a unit in B/(z). It is not a constant unit there:
the two points (a,t,sigma,c)=(0,0,0,0) and
(1,0,1,-1/3) lie on the zero fiber and give v-values 1 and 3.
If z were an ambient polynomial coordinate, B/(z) would be a
polynomial three-ring over C, whose units are precisely C stars.
The exhibited nonconstant unit rules this out.

This example therefore meets the polynomial-ring, invariant-plane,
slice, constant-acceleration, and algebra-generation conditions
displayed above, but fails the original-coordinate hypothesis for
z. No original three-good-coordinate frame is asserted. In
particular it is not a counterexample to the actual descent problem
or a new unrecognized action; E itself is the canonical translation.
The stronger actual-coordinate generation gate must be used in
any argument going beyond the abstract pencil conditions.

## 3. Finite control and provenance

The standalone
[one-method checker](normal_generator_pencil_checks_pass88.py)
checks the recovery identities, tensor difference and its square,
actual E iterates, and the fiber-unit identity and its two values.
These finite controls do not prove a general integrality or
recognition theorem. The arbitrary-degree valuation contradiction
and the coordinate exclusion are written arguments above.

Root supplied the affine-modification control and its strengthened
S-relative cylinder and fiber-unit form. Jet independently
reconstructed every identity and the failed-coordinate gate.
These are separate new pass88 files, leaving the cusp and Laurent
controls and all older artifacts unchanged. The first one-method
run stopped at a structural-expression comparison: SymPy returned
the expanded form X*a-Y*a, while the expected expression was the
equal factored form a*(X-Y). The assertion was corrected to expand
their difference, without changing any mathematical identity.
The final actual run passed one method in 0.027 seconds, exit zero,
with full output in tool chunk cef94f. The complete proof and
checker received an author EOF read; the single assertion delta
was then reread. Final hashes are reported separately. No full
regression or integration runner is added by these files.
