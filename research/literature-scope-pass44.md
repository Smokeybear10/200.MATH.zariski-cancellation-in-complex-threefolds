# Source scope and prior-credit audit for the exceptional-divisor reduction

## Primary passages checked

This is a targeted source and statement audit, not an exhaustive novelty
search. The research question remains corrected complex threefold
cancellation, with the professor's intended field and dimension unconfirmed.
No new general resolution or historical priority is inferred from search.

The main investigation directly reread the following primary passages:

1. Kaliman--Saveliev,
   [*C+-actions on contractible threefolds*](https://www.math.miami.edu/~kaliman/library/KaSa.michigan.2004.quotient.pdf),
   author-hosted final text, Lemma 2.1, Theorem 2.7 with proof,
   Corollary 2.8 with proof. The prior intrinsic-quotient note was also
   read in full across this pass. The cylinder verifies the domination
   premise by an actual affine linear three-plane restriction. The
   common invariant plane is an established input, not a new result.
2. [Stacks Project, Tag 00R4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00R4),
   exact miracle-flatness statement and proof. The local dimension
   equality follows from the source's nonempty curve fibers, smooth
   total space and smooth base. No reduced-fiber assertion is substituted.
3. Michiel de Bondt,
   [*Rational maps H for which K(tH) has transcendence degree 2 over K*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.06046v4),
   retained exact PDF/text pair, Theorem 3.1, Corollary 3.2 with proof,
   and Lemma 3.3 with proof. The relevant conclusion is an actual
   polynomial parameter and B intersect C(u)=C[u], after transcendence
   degree one has been established. No ambient-coordinate conclusion.
4. Daigle--Kaliman,
   [*A Note on Locally Nilpotent Derivations and Variables of k[X,Y,Z]*](https://www.math.miami.edu/~kaliman/library/canada.daigle-kaliman.pdf),
   *Canadian Mathematical Bulletin* 52(4) (2009), 535--543,
   Theorem 1.5, its remarks and the complete gcd proof on printed p. 539.
   The invariant-subtraction mechanism is already published. The new
   support argument applies it to every cokernel class and uses flat
   saturation instead of assuming the total ring is a UFD.

## Two concrete prior-credit corrections

The pass-43 roadmap called the common base unknown or not shown
polynomial. That was overly conservative: the earlier
[intrinsic quotient proof](intrinsic-quotient-surface-pass.md) already
settles its abstract isomorphism type. Its localization is an open
plane. The remaining inclusion and exceptional fibers are not settled.
Historical pass-43 proof files remain frozen; the new common-base note,
current report and metadata explicitly supersede that roadmap wording.

The cokernel lemma's subtraction is not a new proof method. The earlier
[pass-27 plinth note](intrinsic-plinth-boundary-pass27.md) explicitly
credits Daigle--Kaliman's same gcd mechanism for the class [1]. The
all-class annihilator and compatibility applications are written in
full here, without claiming their historical novelty. This correction
was made before final hashing.

## Withdrawn source excluded

[*Affine threefolds admitting Ga-actions*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01849v2),

opening the actual comments resolved that wording issue.
No mathematical theorem from that preprint is used.

## Targeted search and its limits

Queries included the combinations "locally nilpotent", "cokernel",
"faithfully flat" and "principal annihilator", together with a
current-year cancellation/threefold screen. These returned mostly
irrelevant algebra, secondary summaries and generic arXiv results.
The broad current-year query did not establish the present literature
status by itself. No secondary AI collaboration page was used as a
mathematical source, and no search snippet was treated as a proof.

The relevant positive comparison came from following the actual local
proof dependencies to Daigle--Kaliman, not from absence of a search hit.
This pass does not certify coverage of MathSciNet, zbMATH, all theses,
Google Scholar citation chains or every recent preprint. It supports
exact source applicability and definite prior-method credit, not an
exhaustive noncollision or a novelty claim.

## Boundaries retained

Generic plane generation is not global coefficient descent. Polynomial
curve factorization is not ambient coordinate recognition. Faithful
flatness is not geometric connectedness of every fiber. A divisorwise
compatibility criterion is not proof that all divisor obstructions vanish.
No external contact, public posting, submission, payment or credentials
were used. Human specialist review and proof-assistant verification have
not occurred.
