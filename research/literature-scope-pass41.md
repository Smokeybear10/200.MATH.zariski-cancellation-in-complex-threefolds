# Source and scope audit for the one-cubic recognition argument

This is a statement-level dependency and applicability check, not an
exhaustive novelty audit. The complete working cancellation problem is
unchanged. No new result is advertised as novel or externally reviewed.

## Recognition inputs directly checked

The main investigation reread the complete relevant statements in the
retained primary texts. The independent proof reconstructions checked
their use at the exact point of application.

| Input | Exact location | Application and gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wang's linear invariant for a nice three-variable LND | Dasgupta and Gupta, [1701.03635v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.03635v1), Theorem 3.1(i), PDF p. 5 | Apply to the nonzero polynomial nice field g^4 Z over the actual field C(w). No irreducibility assumption is needed for part (i), and no invariance under E of this coefficient field is presumed. |
| Hamann relative cancellation | Same primary text, Theorem 2.6, PDF p. 4 | The established isomorphism S[J]=C[p]^[2] is over C[p]. The conclusion S=C[p]^[1] is only the relative two-variable cancellation theorem. |
| Commuting independent derivations | Maubach, [author manuscript](https://www.math.ru.nl/~maubach/Papers/The_Commuting_Dervations.pdf), Proposition 3.5 and Theorem 3.6, PDF pp. 4-5 | L and U are genuine commuting independent LNDs of the actual polynomial three-ring R. Their joint kernel has an R-coordinate generator. The complete statements and proofs were reread. |
| Planar triangularization | Dasgupta and Gaifullin, [2306.00510v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.00510v2), Theorem 2.7, PDF p. 6 | The primitive derivation restricts to the genuine coordinate plane S, and the zero restriction is handled separately. |
| Slice and three-variable kernel theorems | Same primary text, Theorems 2.5 and 2.8, PDF pp. 5-6 | The original slice makes the original kernel regular; earlier constant-factor branches use the three-variable kernel theorem with their actual coordinate sections. |
| Regular four-variable kernel | Bhatwadekar, Gupta and Lokhande, [1501.01438v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01438v1), Theorem 3.5 and proof, PDF p. 11 | A coordinate of the original ambient four-ring is fixed before the theorem is invoked. Regularity follows from the original slice, not from an auxiliary kernel. |

The new proof specifically uses Theorem 3.1(i) over a field, rather than
mistaking the PID classification for a theorem over a moving invariant
base. The rational differential relation then differentiates that base
correctly. These are established inputs, not new classifications.

## New-source applicability check

The primary [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12567) identifies
Mohamed Ali Belabbas's *Rigidity for Lie algebras of locally finite
derivations*, version 1, as submitted August 12, 2026. It is a preprint,
not an expert endorsement of this investigation.

The main investigation directly read Theorem A, Corollary A-prime,
Theorem B, Theorem D, and Theorem 5.2's complete statement and proof in
the [primary HTML text](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12567v1). The separate
geometry audit also read the proofs of A/A-prime, Lemma 3.1, Proposition
3.3 and Theorem D. The full-Lie-algebra local-finiteness requirement is
stronger than local nilpotence of the supplied generators. A previously
verified actual cubic frame violates it, as reconstructed in the
[separate scope note](lie-rigidity-scope-pass41.md).

Theorem 5.2's second-order-vanishing alternative is a possible future
criterion, but its hypothesis has not been established for an unknown
action. No part of the new one-cubic recognition proof depends on this
preprint.

## Search limits and unresolved novelty

The bounded web search covered the combinations “locally nilpotent,”
“adjoint,” and “cancellation,” the exact nice-derivation title and Wang
attribution, and arXiv searches for current threefold cancellation and
adjoint-derivation work. The exact-title search recovered the primary
[published nice-derivation article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2018.02.025),
December 2018, volume 222, pages 4161-4172. The arXiv author record
retains the January 13, 2017 preprint. Publication and preprint dates
are not confused with search crawl dates.

Broad query results included irrelevant and secondary pages. They were
not used as mathematical evidence. The new relevant Lie-rigidity lead
was followed to its primary text, where its actual gate was compared
with a concrete supplied frame. No absence-of-prior-work conclusion
follows from these searches. A complete bibliographic novelty audit,
author confirmation of the intended conjecture, and human mathematical
review remain outstanding.
