# Constant contact parameter with nonzero directional omega derivative

Working necessary reduction: retain every actual264 hypothesis,
variable coupling, a birational projected line and nonconstant sigma.
Assume that lambda and omega are noncomplex, beta is nonzero, and
the contact parameter tau of482 is complex. In the subcase
\(\mathcal D\omega\ne0\), a licensed actual normalization and the
exact coefficient basis \(z=s/t\) give
\[
\boxed{\begin{aligned}
\mu&=z+t\omega,\\
\mathcal H&=h_1\omega+h_0,\\
\lambda&=\frac{h_1}{4}\omega^2+\frac{h_0}{2}\omega+\lambda_0,\\
k&=-2\lambda_0t^2-h_0tz-\frac{h_1}{2}z^2+C t+D_0z+E.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{1}
\]
All six displayed parameters \(h_0,h_1,\lambda_0,C,D_0,E\)
are complex constants. Necessarily
\((h_0,h_1)\ne(0,0)\) and \((h_1,D_0)\ne(0,0)\).
The coefficient basis is not an ambient coordinate change. This
does not exclude or realize the family, solve its full scalar, or
cover the remaining \(\mathcal D\omega=0\) subcase.

## 2. A valid coefficient basis without a transverse omega assumption

Set \(z=s/t\). Since \(\mathbb C(t,z)=\mathbb C(t,s)\),
this is an exact separating basis in the same finite field L.
Write \(\partial_t|_z\) and \(\partial_z|_t\) for its commuting
extensions. Their relation to the original partials is
\[
\partial_t|_s=\partial_t|_z-\frac zt\partial_z|_t,
\qquad \partial_s=\frac1t\partial_z|_t,
\qquad \mathcal D=\partial_t|_z+\omega\partial_z|_t.
\tag{9}
\]
All subscripts on k and omega below use \((t,z)\), unless
explicitly marked otherwise. This does not assume that
\((t,\omega)\) or \((z,\omega)\) is separating.

The finite exact field \(F_\omega\) supplied by482 contains
lambda and \(\mathcal H\). Its omega derivative is denoted
by a prime, so
\[
d\lambda=\lambda' d\omega,\qquad
d\mathcal H=\mathcal H' d\omega,\qquad
2\lambda'=\mathcal H.
\tag{10}
\]
This field need not equal \(\mathbb C(\omega)\); its prime
constants are precisely \(\mathbb C\). Chain rules in(10)
hold for both exact partials and for \(\mathcal D\).

## 3. The high system determines the Hessian of k

First, (8),(9) give
\[
m=k_z/t,\quad
\mathcal H=-t\beta-m=-\mathcal D(k_z),\quad
\kappa:=\mathcal D\mu=2\omega+t\mathcal D\omega.
\tag{11}
\]
The frozen second derivative of m is
\(\mathcal M_2=\mathcal D\beta-m_s\kappa\).
Consequently the definition of \(B_2\) and the relation
\(B_2=q\beta=-\beta/t\) imply
\[
2B_2=\mathcal D\beta-t m_s\mathcal D\omega=-2\beta/t.
\tag{12}
\]
On the other hand \(\beta=-(m+\mathcal H)/t\) gives
\(\mathcal D\beta=-2\beta/t-\mathcal H'\mathcal D\omega/t\).
Since the original \(m_s=k_{zz}/t^2\), equation(12) becomes
\[
(k_{zz}+\mathcal H')\mathcal D\omega=0.
\tag{13}
\]
Now impose the additional branch condition
\[
\mathcal D\omega\ne0.
\tag{14}
\]
Equations(11),(13) give
\[
k_{zz}=-\mathcal H',\qquad
k_{tz}=\omega\mathcal H'-\mathcal H.
\tag{15}
\]
The defining high equation
\(\mathcal K_2=-4\lambda-2m\omega\), expressed in the
new basis, is
\[
\mathcal K_2=k_{tt}+2\omega k_{tz}+\omega^2k_{zz}
                         -2\omega k_z/t.
\tag{16}
\]
Its last term cancels \(-2m\omega\), so (15) yields
\[
k_{tt}=-4\lambda+2\omega\mathcal H-\omega^2\mathcal H'.
\tag{17}
\]
No division by \(\mathcal H'\), \(\omega_t\), or
\(\omega_z\) has occurred.

## 4. Commuting third derivatives force constant Hessian entries

Differentiate the first equation of(15) with respect to t
and the second with respect to z. Exact commutation gives
\[
-\mathcal H''\omega_t
   =\omega\mathcal H''\omega_z,
\qquad \mathcal H''\mathcal D\omega=0.
\tag{18}
\]
By(14), \(\mathcal H''=0\). The exact prime constants of
\(F_\omega\), followed by (10), give complex constants
\(h_1,h_0,\lambda_0\) such that
\[
\mathcal H=h_1\omega+h_0,\qquad
\lambda=h_1\omega^2/4+h_0\omega/2+\lambda_0.
\tag{19}
\]
One obtains these constants by subtracting explicit polynomials
from existing field elements; no new primitive is adjoined.
The Hessian entries (15),(17) are now
\[
k_{zz}=-h_1,\qquad k_{tz}=-h_0,
\qquad k_{tt}=-4\lambda_0.
\tag{20}
\]
Subtract \(-2\lambda_0t^2-h_0tz-h_1z^2/2\) from k.
Both partials of each of the remainder's first derivatives vanish,
so those derivatives are complex constants C and \(D_0\).
Subtracting \(Ct+D_0z\) leaves another complex constant E.
This proves the full family(1) in the exact field without an
assumption that L is rational.

Returning only at the coefficient level to normalized t,s,
\[
k=-2\lambda_0t^2-h_0s-\frac{h_1s^2}{2t^2}
                            +Ct+\frac{D_0s}{t}+E,
\quad m=-h_0+\frac{D_0-h_1z}{t}.
\tag{21}
\]
Algebraic independence of t,z shows that variable m is equivalent
to \((h_1,D_0)\ne(0,0)\). Noncomplex lambda is equivalent
to \((h_0,h_1)\ne(0,0)\), since omega is noncomplex and the
displayed polynomial has complex coefficients.

## 5. Forward verification and boundaries

For the family(1) and \(\mu=z+t\omega\), direct differentiation
with the ORIGINAL partials in(9) gives
\[
\beta=\frac{h_1z-D_0-h_1t\omega}{t^2},\qquad
B_2=-\frac\beta t,\qquad C_3=\frac\beta{t^2}.
\tag{22}
\]
The definition of omega, both high level coefficients, the minor
\(B_2^2=\beta C_3\) and all four one-form components of474
vanish identically for arbitrary omega in the exact field.
The forward identities themselves do not need(14).

Within(14) and variable m, beta cannot vanish. If \(h_1=0\),
variable m gives \(D_0\ne0\), so \(\beta=-D_0/t^2\ne0\).
If \(h_1\ne0\), beta zero would give
\(\omega=(z-D_0/h_1)/t\), whose directional derivative
\(\omega_t+\omega\omega_z\) is zero. This contradicts(14).
No nonzero value of \(h_1\) is assumed in the reduction itself.

The coefficient change from \((t,z,P)\) to \((t,s,P)\)
has Jacobian t, so the complete scalar still must satisfy
\[
J_{t,z,P}(X)=tJ_{t,s,P}(X)=-t a_0^4/\rho\in L^*.
\tag{23}
\]
This is a retained requirement, not a verified property of the
formal high family. No n, ell, moving levels, lower intercepts,
or actual polynomial realization are constructed here. In
particular the unchanged full scalar and both original returns
are still required. Constant tau with \(\mathcal D\omega=0\),
nonconstant tau, other projected curves and arbitrary chart entry
are outside the conclusion. No global cancellation result follows.
