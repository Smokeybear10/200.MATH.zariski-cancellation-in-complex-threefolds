# Modified Derksen pass for the Koras--Russell cylinder

**Date:** 2026-09-12  

September 18 status update: [673](quadratic-cylinder-flexibility-and-modified-derksen-pass673-working.md)
now proves the FULL invariant HD*(B)=B using Poloni's cylinder transitivity
and a conductor argument. The selected-subgroup calculations below remain
valid, but their subgroup omits the new action. The historical open-status
language and proposed all-SAut invariant-line route below are superseded.
This does not prove B=C^[4]. See also the exact separate conjecture
consequence in [674](russell-cylinder-bgs-conjecture-counterexample-pass674-working.md).

**Status:** bounded source audit plus exact family-specific calculations. This is **not** a solution of cancellation, a computation of the full modified Derksen invariant, or a novelty claim. The calculations labelled “proved in this note” still require literature-priority review.

## Outcome

Let

\[
A=\mathbb C[x,y,z,t]/(x^2y+z^2+t^3+x),\qquad B=A[w].
\]

For the natural commuting locally nilpotent derivations

\[
E_1=2z\partial_y-x^2\partial_z,
\qquad
E_2=3t^2\partial_y-x^2\partial_t,
\]

the algebra generated by the kernels of every slice derivation

\[
D_{\lambda,a,b}=\lambda\partial_w+a(x)E_1+b(x)E_2,
\quad \lambda\in\mathbb C^*,\quad a,b\in\mathbb C[x],
\]

can be computed exactly:

\[
H_0
=\mathbb C[\ker D_{\lambda,a,b}:\lambda,a,b]
=A[xw,zw,t^2w]
=A[(x,z,t^2)w].
\]

In particular, `w` is not in `H_0`. The same obstruction survives after conjugating all these kernels by the explicit `x`-moving locally nilpotent flow used by Dubouloz--Moser-Jauslin--Poloni, and by arbitrary finite compositions of the standard base flows: every resulting kernel reduces to `C[y]` on the plane `x=z=t=0`, so their generated algebra still cannot contain `w`.

This is a genuine but bounded negative result. It proves that the large family of presently explicit slice actions does **not** settle `w in HD*(B)`. It does not classify all slice locally nilpotent derivations of `B`. That missing universal quantifier is exactly where the published open problem remains.

## 1. Current source boundary

Throughout the cited modified-invariant results, the ground field is algebraically closed of characteristic zero and the affine varieties are irreducible.

Boldyrev, Gaifullin, and Shafarevich define

\[
HD^*(X)=\mathbb C[\ker D:D\text{ is a locally nilpotent derivation with a slice}].
\]

Their introduction explicitly says that it remains open whether `HD*` distinguishes the product of the Koras--Russell cubic with a line from affine four-space. Their Theorem 1 says that, for `X=Y x A^1` with `Y` nonrigid, if `HD*(X)` is proper then it is not finitely generated. Their Proposition 3 supplies the relevant geometric sufficient condition: if some cylinder line `{p} x A^1` is invariant under `SAut(X)`, then `HD*(X)` is proper. See [I. Boldyrev, S. Gaifullin, A. Shafarevich, *Modified Derksen invariant*, arXiv:2312.08421v2 (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08421), especially Definition 2, Theorem 1, and Proposition 3.

The earlier paper proves the corresponding modified Makar--Limanov invariant does not help here: whenever a cylinder admits a slice, `ML*=ML`, and it explicitly applies this to the Koras--Russell cylinder. See [S. Gaifullin, A. Shafarevich, *Modified Makar-Limanov and Derksen invariants*, arXiv:2212.05899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05899), published in *Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra* 228 (2024), article 107616.

A July 2026 primary paper still describes the characteristic-zero Zariski cancellation problem as open and revisits locally nilpotent derivations on the Koras--Russell cylinder, but does not claim to compute its modified Derksen invariant: [S. Gaifullin, M. Petrov, *Non-cancellative varieties, maximal tori, and the Makar-Limanov invariant*, arXiv:2607.13593v1 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13593). A targeted title/formula search and the public Semantic Scholar citation endpoint for arXiv:2312.08421 found no later paper resolving the specific `HD*` question. That is limited screening, not a comprehensive priority or current-status guarantee.

## 2. Exact standard-slice calculation (proved in this note)

Put `J=(x,z,t^2) in A`. The derivations `E_1,E_2` commute, kill `x`, and are locally nilpotent. Therefore

\[
E_{a,b}=a(x)E_1+b(x)E_2
\]

is locally nilpotent. The derivation `D_{lambda,a,b}` has slice `w/lambda`. Define, for `q in A`,

\[
\sigma_{\lambda,a,b}(q)
=\exp\!\left(-\frac{w}{\lambda}E_{a,b}\right)(q).
\]

Conjugating `lambda partial_w` by this exponential, or applying the slice theorem directly, gives

\[
\ker D_{\lambda,a,b}=\sigma_{\lambda,a,b}(A).
\]

Writing `u=w/lambda`, the images of the generators are

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sigma(x)&=x,\\
\sigma(z)&=z+a x^2u,\\
\sigma(t)&=t+b x^2u,\\
\sigma(y)&=y-(2az+3bt^2)u
 -(a^2x^2+3b^2x^2t)u^2-b^3x^4u^3.
\end{aligned}
\]

The cubic truncation follows from

\[
\begin{aligned}
E_{a,b}(y)&=2az+3bt^2,\\
E_{a,b}^2(y)&=-2a^2x^2-6b^2x^2t,\\
E_{a,b}^3(y)&=6b^3x^4,\\
E_{a,b}^4(y)&=0.
\end{aligned}
\]

Every displayed image belongs to `A[Jw]=A[xw,zw,t^2w]`. For example,

\[
x^2w^2=(xw)^2,\qquad
x^2tw^2=t(xw)^2,\qquad
x^4w^3=x(xw)^3.
\]

Hence `H_0 subset A[Jw]`.

For the reverse containment it suffices to use four constant-coefficient cases with `lambda=1`.

* Taking `a=1,b=0` in `sigma(z)-z` gives `x^2w`.
* Subtracting the `a=1` and `a=-1` images of `y` gives `4zw`; adding them gives `-2x^2w^2` after subtracting `2y`.
* For `a=0,b=1` and `a=0,b=-1`, the difference of the two images of `y` is `6t^2w+2x^4w^3`. The term `x^4w^3=(x^2w)(x^2w^2)` is already available, hence so is `t^2w`.
* Multiplying the defining equation of `A` by `w` gives

  \[
  xw=-y(x^2w)-z(zw)-t(t^2w),
  \]

  so `xw` is available as well.

Thus `A[Jw] subset H_0`, proving equality.

To prove properness without cancelling a nonunit, let `m=(x,z,t) subset A`. Then

\[
B/mB\simeq\mathbb C[y,w],
\]

while the image of `H_0=A[Jw]` is exactly `C[y]`. Therefore `w notin H_0`. This quotient argument is the essential safe replacement for any invalid inference of the form “`x^2w` is known, so divide by `x^2`.”

## 3. The known `x`-moving flow does not escape the obstruction

Proposition 7.2 of [A. Dubouloz, L. Moser-Jauslin, P.-M. Poloni, *Inequivalent embeddings of the Koras--Russell cubic threefold*, arXiv:0903.4278](https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.4278) constructs an LND `L` of `B` by transporting

\[
\Delta=t^6(-2z\partial_x+(y+1)\partial_z)
\]

through their explicit localized cylinder isomorphism. In particular,

\[
L(x)=-2t^6(z+xw).
\]

This is the action that moves `x` and proves ordinary `ML(B)=C`. It also produces the previously noted membership `t^6xw in HD*(B)`: each `exp(cL)(A)` is the kernel of the slice derivation `exp(cL) partial_w exp(-cL)`, and interpolation in `c` extracts the coefficients of `exp(cL)(x)`.

However, this flow still does not remove the bad-plane factor. Direct substitution in the explicit maps of their Propositions 7.1--7.2 gives, in `B`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
L(x)&=-2s^2(z+xw),\\
L(t)&=0,\\
L(y)&=sF_y,\\
L(z)&=sF_z,\\
L(w)&=-\tfrac12sF_w,
\end{aligned}
\qquad
s=x^2y+x+z^2=-t^3,
\]

for polynomials `F_y,F_z,F_w in B`. Thus

\[
L(B)\subseteq mB,\qquad L(mB)\subseteq mB,
\]

and every `alpha_c=exp(cL)` induces the identity on `B/mB=C[y,w]`.

The same is true for the base flows `exp(f(x)E_1+g(x)E_2)`: their generator images lie in `mB`, and `mB` is invariant. Let `Gamma` be the subgroup generated by these base flows and all `exp(cL)`, and set

\[
H_{\mathrm{known}}
=\mathbb C[\gamma(\ker D_{\lambda,a,b}):
\gamma\in\Gamma,\ \lambda,a,b].
\]

Every algebra in the brackets is again the kernel of a slice LND, because conjugation preserves local nilpotence and slices. But its reduction modulo `mB` is `C[y]`: each original kernel reduces to `C[y]`, and every element of `Gamma` acts trivially on the quotient. Consequently

\[
H_{\mathrm{known}}\bmod mB=\mathbb C[y],
\qquad w\notin H_{\mathrm{known}}.
\]

This includes all finite iterated conjugations by the presently used `x`-moving flow; it is stronger than merely observing `A[x^2w,t^6xw] subset HD*(B)`. It remains only a theorem about an explicit generated subgroup and explicit slice family, not about all of `SAut(B)` or all of `LND*(B)`.

## 4. Exact remaining quantifier

For `n>=0`, the coefficient ideals used in the proof of Boldyrev--Gaifullin--Shafarevich's Theorem 1 are

\[
J_n^*=\{f\in A:f w^n\in HD^*(B)\}.
\]

The calculation above supplies

\[
(x,z,t^2)\subseteq J_1^*,
\]

and the DMP flow supplies `t^6x in J_1^*`. The decisive question is

\[
w\in HD^*(B)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad J_1^*=A.
\]

The quotient `B/mB=C[y,w]` suggests an exact dichotomy for the next pass.

1. **Properness route.** Prove that every slice LND preserves `mB`, or at least that the restriction of every slice kernel to the cylinder line

   \[
   \ell_c=\{x=z=t=0,\ y=c\}\times\mathbb A^1_w
   \]

   consists only of constants. Invariance of one such line under all of `SAut(B)` would invoke Proposition 3 of the 2024 paper and prove `HD*(B)` proper. Establishing this is a major missing classification statement, not a routine consequence of the actions checked here.

2. **Fullness route.** Find a slice LND whose kernel has a reduction modulo `mB` that genuinely depends on `w`; equivalently, escape the `C[y]` reduction shared by the known family. Such a kernel would defeat the bounded obstruction, although a single `w`-dependent residue would not by itself imply `w in HD*(B)`.

The common plane has a clear geometric interpretation. All explicit actions checked here preserve every cylinder line `ell_c`; the slice actions translate along it, while their kernels restrict to constants. What is not known is whether an arbitrary LND of `B` must preserve even one of these lines. Assuming that would simply assume the hard part of the modified-Derksen problem.
