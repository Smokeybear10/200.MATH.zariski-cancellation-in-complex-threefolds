# Prior-work scope of the rank-two Su-cell calculation

## 1. Definite prior statement

The broad rank-two cancellation conclusion is already explicitly stated by Anton Mellit. In [*Toric stratifications of character varieties*, Publications mathématiques de l'IHÉS 142 (2025), 153--240](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10240-025-00158-0.pdf), Section 1.4, journal page 160, the paragraph following the four-puncture example says that in every rank-two case the extra affine line can be removed compatibly with the stratification. The published text specifies a product decomposition \(\widetilde X=X\times\mathbb C\) compatible with those strata. This is an explicit unnumbered statement, not a theorem number invented for this audit.

The same rank-two statement already occurs in [arXiv:1905.10685v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.10685v1), Section 1.4, manuscript pages 7--8. Both neighboring discussions were directly read. The setting is generic semisimple \(GL_n(\mathbb C)\) monodromy with at least one regular semisimple puncture. The general vector-bundle stratification is Theorem 1.2 in the publication, Theorem 1.4.1 in the preprint. The braid walk's torus-times-affine coordinates are already Proposition 5.4.2 of that preprint.

**Priority consequence:** rank-two removal of the extra affine line must receive inherited credit. Our explicit reconstruction in Su's conventions is verification of a specified construction, not evidence for announcing a new rank-two cancellation theorem.

## 2. The precise comparison with Su

We directly read the technical coordinate/action gates in [Su's arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.16657v4), including Definition 1.14, Proposition 1.16, Lemma 2.5 and its proof, Appendix A, and Theorem 2.10's quotient construction. They identify the actual first braid parameter with an affine coordinate shifted by the additive action. Our [selected-cell calculation](su-selected-cell-pass37.md) and the [all-rank-two extension](su-geometry-audit-pass37.md) spell out the resulting coordinate-slice argument.

The [author-hosted journal PDF](https://bimsa.net/doc/publication/5764.pdf), *Advances in Mathematics* 498 (2026), 111043, was successfully downloaded by the root agent and then inspected locally. Its introductory note following Theorem 0.5 and its complete Remark 2.12, journal page 50, still describe the 80 rank-two threefolds as potential cancellation counterexamples. Its acknowledgments, page 63, give no different rank-two assertion. The [author's publication page](https://tao-su-math.github.io/research/) links the arXiv version.

The exact selected coordinates, action, and quotient generators were reconstructed independently rather than attributed to an unseen passage. Mellit's explicit prior rank-two assertion is sufficient to prevent a broad novelty claim; it does not substitute for checking Su's equivariant projection, extra factors, or specific indexed cell.

## 3. Classical slice mechanism and a version distinction

A publicly available [Casals--Gorsky--Gorsky--Simental author manuscript](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~casals/CGGS2arXiv.pdf), *Positroid Links and Braid Varieties*, Proposition 2.2, manuscript page 12, states the classical slice theorem: a locally nilpotent derivation with a central slice splits the algebra as its kernel adjoined that slice. Its complete statement and proof were read. This is inherited machinery, not a new lemma of pass 37. When the slice is additionally an actual polynomial coordinate, its zero hyperplane identifies the kernel as a polynomial algebra.

That author PDF has 57 pages and must not be conflated with the [42-page arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2105.13948v4), whose organization is different. We do not attribute the former's Proposition 2.2 numbering to the latter. Neither inspected passage identifies Su's 80 named indices.
