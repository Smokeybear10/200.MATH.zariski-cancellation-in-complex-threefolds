# Actual low-degree generation away from simultaneous speed zeros

The supplied independent degree-two frame gives a genuine global
generation result on an open subset of the invariant plane:
the intrinsic ring is generated by its degree-at-most-two module
wherever one bad speed or good acceleration is invertible.
Consequently every second-image basepoint lies in the simultaneous
zero set of all four of these invariants.

The chosen low-degree slice is essential. Projection along an
arbitrary supplied slice can have unbounded intrinsic degree even
in the same actual frame. That failure concerns the projected
generating list, not the minimum degree needed to generate the ring.

## 1. Exact supplied frame and degree conventions

Retain the actual hypotheses of
[the generic bad-pair theorem](generic-bad-pair-pass44.md):
\[
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]=A[s_*],\quad E(s_*)=1,\quad
A=\ker E,\quad C\simeq\mathbb C^{[2]},\quad L=\operatorname{Frac}C.
\tag{1}
\]
The good coordinate partials have second adjoint zero, the two bad
partials have third adjoint zero, and their highest fields are
independent. In the remaining degree-two case,
\[
f=Ex_3,\quad g=Ex_4,\quad h_i=E^2x_i\ (i=1,2)
\]
belong to \(C\), with \(f,g\) algebraically independent.
The established actual-generator result is
\[
B_L=L[x_3,x_4],\qquad
x_i=\mathcal Q_i(x_3,x_4),\quad
\mathcal Q_i\in L[X_3,X_4],\quad\deg\mathcal Q_i\le2
\quad(i=1,2).
\tag{2}
\]
In particular \(E=f\partial_{x_3}+g\partial_{x_4}\) on this
generic ring. Equation (2) is the full polynomial generic ring,
not only a dominant parametrization.

Choose the actual primitive intrinsic LND \(D\) with kernel
\(C\), and put
\[
F_n=\ker(D^{n+1}:A\to A),\qquad
\mathfrak a=(f,g,h_1,h_2)C,\qquad H=C[F_2]\subset A.
\tag{3}
\]
The full plinth and image notation are
\(\operatorname{pl}(D)=pC\), \(Dr=p\), and \(I_2=pJ\).
The inherited inclusion \(C\subset A\) is faithfully flat;
its closed fibers are nonempty and one-dimensional.
The exact \(F_2\) module and conic description are those of
[pass 47](second-image-vector-bundle-pass47.md).

Write
\[
j=gx_3-fx_4\in A,\qquad A_L=L[j].
\tag{4}
\]
Since \(D\) kills \(L\), it is a nonzero LND of \(L[j]\).
Thus \(Dj\in L^*\), and the intrinsic \(D\)-degree of an
element of \(A\subset L[j]\) is exactly its \(j\)-degree.
This does not make \(j\) a minimal local slice or an ambient
coordinate. The statement about degrees is unchanged after
localization at actual invariants.

## 2. An actual affine generic slice gives the entire low-degree algebra

Let \(R=C_S\) be any invariant localization, and assume first
that one of \(f,g,h_1,h_2\) is a unit in \(R\).
There is an actual element \(\sigma\in B_R\) with \(E\sigma=1\):
use respectively
\[
\sigma=x_3/f,\quad x_4/g,\quad Ex_1/h_1,\quad Ex_2/h_2.
\tag{5}
\]
In the acceleration cases, (2) gives
\[
Ex_i=f\partial_{X_3}\mathcal Q_i+
g\partial_{X_4}\mathcal Q_i.
\]
It is affine-linear in the generic bad coordinates. Therefore
every choice in (5) has the form
\[
\sigma=\alpha x_3+\beta x_4+\gamma,\qquad
\alpha,\beta,\gamma\in L,\qquad \alpha f+\beta g=1.
\tag{6}
\]
The coefficients in (6) need not belong to \(R\).
Membership of \(\sigma\) in \(B_R\) comes from (5), not
from a denominator-lifting inference.

Over \(L\), the determinant of the coefficient matrix for
\((\sigma-\gamma,j)\) is \(-1\). Its exact inverse is
\[
x_3=f(\sigma-\gamma)+\beta j,\qquad
x_4=g(\sigma-\gamma)-\alpha j.
\tag{7}
\]
The actual slice theorem gives \(B_R=A_R[\sigma]\).
Let \(\rho_\sigma:B_R\to A_R\) be evaluation at \(\sigma=0\).
It is a ring map fixing \(R\). Equation (7) gives
\[
\ell_3:=\rho_\sigma(x_3)=\beta j-f\gamma,\qquad
\ell_4:=\rho_\sigma(x_4)=-\alpha j-g\gamma.
\tag{8}
\]
These are actual elements of \(A_R\), of \(D\)-degree at most
one. Projecting the good coordinates gives
\[
q_i:=\rho_\sigma(x_i)=\mathcal Q_i(\ell_3,\ell_4)\in A_R,
\qquad \deg_D q_i\le2.
\tag{9}
\]
Injectivity into \(L[j]\) proves the degree bound in the
actual ring.

The four original coordinates generate \(B_R\) over \(R\).
Surjectivity of the actual projection therefore proves
\[
A_R=R[q_1,q_2,\ell_3,\ell_4]
   =R[(F_2)_R].
\tag{10}
\]
The last equality uses invariant localization of \(\ker D^3\).
This entire argument precedes any DVR elimination. In particular,
\(R\) can be a two-dimensional closed-point local ring.

More generally, if \(\mathfrak aR=R\), cover
\(\operatorname{Spec}R\) by the four principal opens in (5).
The equalities (10) on those opens imply
\[
\boxed{\quad \mathfrak aR=R\ \Longrightarrow\
A_R=R[(F_2)_R].\quad}
\tag{11}
\]
For an elementary gluing check, regard \(A_R/R[(F_2)_R]\)
only as an \(R\)-module. For each of its elements, its four
local vanishings give annihilating powers of the four entries
of \(\mathfrak a\). Those powers generate the unit ideal.
Their linear combination kills the element. No finite-generation
assumption on this quotient module is needed.

The affine generic inverse and projection mechanism were already
used over DVRs in
[the acceleration extension](acceleration-divisor-extension-pass45.md).
The added content is their validity over the actual open subset
and at its closed points, before any one-dimensional base argument.

## 3. Second-image basepoints must be simultaneous speed zeros

Suppose a closed point \(\mathfrak m\) lies in
\(\operatorname{Supp}(C/J)\) and
\(\mathfrak a\not\subset\mathfrak m\). Put
\(R=C_{\mathfrak m}\), \(\mathfrak n=\mathfrak mR\).
The existing bundle/conic description supplies
\[
(F_2)_R=R\oplus Rz\oplus Rw,\qquad r=az+bw,\qquad
JR=(a,b),
\]
and the exact subalgebra presentation
\[
R[z,w]\simeq
R[Z,W]/\bigl(\epsilon(aZ+bW)^2-2B_2Z+2B_1W-Q\bigr).
\tag{12}
\]
At an actual obstruction point, the
[flat-conic/topology gate](flat-conic-topology-gate-pass47.md)
proves
\[
a,b,B_1,B_2,Q\in\mathfrak n.
\tag{13}
\]
Here the topology input concerns the original smooth contractible
cylinder factor, not an arbitrary conic model.

Equation (11) now makes the subalgebra in (12) the whole \(A_R\).
Reducing its exact presentation using (13) gives
\[
A_R/\mathfrak nA_R\simeq\mathbb C[Z,W].
\tag{14}
\]
Its dimension is two, contradicting the actual one-dimensional
quotient fiber. Thus
\[
\boxed{\quad
\operatorname{Supp}(C/J)
\subseteq V(f,g,h_1,h_2).
\quad}
\tag{15}
\]
Combining with pass 47 also puts this support in
\(V(p,\partial_U p,\partial_V p)\), for coordinates \(U,V\)
of \(C\). No inclusion of the corresponding ideals or equality
of their scheme structures follows from this support argument.

If \(\mathfrak a=C\), (11) holds globally, (15) forces
\(J=C\), and the exact global quadratic equation criterion
applies. Recognition of that equation as affine three-space
still requires its separate hypotheses. Generation by \(F_2\)
alone is not labeled a general polynomial-ring criterion.

## 4. Arbitrary slice projection has no uniform degree bound

The following uses the inherited actual unimodular-pair frame,
not a formal generic coordinate map. On
\[
B=\mathbb C[u,v,a,c],\qquad g=uv-1,
\]
put
\[
E=u\partial_a+g\partial_c,\qquad
(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
=(u+a^2/2,\ v+c^2/2,\ a,\ c).
\tag{16}
\]
These are genuine coordinates, with polynomial inverse
\(u=x_1-x_3^2/2,\ v=x_2-x_4^2/2,\ a=x_3,\ c=x_4\).
The pulled-back actual partials are
\[
T_1=\partial_u,\quad T_2=\partial_v,\quad
T_3=\partial_a-a\partial_u,\quad
T_4=\partial_c-c\partial_v.
\]
Their good tops are
\[
W_1=-(\partial_a+v\partial_c),\qquad
W_2=-u\partial_c,
\]
and their bad tops are
\[
V_3=2u(\partial_a+v\partial_c),\qquad
V_4=2ug\partial_c.
\tag{17}
\]
All required next brackets vanish. The bad projection determinant
is \(4u^2g\ne0\); the supplied profile is genuinely
\((1,1,2,2)\). Also \(E^3x=0\), and the two bad speeds
\(u,g\) are algebraically independent.

Put
\[
s_0=va-c,\qquad r=ga-uc.
\]
Then \(Es_0=1\), \(Er=0\), and
\[
a=us_0-r,\qquad c=gs_0-vr.
\tag{18}
\]
Thus \(A=\mathbb C[u,v,r]\). The restriction of the actual
good top \(W_1\) to \(A\) is the primitive intrinsic
\(D=\partial_r\), with kernel \(\mathbb C[u,v]\).

For every \(N\ge2\), choose the actual polynomial slice
\[
s_N=s_0+r^N.
\tag{19}
\]
Its inverse frame follows by replacing \(s_0\) by
\(s_N-r^N\) in (18); it is polynomial in both directions.
Projection along this slice gives
\[
\begin{split}
\rho_N(x_3)&=-r-ur^N,\\
\rho_N(x_4)&=-vr-gr^N,\\
\rho_N(x_1)&=u+\tfrac12(r+ur^N)^2,\\
\rho_N(x_2)&=v+\tfrac12(vr+gr^N)^2.
\end{split}
\tag{20}
\]
Their actual intrinsic degrees are exactly
\[
(\deg_D\rho_N(x_1),\ldots,\deg_D\rho_N(x_4))
=(2N,2N,N,N).
\tag{21}
\]
The leading coefficients \(u^2/2,g^2/2,-u,-g\) are nonzero
in the invariant domain, so these degrees cannot cancel.

This disproves a uniform \(F_3\), or any fixed \(F_M\), bound
on the projected original generators for an arbitrary supplied
slice. It does not disprove generation of the ring by \(F_3\):
this example is already generated over its kernel by \(F_1\).
The frame and its general nonlinear-gauge boundary appeared in
pass 44; (19)--(21) supply the exact actual-slice quantifier needed
for the present degree question.

## 5. The remaining generating-set obligation

The four projected original generators do generate the entire
intrinsic ring for every slice. What fails is a bound on their
\(D\)-degrees without the additional actual affine generic
slice in (5)--(6).

The open-set theorem removes all possible second-image basepoints
outside \(V(f,g,h_1,h_2)\). At a remaining point, the supplied
slice \(s_*\) still exists, but the argument has not replaced it
by an actual affine generic slice, or by a slice whose projected
generators all lie in \(F_3\). The full DVR models do not supply
that replacement at a simultaneous closed-point zero.

In particular, the new
[cubic image theorem](third-image-cubic-gate-pass48.md) gives
\(D(F_3)\subset\mathfrak mF_3\) at a reduced obstruction point.
It cannot be applied to all of the actual closed fiber until
generation by \(F_3\), or a different adequate generating set,
has been established there. No such assertion is made in this
note. No old file or metadata is changed, and no historical
novelty or proof-assistant verification is claimed.

[frame_filtration_generation_checks_pass49.py](frame_filtration_generation_checks_pass49.py)
contains one bounded actual-frame control. Its completed run passed in
