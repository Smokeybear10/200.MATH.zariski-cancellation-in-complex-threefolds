# Exact-statement source scope for supplied-good nice recognition

A bounded primary-source search did not locate a published theorem
with exactly the hypotheses of the new supplied-good recognition
argument. The nearby results require invariant ambient coordinates
or an actual polynomial presentation over a smaller base.
This is not a certification of novelty or an exhaustive search.

## 1. The statement being compared

The working theorem concerns
\[
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4],\qquad
E\in\operatorname{LND}(B),\qquad Es=1,\qquad E^2x_i=0,
\qquad 0\ne T_c=\sum c_i\partial_{x_i},\quad
[E,[T_c,E]]=0.
\tag{1}
\]
Its conclusion is \(\ker E\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\), and hence global
translation using the supplied slice. The
[written proof](nice-good-direction-recognition-pass79.md)
uses original coordinate generation and an invariant-omission
theorem after the relative quadratic reduction.

Condition \(E^2x_i=0\) is coordinate flow degree one.
It does not mean that the coefficients of E have spatial degree
one or two. The double bracket concerns one constant direction
in that same frame; it is not an invariant-coordinate hypothesis.
A global slice implies fixed-point-freeness and irreducibility,
but a localized slice cannot replace it in (1).

## 2. Dasgupta--Gupta: the precise coordinate gate

The source defines the rank of an LND by the number of coordinates
that must remain outside its kernel, minimized over coordinate
systems. It is not the rank of its polynomial Jacobian matrix.
Its Corollary 3.8 explicitly assumes an irreducible D in actual
coordinates satisfying
\[
K[X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4]=K^{[4]},\qquad
DX_1=0,\qquad D^2X_i=0\quad(i=2,3,4),
\tag{2}
\]
and concludes \(\ker D=K^{[3]}\).[^dg]

Theorem 3.6 works over a PID R containing the rationals, with a
genuine \(R[X,Y,Z]\) and an irreducible nice R-LND. Its proof
clears the coefficients of a generic invariant linear form and
completes its unimodular row over R. Thus the fixed base and the
polynomial presentation precede the proof.

These are useful recognition results, but their coordinate input
is not supplied merely by \(J^2c=0\) in (1). In the nonlinear
branch there need not be any invariant constant linear coordinate.
Obtaining invariant ambient coordinates from the new conclusion
cannot retroactively verify an unproved source premise.

## 3. Dasgupta--Lahiri: latest checked version still has a base gate

The current arXiv record for 2302.13787 is v6, dated 24 July 2026.
The PDF title is *On image ideals of nice and quasi-nice derivations
on R[X1,X2]*; the landing page retains the title ending
*over a UFD*. It is not the older v3 surfaced by some search
results.[^dl]

For an irreducible nice R-LND on the actual polynomial algebra
\(C=R[X_1,X_2]\), its Theorem 4.1 gives
\[
I_j=(\ker D)\cap D^jC
    =(DX_1,DX_2)^j\ker D\qquad(j\ge1).
\tag{3}
\]
The complete proof begins with the two-variable kernel
description over the fixed UFD R. Its PID three-variable
Corollary 4.3 uses the recalled Dasgupta--Gupta theorem and
likewise retains a genuine polynomial base presentation.

Those statements do not recognize an arbitrary threefold merely
because it has two generators over a plane. Before pass79's final
step, that object was a quotient \(S[Y,Z]/(F)\), not \(S^{[2]}\).
Nor did the original E fix a supplied coordinate subring R with
the required relative polynomial presentation. The source is
therefore not a direct substitute for the new cofactor argument.

## 4. What the search supports

The search compared combinations of nice LND, global slice,
four variables, quasi-translation, constant partial, commuting
derivation and double commutator. It was deduplicated against
the retained nice-route and pass74--76 source notes. Unrelated
web pages and unaudited research repositories were not used as
mathematical authority.

The selected de Bondt Theorem 5.1(iv) is an actual input of the
working proof, not an already stated global-translation theorem.
Its complete displayed statement and proof were reread in the
retained primary text during this pass; the exact homogenization
and descent remain in
[omission76](nice-good-direction-invariant-omission-pass76.md).

The safe result of this search is only that the exact conditional
statement (1) was not found among the checked sources. No claim
is made that it is historically new, that every nice sliced action
has a nonzero constant good direction, or that an arbitrary sliced
LND can be made nice. The original cancellation problem is not
settled by this applicability audit.

No numerical test, external contact, full literature-corpus audit,
human expert review or formal verification was performed.
Frozen files through pass78 and shared metadata are unchanged.
