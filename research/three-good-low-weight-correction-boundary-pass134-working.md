# The correction gates as low-weight invariant jumps

The primitive cubic case has an exact finite-filtration reformulation:
both correction gates pass precisely when a nonconstant invariant of
original weight three exists. The multiplicity estimate permits that
weight but has not been shown to force it. An actual cubic frame also
shows why normality of the invariant graded ring would not justify
canceling a leading factor.

This working boundary note proves the reformulation and the explicit
graded-ring control. It does not prove either correction gate, and the
control is not a gate-failing frame.

## 1. The two low-weight jumps

Assume the actual sliced dense quadratic three-good setup and its
primitive coordinate-initial relation, as in the leading dichotomy.[^1]
Thus the original polynomial coordinates are \((x_1,x_2,x_3,z)\),
\(A=\ker E\), the speeds are \(a_i=Ex_i\), and the initial-map kernel is
generated by
\[
\Phi=L(X)Z-F_3(X),\qquad
\operatorname{wt}(X_i)=1,\quad\operatorname{wt}(Z)=2,
\qquad \gcd(L,F_3)=1.
\tag{1}
\]
In particular the initial-map kernel has no nonzero element of weight
less than three. Let \(F_mB\) denote the original-weight filtration and
put
\[
V_{m,n}=F_mB\cap\ker E^{n+1},\qquad F_mA=V_{m,0},
\qquad F_1B=\mathbb C+\sum_{i=1}^3\mathbb Cx_i.
\tag{2}
\]
All these spaces are complex vector spaces. The weights in (2) remain
the original positive coordinate weights, not weights assigned after
adjoining invariant generators.

For any nonzero element of positive weight \(m\le2\), its top part
does not lie in the initial-map kernel. Its \(E\)-degree therefore
equals \(m\). Consequently
\[
F_2A=\mathbb C,\qquad V_{2,1}=F_1B,
\qquad \dim_{\mathbb C}F_1B=4.
\tag{3}
\]
If an element of weight three has \(E\)-degree at most one, its top
weighted part must be a nonzero scalar multiple of \(\Phi\). The
difference of two such elements after matching that scalar lies in
\(V_{2,1}\). Therefore \(\dim V_{3,1}\) is either four or five.
The five-dimensional case is exactly the first correction gate:
after normalizing the top part, the element is
\(\Phi(x,z)+\rho z-G_2(x)\) plus an affine-linear good polynomial,
and its second derivative vanishes.[^2]

Similarly, \(\dim F_3A\) is either one or two. A nonconstant element
of this space has top part proportional to \(\Phi\), and its entire
lower part belongs to \(\mathbb Cz+\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3]_{\le2}\).
It is therefore exactly a corrected invariant of the prescribed form.
Conversely both correction gates produce such an element. In short,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{first gate passes}
&\Longleftrightarrow \dim_{\mathbb C}V_{3,1}=5,\\
\text{both gates pass}
&\Longleftrightarrow F_3A\ne\mathbb C
\Longleftrightarrow \dim_{\mathbb C}F_3A=2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{4}
\]
When the first gate passes, choose its normalized representative
\(K\). The image of \(E:V_{3,1}\to A\) is
\(W_1+\mathbb C E(K)\), where
\(W_1=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb C}\{a_1,a_2,a_3\}\) has dimension
three by homogeneous density. The second gate asks whether this image
still has dimension three, rather than four. This is an exact linear
algebra formulation, not a proof that the smaller image occurs.

## 2. An actual normal graded ring without ambient factorial closure

Use the inverse-verified cubic frame in independent coordinates
\((a,b,v,u)\), with \(E=\partial_u\):[^3]
\[
\begin{gathered}
X=u,\qquad Y=v+au,\qquad Z=a-v^3/2+bu,\\
W=b+3av^2/2+3a^2vu/2+a^3u^2/2,
\qquad \Phi=2XW-Y^3,\\
a=Z-XW+Y^3/2,\qquad v=Y-aX,\\
b=W-3av^2/2-3a^2vX/2-a^3X^2/2.
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]
The last three formulas together with \(u=X\) give both polynomial
inverse orders. The good speeds are \((1,a,b)\), and
\(E^2W=a^3\), so this is an actual dense primitive frame.
Put \(\beta=b+av^2/2\). Then \(A=\mathbb C[a,v,\beta]\), and the
inverse formulas give the exact original-coordinate expression
\[
\begin{gathered}
\beta=W-aY^2+\tfrac12a^2XY,\\
\operatorname{in}_{\mathrm{wt}}a=-\Phi/2,
\qquad\operatorname{in}_{\mathrm{wt}}v=X\Phi/2,
\qquad\operatorname{in}_{\mathrm{wt}}\beta=XY\Phi^2/8,\\
\operatorname{gr}_{\mathrm{wt}}A
=\mathbb C[\Phi,X\Phi,XY\Phi^2]
\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{6}
\]
The three displayed initials are algebraically independent. Indeed
\(\Phi,X,Y\) are algebraically independent in the original polynomial
ring, and are recovered rationally from these three initials. Thus no
nonzero weighted top polynomial in the generators \(a,v,\beta\)
can cancel. This proves the equality of graded rings in (6), rather
than merely an inclusion.

This actual invariant graded ring is normal. Nevertheless it contains
\(X\Phi\) and \(\Phi\), but not \(X\): its nonconstant homogeneous
generators have weights three, four, and eight, whereas \(X\) has
weight one. Also \(X=(X\Phi)/\Phi\) lies in its fraction field.
Thus even a normal polynomial invariant graded ring need not be
factorially closed inside the ambient weighted polynomial ring.
Normality cannot justify division by a leading factor; in this example
\(X\) is not integral over the normal graded subring.

The example does have a weight-three invariant \(a\), and both gates
pass. It disproves the proposed transfer of ambient factorial closure,
not the existence of low-weight invariants in some other actual frame.

## 3. What remains unproved by growth

For an arbitrary invariant with top part \(\Phi^k\Psi\), the proved
multiplicity estimate gives \(\operatorname{wt}\Psi\le k\).[^1]
The degree-four exclusion used in the leading dichotomy does not repeat
here: the allowed quotients \(\Psi\) are no longer forced to be constants.
The existence of three algebraically independent invariants therefore
does not by that same counting argument produce an invariant in
\(F_3A\).

No failure of a correction gate is constructed here. Establishing the
low-weight jump in (4) from the actual coordinate and slice hypotheses
remains the precise missing step for this route. The actual control
above rules out one shortcut, and does not replace those hypotheses
by an arbitrary graded algebra or an unrealized speed tuple.
