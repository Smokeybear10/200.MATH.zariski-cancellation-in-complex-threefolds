## 3. Whole-fiber velocity invariance

The root authored and reread the complete
[velocity proof](fiber-velocity-invariance-pass51.md).
Jet and Geometry each independently audited it in full and reported
no mathematical correction.

The proof first localizes at the two velocities forming a unit ideal,
then descends their derivative vanishing to the whole possibly
nonreduced fiber. In the rank-zero strengthening, contraction of a
unit ideal to the finite-dimensional image algebra uses its Artinian
local factors and their nonzero idempotents under the actual embedding.
It does not assume that embedding is flat. The resulting dimension
bound of six concerns one finite-dimensional subalgebra, not the
whole curve fiber, its components, or every higher-degree element.
No additional finite test methods or formal proof are claimed.
