# Lie-rigidity source boundary for the actual-frame program

The new source does not supply the missing frame or make the Lie algebra
of a supplied frame locally finite. The latter implication already fails
for an actual sliced example in this project. This is a source-applicability
audit, not a new dependency of the one-cubic recognition proof.

## 1. Exact primary-source gate

Mohamed Ali Belabbas, [*Rigidity for Lie algebras of locally finite
derivations*, arXiv:2608.12567v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12567v1),
12 August 2026, is a preprint. The full relevant statements and the proofs
of Theorem A, Corollary A′, Lemma 3.1, Proposition 3.3, Theorem D, and
Theorem 5.2 were directly read. This is not an independent validation of
every cited ingredient.

For a finitely generated commutative characteristic-zero algebra,
Theorem A requires a finitely generated Lie subalgebra whose **every
element** is locally finite. It concludes finite-dimensionality.
Corollary A′, for locally finite generators, equates that condition,
finite-dimensionality, and local finiteness of the whole action.

Theorem 5.2 gives an equivalent gate using
\[
H_I=L\cap\{D:D(A)\subseteq I^2\},
\qquad \dim A/I<\infty,\quad\bigcap_{q\ge1}I^q=0:
\]
solvability, nilpotence, or elementwise local finiteness/nilpotence of
\(H_I\) suffices. Theorem D separately applies when the generated Lie
algebra is solvable. None is a generators-only criterion.

## 2. The already verified supplied-frame countercontrol

Use the unchanged example in
[the pass-39 proof, Section 6](whole-frame-recognition-pass39.md).
In physical coordinates \((a,b,c,d)\), put
\[
g=b+c-d^3/6,\qquad
E=\partial_a+g\left(\partial_d+\frac{d^2}{2}\partial_c\right).
\tag{1}
\]
The change to \((a,b,g,d)\) is polynomial with inverse
\(c=g-b+d^3/6\). There,
\[
E=\partial_a+g\partial_d,\qquad E(a)=1.
\]
Thus \(E\) is an actual LND with an actual slice. More precisely, putting
\(r=d-ag\) gives
\[
B=\mathbb C[b,g,r,a],\qquad E=\partial_a\big|_{b,g,r},
\quad\ker E=\mathbb C[b,g,r].
\tag{2}
\]

Consider the actual frame \((a,b,c-a,d)\). In \((a,b,g,d)\), its
first three partials are
\[
\partial_a+\partial_g,\qquad
\partial_b+\partial_g,\qquad\partial_g.
\]
Their first brackets with \(E\) are all \(-\partial_d\), and
their second brackets vanish. The last actual partial is
\[
T_4=\partial_d-\frac{d^2}{2}\partial_g.
\]
Direct computation gives
\[
[E,T_4]=\frac{d^2}{2}\partial_d-gd\partial_g,
\qquad
U=[E,[E,T_4]]=2gd\partial_d-g^2\partial_g,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
[E,U]=3g^2\partial_d\ne0,\qquad
[E,[E,U]]=0.
\tag{4}
\]
This is the exact adjoint-height profile \((1,1,1,3)\), not merely
a bounded abstract matrix example.

Nevertheless,
\[
U^n(g)=(-1)^n n!g^{n+1}\qquad(n\ge0).
\tag{5}
\]
Induction follows from \(U(g)=-g^2\) and the Leibniz rule. Since \(g\)
is a polynomial coordinate, the displayed iterates are linearly
independent over \(\mathbb C\). Hence \(U\) is **not locally finite**,
not only nonnilpotent. It belongs to the Lie algebra generated by
\(E,T_1,T_2,T_3,T_4\), although each generator is locally nilpotent.

The bracket formulas and six initial iterates were independently
recomputed in exact SymPy arithmetic in this audit. The all-degree
failure follows from (5) and transcendence, not the finite run.
The existing pass-39 checker is reused; no duplicate test suite or
new method count is added.

## 3. Consequence for the remaining program

Even the now-recognized supplied one-cubic frame need not satisfy the
source's elementwise-local-finiteness gate. Knowing bounded
\(\operatorname{ad}_E\)-heights of the coordinate partials does not
control the iterates of their middle brackets on the polynomial ring.
The recognition proof does not rely on this failing inference.

For an unknown sliced action, a separate construction of a suitably
finite or solvable generated Lie algebra could make the source useful.
Alternatively one could establish its precise second-order-vanishing
gate. Neither construction is supplied by the source or by the current
frame arguments. Merely renaming the required full Lie-algebra condition
does not reduce the universal frame-existence gap.

The countercontrol excludes this proposed shortcut for the specified
frame. It does not exclude a different frame for the same action,
contradict the preprint, obstruct the polynomial kernel in (2), or
settle general cancellation. No novelty or expert-review claim is made.
