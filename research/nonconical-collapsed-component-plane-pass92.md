# A collapsed closed component is an actual affine plane

If both relative generators collapse on a reduced closed-fiber
component in the actual nonconical setup, its cylinder inside the
original affine four-space is an affine plane. Two affine-linear
equations in the three original good coordinates cut it out exactly.
The universal generator pencil is essential: it makes the slice
correction constant on that component.

At a generic rank-zero component of the quotient differential, the
original velocity Jacobian has rank exactly one along this plane
and its square vanishes there. These are exact restrictions on a
possible exceptional component, not an exclusion of its existence.

## 1. Actual hypotheses and a collapsed component

Retain the supplied original frame, actual invariant plane, and
completion from
[invariant-plane88](constant-acceleration-invariant-plane-pass88.md),
[pencil88](universal-generator-pencil-pass88.md), and
[global-Jacobian91](nonconical-global-jacobian-field-pass91.md):
\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,z]=A[u],\qquad
E=\partial_u,\qquad u=Ez/q,\qquad q=E^2z\in\mathbb C^*,\\
E^2x_i=0,\qquad
S=\mathbb C[s_1,s_2]\subset A=\ker E,\qquad
b=(Ex_1,Ex_2,Ex_3)^t\in S^3,\\
[b,v_2,v_3]\in\operatorname{GL}_3(S),\qquad
R=S[A_1,A_2],\qquad A=R[c],\\
\Omega=\det[b,b_{s_1},b_{s_2}]\ne0,\qquad
\delta h=h\otimes1-1\otimes h
\text{ is nilpotent in }A\otimes_R A.
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
Here \(h\in A\), and the exact reconstruction is
\[
x_{\rm tr}=b(u-h)+v_2A_1+v_3A_2,\qquad
z=c+\frac q2(u^2-h^2).
\tag{2}
\]
The speed row is unimodular. The quotient map from the smooth
threefold \(\operatorname{Spec}A\) to \(\operatorname{Spec}S\) is
faithfully flat with pure curve fibers.

Fix a closed point \(\mathfrak m\) of S and a height-two prime
\(\mathfrak q\) minimal over \(\mathfrak m A\). Assume both
\(\bar A_i\in A/\mathfrak q\) are constants \(\alpha_i\in\mathbb C\).
The reduced component is one-dimensional and is generated by
\(\bar A_1,\bar A_2,\bar c\), so
\(A/\mathfrak q=\mathbb C[\bar c]\), with \(\bar c\) transcendental.
No statement that R embeds on the whole closed fiber is used.

## 2. Tensor evaluation makes the correction constant

Both quotient homomorphisms from A to copies of
\(\mathbb C[\bar c]\) agree on R: its image is exactly
\(\mathbb C\). They therefore induce the balanced homomorphism
\[
A\otimes_R A\longrightarrow
(A/\mathfrak q)\otimes_{\mathbb C}(A/\mathfrak q)
=\mathbb C[c_1,c_2].
\qquad
\bar h(c_1)-\bar h(c_2)=0.
\tag{3}
\]
The equality follows because the image of the nilpotent \(\delta h\)
is zero in this domain. Evaluating at \(c_2=0\) proves
\(\bar h=\eta\in\mathbb C\). This argument uses the whole tensor
nilpotence, not a cancellation of a tensor factor or a presumed
descent \(h\in R\).

Set \(b_0=b(\mathfrak m)\ne0\) and
\(o=-\eta b_0+\alpha_1v_2(\mathfrak m)+\alpha_2v_3(\mathfrak m)\).
Since \(B=A[u]\), extension of \(\mathfrak q\) is a prime ideal
and (2) becomes
\[
B/\mathfrak qB=\mathbb C[\bar c,u],\qquad
\bar x_{\rm tr}=b_0u+o,\qquad
\bar z=\bar c+\frac q2(u^2-\eta^2).
\tag{4}
\]

## 3. Exact plane ideal and the two induced actions

Choose a constant row \(\lambda\) with \(\lambda b_0=1\), and a
basis \(\xi_1,\xi_2\) of the two-dimensional annihilator of \(b_0\).
Define actual affine-linear good coordinates
\[
\begin{gathered}
X=\xi_1(x_{\rm tr}-o),\qquad
Y=\xi_2(x_{\rm tr}-o),\qquad
W=\lambda(x_{\rm tr}-o),\\
\boxed{\ \mathfrak qB=(X,Y)=:\mathfrak P\ },\qquad
B/\mathfrak P=\mathbb C[W,z],\qquad \bar u=W.
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]
The three constant rows are independent, so \(X,Y,W,z\) are
polynomial coordinates on the original B. Equation (4) shows
\((X,Y)\subset\mathfrak qB\). Both are height-two primes in B:
the first is an affine-plane ideal and the second has quotient
\(\mathbb C[\bar c,u]\). Strict containment of these primes would
increase height, proving equality. Equivalently (4) recovers
both u and \(\bar c\) from W and z, so the displayed quotient
map is an isomorphism.

The original partial \(T=\partial_z\) preserves \(\mathfrak P\)
because X and Y do not involve z. E preserves it because
\(\mathfrak q\subset A=\ker E\). Thus their commutator
\(Q=[E,T]\) also preserves it, and the induced derivations are
\[
\bar E=\partial_W+qW\partial_z,\qquad
\bar T=\partial_z,\qquad
\bar Q=[\bar E,\bar T]=0.
\tag{6}
\]
In particular \(Q(B)\subset\mathfrak P\). The cylinder is an actual
stable plane for these two derivations, not merely an abstract
polynomial quotient. Also \(\Omega(\mathfrak m)=0\): otherwise
the retained equality \(A_\Omega=R_\Omega\) would make
\(A/\mathfrak q\) generated by constants, contradicting its
dimension.

## 4. Rank-zero refinement in the ambient cotangent space

Suppose in addition that both \(ds_i\) vanish in
\(\Omega_{A/\mathbb C}\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak q)\).
This is not their automatically zero image in the differentials
of the residue function field. By the polynomial extension
\(B=A[u]\), both differentials vanish generically along the prime
\(\mathfrak P\) in the ambient cotangent module of B. Consequently
all their polynomial coefficients in the original, or the new
affine-linear, coordinate basis belong to \(\mathfrak P\).

Write \(m_i=s_i(\mathfrak m)\). Since \(s_i-m_i\in(X,Y)\),
expand this polynomial in X and Y over \(\mathbb C[W,z]\).
Vanishing of its two transverse first derivatives modulo
\((X,Y)\) removes both linear terms. Therefore
\[
\mathfrak mB\subset\mathfrak P^2,\qquad
EX,EY,EW-1\in\mathfrak P^2,\qquad
u-W\in\mathfrak P.
\tag{7}
\]
The speed statements follow because
\(EX=\xi_1b\), \(EY=\xi_2b\), and \(EW=\lambda b\);
their indicated constant terms at \(\mathfrak m\) are zero,
zero, and one.

Let J be the Jacobian of the original velocity vector
\((b_1,b_2,b_3,qu)^t\). The first three rows vanish modulo
\(\mathfrak P\), by the chain rule through \(s_1,s_2\).
The final row is \(q\,du\), which is nonzero everywhere on the
plane: \(u-W\in\mathfrak P\) gives \(u_W=1\) modulo
\(\mathfrak P\). It also gives \(u_z=0\) modulo that ideal.
Hence
\[
\boxed{\quad
\operatorname{rank}(J\bmod\mathfrak P)=1
\text{ at every point of the plane},\qquad
J^2\equiv0\pmod{\mathfrak P}.
\quad}
\tag{8}
\]
The square assertion can be checked in the original coordinates:
the reduced matrix has only its fourth row, \(q\,du\), and its
fourth entry is \(q u_z=0\).

The bound in
[closed-fiber-multiplicity92](nonconical-closed-fiber-multiplicity-pass92.md)
shows that the rank-zero hypothesis at a generic closed-fiber
component automatically implies the collapse assumed in Section 1.
Thus all conclusions above apply to every such component.

## 5. Scope and evidence

The ideal \((X,Y)\) is stable under E and the actual T, but neither
generator is asserted to be E-invariant. Stability of a
codimension-two ideal does not produce a nonconstant common
invariant or a stable principal invariant divisor. Accordingly
(5)--(8) do not contradict the retained
\(\ker E\cap\ker T=\mathbb C\), and the rank drop along this plane
does not contradict generic nilpotent Jordan type four.

This note proves the original-coordinate plane and Jacobian
consequences directly from the retained actual-frame identities.
It adds no external theorem, numerical method, or assertion that
the hypothetical exceptional component exists. The general
cancellation problem and recognition of the remaining supplied
nonconical branch are not settled here.
