# Quadratic cylinders: explicit flexibility and full modified Derksen invariant

General cancellation remains UNSOLVED and novelty UNKNOWN. This is a direct
extension and reconstruction of a newly located primary preprint, not an
independent discovery of its cubic theorem. The argument below works for any
NONCONSTANT polynomial h in C[t]. It does not show that any of the cylinders
is affine four-space.

## 1. Statement and provenance

Put

    A_h=C[x,y,z,t]/(x^2*y+z^2+x+h(t)),
    X_h=Spec A_h,       B_h=A_h[u].                         (1)

Then X_h times A1 is flexible: at every point its tangent space is spanned
by values of locally nilpotent derivations. More precisely, four explicit
ambient additive actions below generate a transitive group on the cylinder.
Also its modified Derksen invariant is full:

    HD*(B_h)=B_h.                                         (2)

Here HD* denotes the algebra generated by kernels of ALL locally nilpotent
derivations having a slice. This is not just an algebra from selected kernels.

Poloni's arXiv:2609.14090v1, submitted September12,2026, proves the cubic
case h=t^3 with four ambient actions. Root and General read its complete
argument. The new action and adapted embedding used here come from that
preprint. Our argument replaces t^3 by nonconstant h(t), handles arbitrary
critical values of h, and gives the entire polynomial flow. No priority is
claimed for this extension. The preprint is not represented as peer reviewed.
[Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14090v1).

The conductor argument in Section6 uses the image-ideal mechanism in the
proof of Theorem1 of Boldyrev--Gaifullin--Shafarevich's *Modified Derksen
invariant*, arXiv:2312.08421v2. We supply its short transitivity consequence
in full rather than assume finite generation of HD*.
[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08421v2).

## 2. Smooth adapted hypersurface and four whole derivations

The automorphism

    Theta(x,y,z,t,u)=(x,y+u^2,z-x*u,t,u)

carries the cylinder (1) to Y_h=V(F_h) in A5, where

    F_h=x^2*y+2*x*z*u+z^2+x+h(t).

Indeed Theta*(F_h)=x^2*y+z^2+x+h(t), with polynomial inverse
(x,y,z,t,u)->(x,y-u^2,z+x*u,t,u). The original equation is primitive
and linear in y over C[x,z,t], hence defines a domain. Its gradient cannot
vanish on X_h: it would force x=z=0, where the x-derivative equals1.
Thus X_h and Y_h are smooth irreducible varieties of dimensions3 and4.

In the AMBIENT ring C[x,y,z,t,u], set

    a=1+x*y+2*z*u, c=z*y-2*a*u, m=z*y+2*a*u.               (3)

These symbols are auxiliary polynomials, not new independent coordinates,
and are unrelated to the base variables in671--672. Define

    tau=partial_u-x*partial_z+2*u*partial_y,
    delta_z=x^2*partial_z-2*(z+x*u)*partial_y,
    delta_t=x^2*partial_t-h'(t)*partial_y,                 (4)

and define E by

    E(x)=h'(t)*m, E(y)=0, E(z)=-a*y*h'(t),
    E(t)=a*c, E(u)=-y^2*h'(t)/2.                          (5)

The first three are conjugates of the cylinder translation and standard
triangular actions before Theta, so are locally nilpotent. Each kills F_h.
For E direct calculation in the ambient polynomial ring gives

    E(a)=E(c)=E(y)=0,
    E(m)=-2*a*y^2*h'(t),
    E(F_h)=a*h'(t)*(m-2*z*y+c)=0.                         (6)

We now prove whole local nilpotence, not only preservation of the equation.

## 3. Polynomial flow, including a*c=0

Let e=deg h>=1 and introduce a parameter s. Define the POLYNOMIAL

    J_s=sum_(j=1)^e h^(j)(t)*(a*c)^(j-1)*s^j/j!.           (7)

This is the polynomial divided difference of h(t+a*c*s)-h(t) by a*c;
the sum, not division by a possibly zero value, is its definition. The flow is

    t_s=t+a*c*s,          y_s=y,
    z_s=z-a*y*J_s,        u_s=u-y^2*J_s/2,
    x_s=x+m*J_s-a*y^2*J_s^2.                              (8)

All expressions lie in C[x,y,z,t,u,s], including at a=0 or c=0.
Substitution into (3) leaves a and c fixed and sends m to
m-2*a*y^2*J_s. Also dJ_s/ds=h'(t+a*c*s). Therefore (8) solves the
polynomial differential equations associated with (5), with the identity
initial value. The additivity identity

    J_(s+v)(t)=J_s(t)+J_v(t+a*c*s)

proves the group law in (8); the cross term in x is precisely accounted
for by the displayed change in m. The inverse is the same formula at -s.
This proves an ambient polynomial Ga-action, and hence local nilpotence
of E. Its restriction preserves Y_h by (6). In particular
E^(2e+1)(x)=0, and E^(e+1)(z)=E^(e+1)(u)=0.

On the locus a=0 the flow specializes to

    (x,y,z,t,u) -> (x+s*z*y*h'(t), y, z, t,
                    u-s*y^2*h'(t)/2).                    (9)

No localization is used anywhere in this action.

## 4. Transitivity, including every critical value of h

Let G be generated by the four flows in (4)--(5), and G0 by the first
three. All G0 actions fix x. On any fiber x=xi!=0, they can successively
prescribe u, z and t arbitrarily; F_h=0 then determines y uniquely. Thus
G0 is transitive on each such fiber. For example, delta_t translates t by
s*x^2 and changes y by the polynomial

    -sum_(j=1)^e h^(j)(t)*x^(2*j-2)*s^j/j!,

so the assertion remains valid even when h has critical values or h(0)!=0.

Choose t0 with h(t0)*h'(t0)!=0, possible because h is nonconstant, and
choose z0!=0 with z0^2=-h(t0). For every xi in C put

    q_xi=(xi,-1,z0,t0,(xi-1)/(2*z0)).                      (10)

Each lies in Y_h and has a=0. Formula (9) sends q_xi to
q_(xi-s*z0*h'(t0)). Its nonzero speed connects all nonzero-x fibers,
using the G0 transitivity just proved. All points with x!=0 therefore
belong to one G-orbit, which contains q_1.

It remains to send each point with x=0 into this orbit. At x=0 the equation
is z^2+h(t)=0. Three cases exhaust it.

1. If z*h'(t)!=0, use tau to prescribe u=-1/(2*z), and then delta_z to
   prescribe y=-1. At x=0, delta_z changes y at speed -2*z while leaving
   z,t,u fixed. The resulting point has a=0, and (9) changes x at the
   nonzero speed -z*h'(t). It reaches x!=0.

2. If z=0, use tau to make u=1. Then a=1, c=-2, and E(t)=-2. Hence t is
   nonconstant on this E-orbit. If x is nonconstant on that orbit, some
   point already has x!=0. Otherwise x stays zero, and one can choose a
   parameter with h(t)*h'(t)!=0: a nonconstant polynomial t(s) cannot take
   values only in the finite zero set of h*h'. The equation then forces
   z!=0, reducing to Case1.

3. If z!=0 but h'(t)=0, use tau to make u=0 and delta_z to make y=-1.
   Now a=1, c=-z and E(t)=-z!=0. The identical orbit argument in Case2
   either reaches x!=0 or reaches Case1.

This proves transitivity on all of Y_h. There is no omitted component
over a multiple root of h. The constant-polynomial case is deliberately
excluded: then E(x)=0 and this group fixes x, so this proof cannot work.

## 5. Tangent spanning without a group-closure assumption

At q_1 the fields tau,delta_z,delta_t are linearly independent and span
the tangent space of the smooth fiber x=1. This is immediate from their
u,z,t components, whose matrix has nonzero determinant. Also

    E(x)(q_1)=-z0*h'(t0)!=0.

Thus these four LND values span the four-dimensional tangent space there.
Given any other point, the transitivity proof supplies an element of G
carrying q_1 to it. Conjugating the four actions by that element transports
the spanning tangent vectors to that point. These conjugates are still
locally nilpotent actions. This proves flexibility directly.

The group generated by four one-parameter subgroups is not thereby a
finite-dimensional algebraic group. No finite-dimensional Lie closure,
simply transitive unipotent group, or polynomial-coordinate frame follows.

## 6. A conductor lemma and the full modified Derksen invariant

Here is a useful general observation. Let A be a finite-type C-domain with
a nonzero LND, put B=A[u], and suppose some group of automorphisms of
Spec B is transitive on complex points. Then

    HD*(B)=B.                                             (11)

Proof. Write H=HD*(B). Since partial_u has a slice, A is contained in H.
Conjugation preserves LNDs and slices, so H is invariant under every
automorphism of B. In particular it is invariant under every LND L:
interpolation in s in exp(sL)(f), for f in H, extracts L(f) as a finite
C-linear combination of elements of H. This uses characteristic zero.

Let I in A be generated by all D(f), D an LND of A and f in A. It is
nonzero by nonrigidity. Extend D to kill u. For every j>=0 the derivation
u^j*D is locally nilpotent on B. The preceding invariance shows

    u^j*D(f) belongs to H,
    I*B is contained in H.                               (12)

Define the conductor C_H={b in B: bB is contained in H}. It is an ideal
of B, contains the nonzero ideal IB, and is invariant under every
automorphism, because H is. Thus its zero locus is a proper invariant
closed subset of Spec B. Transitivity forces that zero locus to be empty.
Hilbert's Nullstellensatz now gives C_H=B, hence 1 in C_H and H=B.
No finite generation, normality or fraction-field equality of H was assumed.

Apply (11) to A_h. It is nonrigid, since x^2*partial_z-2*z*partial_y
is a nonzero LND on (1). Sections4--5 supply the transitive group on B_h.
This proves (2). The conductor proof adapts the nonzero image ideal in
the cited BGS Theorem1 proof; no priority claim is made for the observation.

## 7. Consequences and limits for cancellation

For h=t^3 the base is the established nonpolynomial Russell cubic.
Poloni's theorem and Section6 therefore rule out BOTH a proper all-SAut
invariant subset and a proper modified Derksen invariant as obstructions
to polynomiality of that particular one-cylinder. They do not establish
its polynomiality. Full flexibility of a cylinder does not force its base
to be affine three-space, as this known nonpolynomial base demonstrates.

The historical bounded subgroup calculations in modified-derksen-pass.md
remain correct as scoped there. Their selected subgroup misses the new
action (5). The earlier full-flexibility uncertainty in contractible-symmetry-
pass31.md is superseded for the cubic by the September12 primary preprint.
Neither update asserts a solution to general cancellation.

Do not transfer this theorem to the genuine two-stage family671--672:
its equation is different, and no isomorphism to (1) has been supplied.
Its full flexibility and polynomial-cylinder status remain undetermined.
Novelty and human expert validation of the present deductions are outstanding.
