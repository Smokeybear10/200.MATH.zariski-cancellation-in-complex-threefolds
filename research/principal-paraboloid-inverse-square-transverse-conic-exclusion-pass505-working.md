## 7. The actual coordinate prime contradicts the resulting square

coefficient becomes \(t^2X^2+d_0\), while its linear Q coefficient

\[

\tag{36}
\]

All of P,z,t lie in \(\operatorname{Frac}A\), and X lies in
\(\mathcal B\). Thus \(Q+tX\) is an existing element of
\(\operatorname{Frac}\mathcal B\).
It is not an assertion that this element is an ambient coordinate.

In the ACTUAL polynomial ring
\(\mathcal B=\mathbb C[X,Y,Z,W]\), the polynomial \(Z-d_0\) is
irreducible and prime because \(d_0\in\mathbb C\).
Its prime valuation on the fraction field assigns value one to
\(Z-d_0\), but assigns the even integer \(2v(Q+tX)\) to the
right side of (36). This is impossible. Equivalently, writing the
existing square root as a reduced rational function contradicts
unique factorization at that prime.
