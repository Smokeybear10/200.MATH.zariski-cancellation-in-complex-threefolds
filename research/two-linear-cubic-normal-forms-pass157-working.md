# Cubic initial normal forms and an actual split-cubic boundary

**Status: working algebraic reduction and explicit actual example.**

Under the height-one hypotheses of the
[two-linear/two-quadratic bound157](two-linear-two-quadratic-initial-bound-pass157-working.md),
a weight-three initial relation reduces to one of
\[
XZ+YW,\qquad XZ-Y^3.
\tag{1}
\]
The changes are actual constant linear changes and triangular polynomial
shears. If a quadratic slot loses its quadratic time term during the
reduction, the retained three-good theorem156 already applies.

This does not recognize either surviving normal form. In particular
the first one occurs in an actual sliced polynomial frame, as shown
below. Its displayed polynomial need not be invariant.
No formal leading equation is being substituted for an invariant
correction in the original ring.

## 1. Hypotheses and the coefficient-row split

Assume an actual polynomial frame with time degrees \((1,1,2,2)\)
for a locally nilpotent derivation \(E\) with a global slice.
The first two ordinary leading coefficients are not proportional.
The homomorphism generated by the four weighted initial forms has
full prime kernel \((F)\), and \(\operatorname{wt}F=3\).
Use weights one for \(X,Y\), two for \(Z,W\).

Every such polynomial is
\[
F=L_1(X,Y)Z+L_2(X,Y)W+J_3(X,Y),
\tag{2}
\]
where the \(L_i\) are homogeneous linear forms and \(J_3\) is a
homogeneous binary cubic. At least one \(L_i\) is nonzero because
the initial forms of \(X,Y\) are algebraically independent.
Thus the span of \(L_1,L_2\) has dimension one or two.

All operations below preserve weighted homogeneity as operations
on the formal polynomial ring. They also define actual polynomial
coordinate changes in the original ring. These are separate facts.
If the transformed four time degrees remain exact, their full
initial kernel is the transformed principal ideal. If any new
quadratic slot has time degree at most one, there are three good
actual coordinates, so retained156 recognizes the kernel.

## 2. Independent linear coefficients

If \(L_1,L_2\) are independent, set \(X'=L_1(X,Y)\),
\(Y'=L_2(X,Y)\), retaining \(Z,W\). The constant matrix is invertible.
Both new good speeds remain nonzero: a zero constant linear
combination would contradict the nonproportionality hypothesis.

Express the resulting binary cubic as
\(J_3(X',Y')=X'Q_2(X',Y')+Y'R_2(X',Y')\).
For instance assign every term divisible by \(X'\) to the first
summand and the remaining pure \(Y'^3\) term to the second.
Now set
\[
Z'=Z+Q_2(X',Y'),\qquad W'=W+R_2(X',Y').
\tag{3}
\]
The inverse subtracts these same polynomials and then uses the
inverse constant good matrix. In these coordinates the literal
polynomial \(F\) is \(X'Z'+Y'W'\).

This is a weight-three relation but an ordinary quadratic polynomial.
Its ordinary degree does not change the time-weight calculation.

## 3. Dependent linear coefficients

If their span has dimension one, write \(L_i=c_iL\), with
\((c_1,c_2)\ne(0,0)\). Use the actual bad linear change
\(Z_1=c_1Z+c_2W\), completed by any independent constant row
to a coordinate pair \((Z_1,W_1)\).
Choose a second good linear form independent of \(L\), and call
the new good pair \(X_1=L,Y_1\).

Then
\[
F=X_1Z_1+J_3(X_1,Y_1)
 =X_1\{Z_1+Q_2(X_1,Y_1)\}+\gamma Y_1^3.
\tag{4}
\]
Here \(J_3=X_1Q_2+\gamma Y_1^3\).
Irreducibility forces \(\gamma\ne0\). Otherwise \(X_1\) is a
proper factor of \(F\), while the other factor still involves \(Z_1\).
Set
\(Z'=-(Z_1+Q_2)/\gamma\), and retain \(X_1,Y_1,W_1\).
This is an actual shear followed by a nonzero constant scaling,
with inverse \(Z_1=-\gamma Z'-Q_2\).
Equation (4) becomes
\(-\gamma(X_1Z'-Y_1^3)\).
Multiplying a principal ideal generator by a nonzero complex
constant does not change the initial kernel.

No coefficient-field automorphism, division by a coordinate, or
algebraic extension has entered either reduction.

## 4. The split form occurs in an actual sliced frame

Start with \(B=\mathbb C[u,a,b,c]\), \(E=\partial_u\).
Put
\[
\begin{gathered}
X=u,\qquad Y=a+bu,\qquad Z_0=c-bu,\qquad W_0=b+u,\\
H=XZ_0+YW_0=ab+u(c+a+b^2),\\
Z=Z_0+YH,\qquad W=W_0-XH.
\end{gathered}
\tag{5}
\]
The intermediate frame is actual: from \(X,Y,Z_0,W_0\) recover
\[
u=X,\qquad b=W_0-X,\qquad
a=Y-bX,\qquad c=Z_0+bX.
\tag{6}
\]
For the second change the polynomial \(H\) is preserved exactly:
\[
XZ+YW=XZ_0+YW_0.
\]
Hence its polynomial inverse is
\[
H=XZ+YW,\qquad Z_0=Z-YH,\qquad W_0=W+XH.
\tag{7}
\]
Substituting (7) into (6) gives a complete polynomial inverse.
In the forward-after-inverse order,
\(XZ_0+YW_0=X(Z-YH)+Y(W+XH)=H\);
the two added terms therefore cancel again.
In the inverse-after-forward order the same preservation identity
returns \(Z_0,W_0\), and (6) returns \(u,a,b,c\).
Both orders hold on the entire fourfold, including \(X=0\).

Write \(p=c+a+b^2\). The ordinary leading coefficients in the
new frame are
\[
(\operatorname{lc}_u X,\operatorname{lc}_u Y,
  \operatorname{lc}_u Z,\operatorname{lc}_u W)
=(1,b,bp,-p).
\tag{8}
\]
All are nonzero polynomials, so the time degrees are exactly
\((1,1,2,2)\). The initial map sends
\((X,Y,Z,W)\) to \((\eta,b\eta,bp\eta^2,-p\eta^2)\).
Its fraction field contains
\(\eta,\ b=Y/X,\ p=-W/X^2\), which are algebraically independent.
Indeed \(a,b,p\) are polynomial coordinates for the invariant
ring, via \(c=p-a-b^2\).
Thus the initial image has transcendence degree three.
The irreducible polynomial \(XZ+YW\) vanishes in it, so height one
forces its full prime kernel to be precisely \((XZ+YW)\).
Irreducibility follows, for example, by treating it as a primitive
linear polynomial in \(Z\) over \(\mathbb C[X,Y,W]\), since
\(\gcd(X,YW)=1\).

But the corresponding actual polynomial is not invariant:
\[
E(XZ+YW)=p\ne0,\qquad E^2(XZ+YW)=0.
\tag{9}
\]
It has time degree one, not zero. The actual inverse, the slice,
the principal initial ideal and the cubic normal form therefore
do not justify assuming this polynomial itself belongs to \(\ker E\).

## 5. Exact selected-partial shifts in the example

The inverse above gives
\[
b=W+XH-X,\qquad a=Y-Xb,\qquad
c=Z-YH+Xb,\qquad H=XZ+YW.
\]
For \(T_Z=\partial_Z\), differentiating at fixed \(X,Y,W\) gives
\[
T_Zb=X^2,\qquad T_Za=-X^3,\qquad
T_Zc=1-XY+X^3,\qquad T_Zu=0.
\tag{10}
\]
For \(T_W=\partial_W\), the corresponding formulas are
\[
T_Wb=1+XY,\qquad T_Wa=-X-X^2Y,\qquad
T_Wc=X-Y^2+X^2Y,\qquad T_Wu=0.
\tag{11}
\]
Substitution of \(X=u,Y=a+bu\) shows that both actual all-ring
shifts are three. The respective top intrinsic derivations are
\[
N_Z=-\partial_a+\partial_c,\qquad
N_W=b(-\partial_a+\partial_c).
\tag{12}
\]
They are nonzero LNDs and fix \(1,b,bp,-p\), as required by the
structural theorem157. This boundary is consistent with that
theorem's necessary cubic shift lower bound; it does not tighten
the bound to equality two.

The example is a polynomial translation with coordinate slice \(X=u\).
It is not a hypothetical nonpolynomial-kernel example or a true
minimum-degree-six frame: the initial base frame has a much smaller
degree sum. It prevents an unconditional exclusion of split-cubic
initial relations, but it does not obstruct recognizing all such
frames by a different argument.

## Remaining task and verification boundary

The next problem is to derive additional actual compatibility
conditions, construct an invariant correction where justified,
or recognize the original kernel by another route. The two normal
forms alone do none of those things. The weight-four and
height-two-initial cases of157 also remain open.

The algebraic changes and the explicit example are written out here.
Any finite accompanying controls check those formulas only; they do
not prove a universal recognition or an arbitrary-entry statement.
No novelty clearance, external contact, human expert review or
formal proof-assistant verification is claimed.
