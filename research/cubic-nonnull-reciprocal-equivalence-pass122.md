# A non-null reciprocal scalar is ambient-equivalent to a cubic scalar

The reciprocal scalar alternative121 is not a separate branch for a
non-null axis. A constant linear change of the second and third original
good coordinates turns it into a cubic polynomial scalar with nonzero
leading coefficient. The change is an actual global polynomial
automorphism, not a rational reparametrization asserted to be an
automorphism of the invariant plane.

The first-integral field need not be generated by the normal ratio.
Its possible finite extension is unchanged. This is an equivalence
of supplied actual frames, not their recognition or a cancellation
result.

## 1. The non-null scalar data

Retain the actual framework90 and the non-null normal form and scalar
classification of
[normal forms121](cubic-axis-scalar-normal-forms-pass121.md) and
[non-null classification121](cubic-nonnull-axis-scalar-constraint-pass121.md).
In particular,

\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,z]=A[u],\quad Eu=1,\quad Ez=q_0u,\quad
q_0\in\mathbb C^*,\\
S=\mathbb C[s_1,s_2],\quad K=\operatorname{Frac}S,\quad
b=E(x_1,x_2,x_3)^t=(g,P,Y)^t\in S^3,\quad
h=P/Y\notin\mathbb C,\\
L=\mathbb C(H)\subset K,\quad L/\mathbb C(h)\text{ finite},\quad
\partial h=1,\quad L^\partial=\mathbb C,\quad
D\in L,\quad g^2-DY^2=1,\\
Q=\operatorname{diag}(-1,M),\qquad
M=\begin{pmatrix}
D''/2&(D'-hD'')/2\\
(D'-hD'')/2&D-hD'+h^2D''/2
\end{pmatrix},\\
D\ne0,\quad D'''\ne0,\quad \det Q\ne0,\quad
\Omega=\det[b,b_{s_1},b_{s_2}]\ne0 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]

Primes in (1) denote the unique extension of d/dh to L. The actual
intrinsic derivation, its full kernel S, the supplied slice u and the
original polynomial-coordinate inverse remain part of the retained
framework. The generic relative equation has exact degree three.
The axis is the first coordinate vector, with constant Q-norm -1.

## 2. General constant changes of the orthogonal complement

Choose constants alpha,beta,gamma,delta with Lambda nonzero below.
A tilde denotes transformed data, not differentiation. Put

\[
\begin{gathered}
T=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha&\beta\\\gamma&\delta\end{pmatrix}
 \in\operatorname{GL}_2(\mathbb C),\quad
\Lambda=\alpha\delta-\beta\gamma,\quad \sigma=\gamma h+\delta,\\
\binom{\widetilde x_2}{\widetilde x_3}
 =T\binom{x_2}{x_3},\qquad
\binom{\widetilde P}{\widetilde Y}=T\binom{P}{Y},\qquad
\widetilde x_1=x_1,\quad \widetilde z=z,\\
\widetilde Y=\sigma Y,\quad
\widetilde h=\frac{\alpha h+\beta}{\gamma h+\delta},\quad
\widetilde Q=\operatorname{diag}(-1,T^{-t}MT^{-1}),\qquad
\widetilde D=\frac{D}{\sigma^2}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{2}
\]

The actual coordinate change has polynomial inverse T inverse on the
last two good coordinates and fixes x1,z. Thus the new coordinates
generate exactly B. Their speed entries are still polynomials in S.
The function sigma is nonzero: h is nonconstant and the second row
of T is nonzero. In particular the new Y is nonzero. The displayed
fractional expressions are used in K, not to define the ambient
coordinate change.

The metric congruence follows by substituting
b=diag(1,T inverse) times the new b into the old quadratic form.
It also shows that the axis and its fixed orthogonal complement are
preserved. With v=(h,1) transposed and new v=(new h,1) transposed,
one has new v=Tv/sigma. Consequently new v transposed new M new v
is D/sigma squared, proving the last formula in (2).

## 3. The differential and metric transformation laws

The fractional linear transformation is invertible, so
C(new h)=C(h) as subfields of L. It does not change the degree of
the possibly proper extension L over that field. Direct differentiation
and normal transformation give

\[
\begin{gathered}
\partial\widetilde h=\Lambda/\sigma^2,\qquad
\widetilde\partial=(\sigma^2/\Lambda)\partial,\qquad
\widetilde\partial\widetilde h=1,\quad
L^{\widetilde\partial}=\mathbb C,\\
T^{-t}\binom{1}{-h}
 =\frac{\sigma}{\Lambda}\binom{1}{-\widetilde h},\\
\widetilde\partial\,\widetilde M
 =\frac{\sigma^4D'''}{2\Lambda^3}
 \binom{1}{-\widetilde h}(1,-\widetilde h),\qquad
\widetilde\partial^{\,3}\widetilde D
 =\frac{\sigma^4}{\Lambda^3}D'''\ne0 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{3}
\]

For the matrix derivative, T is constant, the old M derivative is
D'''/2 times (1,-h) transposed times (1,-h), and the second line
of (3) gives the displayed factor. The scalar reconstruction in
normal forms121 now applies in the differential field
(L,new partial): new M is exactly the same three-entry formula
in new D and new h as in (1). Its upper-left derivative is one
half of the third derivative of new D. This proves the last line
without assuming D is rational in h.

The remaining rank and scalar identities are

\[
\begin{gathered}
\det\widetilde Q=\frac{\det Q}{\Lambda^2}\ne0,\qquad
\widetilde D\ne0,\qquad
g^2-\widetilde D\,\widetilde Y^2=1,\\
\operatorname{rank}\widetilde Q=3,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}(\widetilde Q|_{\ker(0,1,-\widetilde h)})=2 .
\end{gathered}
\tag{4}
\]

The plane restriction follows either from congruence or from
its diagonal entries -1 and new D. Thus the derivative and
nondegeneracy hypotheses of the non-null normal form survive.

## 4. The reciprocal alternative becomes an exact cubic

Now use the classified reciprocal scalar and the indicated constant
matrix:

\[
D(h)=a_2h^2+a_1h+a_0+\frac{c}{\lambda h+\mu},
\quad a_i,c,\lambda,\mu\in\mathbb C,\quad c\lambda\ne0,\qquad
T=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\\lambda&\mu\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{5}
\]

Here Lambda=-lambda and sigma=lambda h+mu. The actual coordinate
change and its two-sided inverse are especially simple:

\[
\begin{gathered}
\widetilde x_2=x_3,\quad
\widetilde x_3=\lambda x_2+\mu x_3,\qquad
x_3=\widetilde x_2,\quad
x_2=(\widetilde x_3-\mu\widetilde x_2)/\lambda,\\
\widetilde P=Y,\quad \widetilde Y=\lambda P+\mu Y,\quad
\xi:=\widetilde h=\frac1{\lambda h+\mu},\\
\widetilde D
=\xi^2D\!\left(\frac{\xi^{-1}-\mu}{\lambda}\right)
=c\xi^3+
 \left(a_0-\frac{\mu a_1}{\lambda}
              +\frac{\mu^2a_2}{\lambda^2}\right)\xi^2
+\left(\frac{a_1}{\lambda}
              -\frac{2\mu a_2}{\lambda^2}\right)\xi
+\frac{a_2}{\lambda^2}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{6}
\]

Expansion proves the last formula, whose leading coefficient is
c nonzero. In particular it is an exact cubic polynomial, not merely
a polynomial of degree at most three. The new third derivative is
6c, also obtained from (3). No divisor was inverted in the ambient
polynomial ring: the only division in the inverse coordinate map
is by the nonzero complex number lambda.

## 5. All actual potential and global frame hypotheses persist

Let R=diag(1,T), a constant three-by-three matrix. In the original
invariant-plane coordinates, the exact reconstruction90 is
x_tr=b(u+rho)+sum b_si y_i. Multiplication by R gives the new
reconstruction with exactly the same rho and y_i. Since R is
constant, every derivative of the new b is R times the old derivative.
The radial coefficients G_ij, the tangential connection coefficients,
and the cubic tensor obtained by expanding b_ij in its moving frame
are therefore unchanged. The corresponding conclusions are

\[
\begin{gathered}
\widetilde\rho=\rho,\quad \widetilde y_i=y_i,\quad
\widetilde G_{ij}=G_{ij},\quad
\widetilde C^k_{ij}=C^k_{ij},\quad
\widetilde T_{ijk}=T_{ijk},\\
\widetilde\chi=\chi,\quad \widetilde\Psi=\Psi,\quad
\widetilde\Phi=\Phi,\qquad
\widetilde\Omega=\Lambda\Omega,\quad
\widetilde{\mathcal J}=\Lambda\mathcal J,\quad
\frac{\widetilde{\mathcal J}}{\widetilde\Omega}
 =\frac{\mathcal J}{\Omega}.
\end{gathered}
\tag{7}
\]

Here the constant matrix T in (2) and the three-index cubic tensor
in (7) are different objects. The scalar chi and quadratic Psi
are unchanged because z,u are fixed and the radial data and rho
are unchanged. The cubic potential is built from those same data.
The original volume and the moving-frame determinant both pick up
the same nonzero constant determinant of R.

In particular, the actual generic generators and their evaluation
ideal are unchanged; its exact relative degree remains three.
The derivation E, its highest original-z adjoint, embedded base S,
slice u, rational cubic direction V and its field L remain the same.
There is one orientation normalization to retain: the Jacobian
derivation of [global field91](nonconical-global-jacobian-field-pass91.md),
defined using the ordered new original coordinates, is the old N
divided by Lambda. Thus its full kernel, degree filtration and
primitivity are unchanged, while it need not be the identical
normalized derivation. This agrees with the unchanged highest
adjoint being twice new Omega times new N. The actual original
coordinate isomorphism is preserved globally by the explicit linear
automorphism, not inferred from a nonzero generic Jacobian.

Thus any supplied non-null reciprocal actual frame would already
give a supplied non-null cubic actual frame. The two scalar labels
are not separate branches modulo these allowed constant changes.
This equivalence does not exclude the resulting cubic scalar and
does not identify its kernel as a polynomial ring.
