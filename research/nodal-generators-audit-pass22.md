# Independent audit of the five-generator conductor presentation

The complete [five-generator note](nodal-generators-pass22.md) passes
this independent audit. Its five functions generate the coordinate
ring of the original fixed pinched surface, not a modified gluing or
only its normalization. The conclusion is an explicit embedding in
affine five-space. It does not establish minimality, an embedding in
affine three-space, or compatibility with an ambient quotient LND.

The [pass20 conductor construction](nodal-conductor-pass20.md) and
the [pass21 embedding note](nodal-embedding-pass21.md) were reread
completely for the inherited ring and local hypotheses. No new search
or priority assessment was performed in this audit.

## 1. The normalized surface is the original surface

The change w=a_0*u and z_new=a_0^2*z_old changes the original equation
to (u-1)(u-i)z_new=y(y-1). The old conductor generator becomes
3(u^4-1), so its ideal is unchanged. On every split and unsplit line,
the new common parameter is exactly a_0^3 times the old parameter.
This same nonzero scale on all six lines preserves every original
pairwise identification.

I independently substituted the six line parametrizations into
c,tau,kappa,zeta. The two branches in each pair give exactly the
displayed row of the restriction table. In particular, c has the
three distinct values 1+i, 1-i and -1+i, while tau equals the common
parameter on every line. Thus all five proposed generators belong to
the same ring R. The distinction between u in the normalization and
functions belonging to R is respected throughout the proof.

## 2. Global finiteness is justified

The displayed polynomials U,V satisfy Ub+Vp=1 exactly. I checked
this identity and the independent polynomial reconstruction of y
from u,c,tau,z by direct expansion. Together with
z=zeta+beta(c,tau)u, this proves B=S[u] for the proposed subring S.
The relation u^2-cu-kappa=0 is monic. Hence B is a finite S-module
generated by 1,u, and its submodule R is finite because S is
Noetherian.

There is no inference that this two-generator module is free or that
the common fraction field has degree two. Nor is point separation
being used as a substitute for this necessary finiteness argument.

## 3. The global point-collision analysis is exhaustive

When two normalization points have the same u and generator values,
the polynomial reconstruction recovers both y and z, so the points
are equal. Distinct u-values with equal kappa and d must satisfy
u'=epsilon*u and c=(1+epsilon)u, with u nonzero and
epsilon in {-1,i,-i}.

The d=0 case is completely covered by the six restrictions. The
three distinct c-tags exclude collisions between different pairs,
and tau separates the points along each identified line. These are
exactly the identifications already present in R.

Away from d=0, A does not vanish. The only additional root of b is
m=(1+i)/2. The identity a(m)=3m/2 excludes this root for either
member of any proposed collision: neither 1+epsilon nor
1+1/epsilon equals 3/2. Thus the rational chart used in the
remaining case has nonzero denominators at both points.

I independently constructed Y,Z,T from their defining formulas,
without importing the companion checker. For all three epsilon,
the reduced numerators of the tau and zeta differences have monic
gcd u over Q(i)[u]. This agrees with the checker's separate exact
polynomial gcd and extended-Bézout controls. A remaining collision
would force u=0, contradicting the distinct-root case. The special
root u=0 is not discarded without reason: equality of d then forces
the other u-value to be zero, already handled by reconstruction.

Finally, d separates conductor from nonconductor points. Thus no
additional mixed case is missing. Finiteness supplies surjectivity
on closed points, and all their residue fields are C.

## 4. Tangent conditions hold on the pinched ring itself

Off the conductor, the normalization is the surface itself. For
u nonzero the differential of d supplies the u-direction. At fixed
u, c supplies the other direction unless b=0; at that exceptional
root tau has nonzero derivative p(m). At u=0, the coordinates u,c
are valid. Kappa supplies the missing direction when c is nonzero;
the displayed nonzero derivative of T handles c=0.

As a separate check of that last exceptional point, I computed the
ambient gradients of the surface relation, c,tau,zeta at
(u,y,z)=(0,1,0). They have rank three, so the restrictions of the
proposed functions span the two-dimensional surface cotangent space.

At the conductor, the completed local ring has two independent
normal directions and one common tangent direction. It is not enough
to immerse each normalization branch separately. The main proof
retains this distinction: tau provides the common tangent, and the
normal coefficients of d on both branches are nonzero.

I independently formed the three-by-three Jacobian of u, the surface
relation, and tau on each of the six branches. Solving that implicit
system gives the derivative vector with du=1, relation derivative
zero and tau fixed. Applying the gradients of c and zeta, then
dividing by the derivative of d, reconstructs the two normal ratios.
This calculation does not reuse the checker's branch-normal routine.

The differences of the c-ratios are precisely the three linear
polynomials in the main table. At their respective unique roots,
the zeta-ratio differences are

\[
\frac{3}{16},\qquad
\frac{9381}{20000}+\frac{4691}{40000}i,\qquad
\frac{9381}{20000}-\frac{4691}{40000}i.
\]

All are nonzero. Thus d together with c or zeta spans both normal
directions at every complex parameter value. Along with tau, these
span the entire three-dimensional cotangent space of R at every
conductor point. No sampling of a finite set of parameter values is
used to assert the unrestricted tangent statement: the first minor
is linear and its only possible zero is handled exactly.

## 5. The final recognition and elimination steps are valid

The finite, closed-point-bijective, cotangent-surjective map is an
isomorphism here by the direct two-stage Nakayama argument in the
note. After localizing at a maximal ideal of S, the finite source
algebra has exactly one maximal ideal and the same residue field.
Cotangent surjectivity first gives n=mB+n^2. Nakayama over the local
source gives n=mB. Residue-field equality then gives B=A+mB, and
Nakayama on the finite target-local module B/A makes that quotient
zero. The finite global module R/S vanishes at every maximal ideal,
hence vanishes. This uses cotangents of R, not just B's normalization
cotangents.

The elimination presentation also has no missing saturation. In
P[U_0]/J the displayed y_* and z_* satisfy the normalized surface
relation. Conversely the remaining relations identify C,T,K,D with
their proposed expressions, and the definition of z_* recovers
Z_0. These two algebra maps are inverse, establishing P[U_0]/J=B.
Restricting to P then gives the actual subring R and kernel J cap P.
The note correctly does not claim to have computed a minimal list of
relations in the five generators.

Both failed-triple statements are scoped correctly. A ring generated
by d,du,g would give a one-generated quotient R/(d,du)=C[t]^3,
which is impossible. The second explicit pair consists of distinct
nonconductor points with equal c,tau,zeta and different d. Neither
argument excludes arbitrary other triples or four-generator sets.

## 6. Verification and limitations

The initial independent standalone run of
`research/nodal_generators_checks_pass22.py` passed all seven methods
in 1.508 seconds. After the exactness cleanup below, the complete
seven-method suite independently passed again in 1.503 seconds.
Separate inline controls checked the six branch
restrictions, Bézout and y reconstruction, all three collision gcds,
the exceptional smooth tangent, and the implicit-Jacobian conductor
calculation described above. These inline controls are not additional
regression methods.

The main agent noticed a Python integer reciprocal producing a float
in one exceptional-root inequality check. That integer was replaced
with its exact SymPy counterpart before the final rerun.
This is an exactness cleanup for the checker, not a failed mathematical
assertion. The identities and inequalities were also verified
independently with exact complex rational arithmetic in this audit.

No mathematical correction was required. The resulting proven bound
for this fixed ring is three to five algebra generators. The exact
minimum, the affine-three-space embedding question, and the ambient
LND realization remain unresolved. The proof contains finite exact
computer-algebra certificates together with written ring and local
arguments; it is not a Lean proof or a human expert review.
