## Actual surface nonplanarity

The main investigator and a separate investigator reconstructed the
complete principal-component proof. The ring and conductor gluing
are the actual ones already constructed in earlier passes.

The first component prime is (d+1,e,h), with polynomial quotient
generated by g. The section component has normalization C[u],
using the monic relation u^4=d+1 and rational inverse u=e/d.
Their sole intersection is off the conductor, where the actual
local ring is C[u,y]_(u,y) and their ideals are (u),(y).
The intersection has scheme-theoretic length one.

Under a hypothetical plane embedding of the entire h=0 fiber,
the first component's inverse-image prime is height one in C[X,Y]
and hence principal. Its generator pulls back on the second
component's normalization to a polynomial with one simple zero,
necessarily c*u. The actual identification 1~i makes this impossible.
The full fiber need not be reduced, and no plane-line rectification
theorem is assumed.

This excludes every generating triple containing h, not all
three-generator presentations. The specialization of the rank-nine
determinant is a global nonmonogenicity statement on H=0; it does
not assert one closed finite base fiber obstructs every possible
candidate.
