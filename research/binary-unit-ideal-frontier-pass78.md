# The binary zero locus is excluded in the supplied-good branch

The actual binary parameters in the nonlinear supplied-good nice
branch generate the unit ideal. A polynomial tangent-plane invariant
rules out their common zero, including ramified and nonreduced
possibilities. This closes the proper-parameter obstruction left
open in pass77. It does not solve unrestricted cancellation.

The working target remains A[s]=C^[4] implies A=C^[3]. This is the
unconfirmed complex-threefold interpretation of the professor's

Neither the intended correction nor the field and dimension have

## 1. The exact restricted setting

Let
\[
\begin{gathered}
B=\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,w]=A[s],\quad A=\ker E,\quad Es=1,\\
E=P\cdot\partial,\quad E(P_i)=0,\quad
(\operatorname{Jac}P)^2\ne0,\quad T=\partial_w,\quad [E,[T,E]]=0.
\end{gathered}
\tag{1}
\]
Here E is an actual polynomial locally nilpotent derivation. Niceness
means that all four displayed coordinate trajectories are straight
lines. It is not automatic from the existence of a slice. The
constant good direction T must already be available. Assume also
the nonlinear ternary velocity cone of pass76.

The [binary descent](nice-binary-parameter-descent-pass77.md) supplies
actual coprime invariant polynomials p,q and three independent
homogeneous binary forms h_i of one degree e>=2 with P_i=h_i(p,q)
for i<=3. Its published parametrization input is recorded separately;
no ambient-coordinate status for p,q is assumed.[^binary]

With Q=[T,E], the common-invariant argument gives Qp=Qq=0. The
restriction delta=Q|A is nonzero. Its primitive direction D has
common invariant ring S=ker D=ker delta isomorphic to C[u,v]. The
actual inclusion S into B is faithfully flat. This last assertion
uses the intrinsic quotient theorem, not a generic-rank inference.
The cylinder makes Spec A smooth and contractible and provides
domination by an affine three-plane, so the precise
Kaliman--Saveliev result applies.[^quotient]

## 2. Why the proper parameter ideal is impossible

The complete [normal-invariant proof](binary-normal-syzygy-exclusion-pass78.md)
uses the formal binary normal N=h_p cross h_q. Its three components
are independent complex homogeneous forms of degree 2e-2. Their
independence follows from the elementary fact that two homogeneous
binary forms of the same positive degree with zero Jacobian are
proportional. Both E and Q kill the original polynomial
\[
c=\sum_{i=1}^3N_i(p,q)x_i\in S.
\tag{2}
\]

Faithful flatness contracts c from the ideal generated by N_i in B
to that same ideal in S. Thus c=sum N_i a_i for actual a_i in S.
Suppose I=(p,q)B were proper and take a minimal prime n over I.
Coprimality gives height n=2. In the regular local ring L=B_n,
the parameter sequence has the full, possibly nonreduced associated
graded (L/I L)[xi,eta].[^graded]

Taking degree 2e-2 in sum N_i(p,q)(x_i-a_i)=0 forces every
x_i-a_i into I L. Independence of the N_i persists over the
nonreduced coefficient ring because a nonzero complex coefficient
minor is a unit there. The contraction n intersect S is a closed
point m. Consequently n contains the three independent affine
linear equations x_i-a_i(m), which generate an ideal of height
three. This contradicts height n=2.

The precise resulting conclusion is
\[
(p,q)S=S,\qquad(P_1,P_2,P_3)S=S,
\qquad V_B(p,q)=V_B(P_1,P_2,P_3)=\varnothing.
\tag{3}
\]
The equalities extend to A and B. They are stronger than gcd one.
No division by a ramification determinant, reduced-fiber assumption,
or coordinate change of the parameter pair is used.

## 3. A constructive relative hypersurface reduction

The [relative presentation](binary-relative-hypersurface-pass78.md)
uses (3) to build an invariant-coefficient transverse frame. Divide
the binary normal by its full binary gcd. Its evaluated primitive
row n and the transverse velocity H both generate unit ideals over
S, with nH=0. Choose lambda H=1 and nW0=1, then put
W=W0-(lambda W0)H and V=n cross lambda.
The matrix [H,V,W] has determinant one. This elementary identity
supplies its inverse without a projective-module classification.

The first two inverse rows applied to the actual transverse
coordinates give sigma,Y with E sigma=1 and EY=0. The last row
gives a common invariant c0 in S. Set Z=w-P4 sigma. The explicit
inverse expresses every original coordinate in S[sigma,Y,Z].
The slice identity consequently gives
\[
A=S[Y,Z]\simeq S[U,V]/(F),\qquad
B=A[\sigma],
\tag{4}
\]
where the presentation kernel is a height-one principal prime.
The notation S[Y,Z] means the generated algebra, not algebraic
independence of Y,Z. This is a relative plane-curve presentation,
not a claim that F is a coordinate or that sigma is an ambient
coordinate of the original four-space.

## 4. Other deductions and superseded obstructions

The [Wronskian theorem](binary-wronskian-derived-rank-pass78.md)
proves generic rank four for E,T,Q,[T,Q] throughout this nonlinear
supplied-good branch, including dependent and unit parameter pairs.
It also proves Q^2(x) is not rationally parallel to Q(x). Thus Q is
not nice in the original coordinates. No universal third-iterate
bound follows. The retained conic example realizes the determinant
with value two and is already a recognized action.

The [Euler matrix](binary-euler-reduction-pass78.md) gives an exact
fraction-field factorization of the original Jacobian square when
p,q are independent. It does not assert regularity of parameter
derivatives. The [socle calculation](binary-ramification-socle-pass78.md),
[reduced-component exclusion](binary-reduced-fiber-exclusion-pass78.md)
and [derivative-pole obstruction](binary-regular-derivative-exclusion-pass78.md)
remain valid intermediate arguments. Their proper-parameter premise
is now excluded by (2)--(3). They should not be used to keep that
same branch on the active search list. The genuine pole example
still disproves pole removal from weaker intrinsic-plane data alone.

Separately, the [nonlinear-shear theorem](nonlinear-shear-projective-obstruction-pass78.md)
excludes nonplanar nilpotent-Jacobian image when the projective
parameter has the form y-F(x) with nonconstant F'. It covers a
specified larger class, not arbitrary rational projective parameters.
Nilpotence is not treated as a polynomial-inverse certificate.
