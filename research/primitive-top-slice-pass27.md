# A sliced primitive pair with a nonsliced primitive highest adjoint

Two genuine coordinate LNDs can have a primitive highest adjoint with
no slice, even when the original pair has only constant joint rational
invariants and generates a finite-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra.
Thus the missing slice does not follow from any of those properties.

The example below satisfies the actual polynomial-pair hypotheses:
B=C^[4], delta(lambda)=1, Delta(lambda)=0, with both delta and Delta
primitive and sliced. It is not asserted to be the canonical vertical
action of a nonunit-plinth local-slice homotopy. That narrower possible
hypothesis is not refuted by this example.

There is a positive boundary too. One further bracket produces a
sliced tangent coordinate partial. The calculation does not defeat
every finite Lie-word strategy or settle cancellation.

## 1. The actual two coordinate actions

Put B=k[lambda,a,b,c], where k=C, and define

\[
\delta=\partial_\lambda,\qquad
\Delta=\partial_a+\lambda a\partial_b+\lambda b\partial_c.
\tag{1}
\]

Both are triangular LNDs in the order lambda,a,b,c. They satisfy
delta(lambda)=1, Delta(lambda)=0 and Delta(a)=1. In particular
both are sliced and primitive, since one of their generator values
is one.

There is an independent full coordinate certificate for Delta.
Set

\[
U=b-\frac{\lambda a^2}{2},\qquad
V=c-\lambda ab+\frac{\lambda^2a^3}{3}.
\tag{2}
\]

Then Delta(U)=Delta(V)=0. The exact polynomial inverse is

\[
b=U+\frac{\lambda a^2}{2},\qquad
c=V+\lambda aU+\frac{\lambda^2a^3}{6}.
\tag{3}
\]

Thus B=k[lambda,U,V,a] and Delta is partial_a in those coordinates.
The symbols a and lambda are retained by the coordinate map.
These identities verify both inverse directions, rather than merely
checking a Jacobian or declaring a polynomial with derivative one
to be a slice for an unproved LND.

## 2. The highest adjoint is already primitive and has no slice

The exact adjoint orbit is

\[
L=[\delta,\Delta]=a\partial_b+b\partial_c,
\qquad [\delta,L]=0.
\tag{4}
\]

Hence L is the nonzero highest adjoint. It kills lambda and a,
and is locally nilpotent by triangularity. Its nonzero coefficients
a,b have gcd one, so L is already primitive. Nevertheless its fixed
locus is the plane a=b=0 in B. Every value of L belongs to (a,b),
so it cannot send any polynomial to one.

In fact the full kernel and plinth are exact:

\[
C_L=\ker L=k[\lambda,a,H],\qquad H=2ac-b^2,
\]
\[
\ker L^2=C_L\oplus C_Lb,
\qquad L(\ker L^2)=aC_L.
\tag{5}
\]

To reconstruct them, localize at a. Then
B_a=k[lambda,a,a^(-1),H,b], with L=a*partial_b.
An element killed by L^2 is of the form

\[
a^{-N}(Q_0(\lambda,a,H)+bQ_1(\lambda,a,H)).
\tag{6}
\]

Choose N minimal with Q_0,Q_1 polynomial. If N>0 and (6) lies
in B, reduction of its numerator modulo a gives
Q_0(lambda,0,-b^2)+bQ_1(lambda,0,-b^2)=0.
The even and odd powers of b separate. Substitution H->-b^2
is injective on k[lambda,H], so both Q_i are divisible by a,
contradicting minimality. Thus N=0. The same argument with
Q_1=0 proves the kernel assertion, and applying L gives the
displayed full plinth. No truncation or degree bound was used.

Primitive normalization cannot help, since it has already been
performed. More generally, every regular derivation in the rational
line Frac(B)*L is a polynomial multiple of L. Indeed a denominator
in lowest terms would divide both a and b and hence be a unit.
All such regular multiples still vanish on a=b=0. Thus even a
regular rational saturation confined to this orbit direction cannot
produce a polynomial with derivative one.

This is the inherited primitive-UFD saturation principle applied
to the exact control, not a new saturation theorem.[^ufd]

## 3. The original pair has no joint rational invariant

The joint invariant ring and field of the original actions are

\[
\ker\delta\cap\ker\Delta=k,
\qquad
\operatorname{Frac}(B)^\delta\cap
\operatorname{Frac}(B)^\Delta=k.
\tag{7}
\]

For the field assertion, delta-invariance puts a rational function
F in k(a,b,c). Its Delta-derivative is

\[
F_a+\lambda(aF_b+bF_c).
\tag{8}
\]

As lambda is transcendental, the two coefficients vanish.
The first gives F in k(b,c). Now a is transcendental over that
field, so the second forces F_b=0 and bF_c=0. Characteristic
zero gives F in k. The polynomial statement follows immediately.

Thus the nonsliced top is not caused by insisting on a common
invariant plane of the two original actions: no nonconstant common
rational invariant exists. Its own joint kernel with delta is
k[a,H], but that plane is a different invariant object.

## 4. Finite Lie closure does not repair the highest field automatically

Let B_1=partial_b, J=lambda*partial_c and C_1=partial_c.
The complex Lie algebra generated by delta and Delta is exactly
the span of

\[
\delta,\Delta,L,B_1,J,C_1.
\tag{9}
\]

The nonzero brackets, up to skew symmetry, are

\[
[\delta,\Delta]=L,\quad [\Delta,L]=B_1,\quad
[\Delta,B_1]=-J,\quad [L,B_1]=-C_1,\quad
[\delta,J]=C_1.
\tag{10}
\]

All other brackets between distinct displayed basis elements vanish.
These identities prove closure and show that every basis element
is generated. They are linearly independent: compare successively
the partial_lambda and partial_a coefficients, then the coefficient
of a in the partial_b component, and finally the remaining
partial_b, lambda*partial_c and partial_c components.

The lower central series has dimensions 6,4,3,2,1,0. Explicitly its
successive nonzero commutator spaces after the first are
span{L,B_1,J,C_1}, span{B_1,J,C_1}, span{J,C_1}, and span{C_1}.
It is therefore a six-dimensional nilpotent Lie algebra of class five.
Every constant linear combination in (9) is triangular in the same
variable order, so it too is an LND. This example does not rely on
the infinite-dimensional Lie phenomenon of pass 26.

Even these stronger finiteness properties do not make the primitive
highest adjoint L sliced: its exact plinth is still aC_L.

## 5. One further bracket succeeds, outside the failed rational direction

There is an explicit successful additional step:

\[
[\Delta,L]=\partial_b,
\qquad \partial_b(b)=1,
\qquad [\delta,\partial_b]=0.
\tag{11}
\]

This sliced tangent LND kills lambda and gives the new joint plane
k[a,c] with delta. It does not lie in Frac(B)*L: generically L has
a nonzero partial_c component, whereas partial_b does not.
Consequently the positive repair leaves the rational direction
whose saturation was excluded in Section 2.

The example settles a precise inference negatively: from two sliced
primitive polynomial LNDs, trivial joint rational kernel, and finite
nilpotent Lie closure, one still cannot infer a slice for the
primitive highest adjoint. It does not exclude obtaining a slice
from a different Lie word, as (11) demonstrates.

For a universally quantified unknown kernel K=ker delta, the remaining
problem is to guarantee some usable additional direction and prove
its local nilpotence and slice property. No universal bound on the
number or degree of brackets needed for that purpose is asserted.

## 6. Verification and exact scope

The standalone checker uses only unittest and SymPy. It reconstructs
the two polynomial coordinate inverses, both actual LNDs, the highest
adjoint and its primitive coefficients, the kernel and plinth identities
in finite controls, the joint-kernel coefficient equations, the entire
six-field bracket table, and the additional sliced bracket.

~~~sh
/opt/anaconda3/bin/python research/primitive_top_checks_pass27.py
~~~

The eight methods passed in 0.068 seconds in the recorded local run.
A second agent independently reconstructed the coordinate inverse,
the displayed nonzero brackets, and the joint rational-kernel proof
without finding an algebraic correction.

The all-degree kernel and nonslice conclusions are proved in Sections
2-3; the Lie-algebra assertions follow from the complete bracket table.
They are not extrapolated from sampled polynomial degrees.

Both original fields in (1) have unit plinth, so this is not a
realization of the canonical vertical action whose full plinth is
a prescribed nonunit p. That narrower structural requirement might
support a stronger criterion and remains separate. The example does
meet the stated unrestricted actual-pair assumptions exactly.

The leading-LND extraction itself remains the established argument
recorded in the [moving-plane note](moving-plane-lie-pass26.md).
This countercontrol concerns its additional proposed slice conclusion.
General cancellation, priority, expert review and formal verification
remain unresolved. No registry or old proof is changed.
