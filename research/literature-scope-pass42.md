## Direct theorem checks

The main investigation reread the following exact retained passages
before final synthesis. Independent reconstructions checked their
hypotheses at their actual points of application.

| Established input | Primary location | Hypotheses retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Complex commuting derivations | Maubach, [author manuscript](https://www.math.ru.nl/~maubach/Papers/The_Commuting_Dervations.pdf), Theorem 3.6, PDF p. 5 | Two independent commuting polynomial LNDs of the actual complex polynomial three-ring. The result produces an ambient coordinate, not merely a polynomial abstract joint kernel. |
| Nice-field linear invariant | Dasgupta and Gupta, [1701.03635v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.03635v1), Theorem 3.1(i), PDF p. 5, attributed to Wang | Nonzero LND with second iterate zero on all three actual coordinates. This part requires no slice or irreducibility. |
| Planar triangularization | Dasgupta and Gaifullin, [2306.00510v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.00510v2), Theorem 2.7, PDF p. 6 | A genuine polynomial plane, reached in the earlier single-partial lemma by primitive division. Not an arbitrary invariant surface presumed to be a plane. |
| Original polynomial slice | Same retained text, Theorem 2.5, PDF p. 5 | The original action has a polynomial slice; no slice is transferred to its base restriction or a commuting action. |
| Regular kernel with a fixed coordinate | Bhatwadekar, Gupta and Lokhande, [1501.01438v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.01438v1), Theorem 3.5 and full proof, PDF p. 11 | Nonzero LND of an actual four-ring, fixing an actual ambient coordinate, with regular original kernel. The coordinate is proved first; the original slice gives regularity. |
| Relative cancellation | Dasgupta and Gupta, Theorem 2.6, PDF p. 4, attributed to Hamann | The preceding one-good-partial lemma applies it to its proved isomorphism over the exact base C[p]. No unrestricted threefold cancellation is substituted. |

The companion-image calculation and UFD power law do not invoke a
polynomial curve-factorization theorem. Constancy of component ratios
comes from all ambient partials vanishing, and the common root comes
from every irreducible multiplicity in the original UFD. This avoids
an unnecessary extra coordinate or normalization assumption.

## Bounded current search

Queries covered combinations of "locally nilpotent", "adjoint",
"three variables", "D^3", "four", "rank", "quadratic", "slice",
"degree two", and "quasi-nice". The exact Dasgupta-Gaifullin title
was also searched. These searches recover known rank and centralizer
literature but do not establish that the present conditional statements
are absent from it.

The primary publisher result for Dasgupta and Gaifullin identifies
the published article as *Sbornik: Mathematics* 216:4 (2025), 456-484,
[DOI 10.4213/sm10094e](https://doi.org/10.4213/sm10094e).
Its displayed publication date is June 17, 2025. A later direct DOI
open failed at the redirect; the retained arXiv version, not an
unread publisher theorem, remains the mathematical dependency.
The source's rank-three examples show why an unrestricted
three-variable rank-at-most-two assertion would be false. The new
theorem has three explicit adjoint bounds.

Other results included a primary book-chapter abstract on dimension
three, an A2-fibration abstract with extra slice hypotheses, papers
about images of derivations, and unrelated secondary pages. Abstracts
were treated as leads, not substitutes for theorem statements.
No new classification input was taken from them.

The earlier [Lie-rigidity applicability audit](lie-rigidity-scope-pass41.md)
is unchanged. Local nilpotence of supplied generators is not the
elementwise local finiteness of their entire generated Lie algebra.
The new recognition theorem does not depend on that preprint.
