# Slice-scaling symmetries and a whole-frame extraction obstruction

Date: September 18, 2026.

For a complex affine domain A, all homogeneous locally nilpotent
derivations of A[u] for the grading deg(A)=0, deg(u)=1 can be
classified directly. Their generated group has precisely the product
orbits of the intrinsic special automorphism group of A with the u-line.
Thus the supplied scaling symmetry does not automatically transfer
the transitivity of the full ambient automorphism group to A.

There is a stronger exact control for extracting these symmetries from
an ambient frame. In the actual polynomial frame of643, consider EVERY
homogeneous component of EVERY constant translation direction, retaining
only components that are locally nilpotent. Even after primitive
division, all retained directions are E or D. A saturated relative
derivation module traps their polynomial combinations, brackets,
replicas, primitive divisions, and conjugations by their own flows.
They cannot move a point horizontally on the specified fixed line.

This does not concern all homogeneous LNDs of the ring: other such
derivations exist in this polynomial example and immediately escape.
Nor does it rule out using the original whole coordinate fields in a
nonlinear repair. General cancellation is UNSOLVED; novelty is UNKNOWN.

## 1. Classification for the actual slice-scaling torus

Let A be a finitely generated complex domain, B=A[u], and E=partial_u.
The torus acts on the ring by tau_lambda(a)=a and tau_lambda(u)=lambda u.
It need not be diagonal in a supplied polynomial coordinate frame of B.
An integer-degree derivation delta sends B_i into B_(i+d).

The complete list of homogeneous LNDs is

    d=-1: delta=h E, h in A;
    d>=0: delta=u^d D, D in LND(A), extended to kill u;
    d<-1: delta=0.

Zero fields may be omitted. For d<-1 both delta(A) and delta(u)
vanish by degree. For d=-1, delta(A)=0 and delta(u) belongs to A,
which gives the first family. For d>=0 write

    delta|A=u^d D,    delta(u)=h u^(d+1),    h in A.

An LND on a characteristic-zero domain cannot send a nonzero element
to a nonzero multiple of itself. Indeed its degree function satisfies
deg_delta(rs)=deg_delta(r)+deg_delta(s), whereas a nonzero delta(r)
has degree deg_delta(r)-1. Apply this to r=u to obtain h=0.
Consequently delta^n(a)=u^(dn)D^n(a), so D is an LND. Conversely
this identity proves local nilpotence for every listed u^d D, and
hE is visibly locally nilpotent. No polynomial presentation of A
is used in this classification.

The normalized additive subgroups here include degree-zero subgroups
centralized by the torus. This convention avoids ambiguity in the word
root. If degree zero is excluded from that word, the generated group
below is unchanged: the commuting-bracket identity [E,uD]=D yields
the flow of D as a group commutator of flows of E and uD.

## 2. Exact product orbits, not automatic transitivity

Let G_hom be the group generated by the flows of all the preceding
homogeneous LNDs, and let SAut(A) mean the group generated by all
intrinsic LND flows on Spec(A). On complex points,

    G_hom . (p,t) = (SAut(A) . p) x A1.

One inclusion follows because the degree-zero fields D move p by
any intrinsic additive flow while leaving u fixed, and E translates
u arbitrarily. For the converse, hE leaves p fixed. The flow of u^d D
at a point with u=t acts on the base by the intrinsic flow of D with
parameter multiplied by t^d. Every generator therefore preserves
each displayed product set. Finite compositions do too.

The corresponding invariant ring is exactly

    B^(G_hom)=ML(A).

Invariance under E first places an element in A. Invariance under all
degree-zero D is precisely the defining intersection for ML(A).
Conversely an element of ML(A) is killed by every listed derivation.
Triviality of this invariant ring is not asserted to imply transitivity
for arbitrary A. Under the supplied-pair hypotheses of641 it is a
recognition criterion only because641 separately proves that implication.

For B isomorphic to C^[4], the full ambient special automorphism group
is transitive. This says nothing by itself about the specified subgroup
G_hom. When A is already C^[3], G_hom is indeed transitive, since it
contains the three intrinsic coordinate translations as well as E.

## 3. Reusing the whole frame of643

Now take B=C[a,b,c,u], A=C[a,b,c], and

    D=a partial_b+b partial_c,    E=partial_u,    H=D+E.

Use the actual coordinates

    alpha=a,
    beta=b-au,
    gamma=c-bu+au^2/2,
    F=alpha gamma^2+beta gamma^3+gamma^5,
    U=u+F.

The whole inverse is u=U-F, a=alpha, b=beta+alpha u,
c=gamma+beta u+alpha u^2/2. These are successive polynomial
shears, so both compositions are identities. Their partials are

    T_alpha=(partial_a+u partial_b+u^2 partial_c/2)-gamma^2 H,
    T_beta=(partial_b+u partial_c)-gamma^3 H,
    T_gamma=partial_c-G H,
    T_U=H,
    G=2 alpha gamma+3 beta gamma^2+5 gamma^4.

These entire commuting LNDs and their inverse were already established
in [643](primitive-leading-fixed-locus-frame-obstruction-pass643-working.md).
For constants k1,k2,k3,k4, put delta=sum k_i T_i in this order.
Every nonzero such delta is a coordinate translation direction.
Write delta_j for its degree-j homogeneous component under the
ACTUAL slice grading, not an ambient positive weighted Euler grading.

## 4. Every locally nilpotent component of every constant direction

For j>=0 the component delta_j can be an LND only if

    [u^(j+1)] delta(u)=0.

This follows from Section1, since its value on u would otherwise be
a nonzero multiple of u. Here the whole vertical polynomial is

    delta(u)=k4-k1 gamma^2-k2 gamma^3-k3 G.

For every 0<=r<=2n, the coefficient [u^r]gamma^n is a nonzero
homogeneous polynomial of total (a,b,c)-degree n. To verify nonzero
without sampling, if r<=n use the monomial b^r c^(n-r); if r>=n
use a^(r-n)b^(2n-r). Each occurs with nonzero multinomial coefficient.
Distinct choices of factors give distinct powers of a,b,c, so there
is no hidden cancellation.

Let r=j+1>=1. For r<=8, the base-degree-four part of the vertical
coefficient is -5 k3 [u^r]gamma^4; all other terms have smaller
base degree. Hence k3=0 is necessary. If also r<=6, its remaining
base-degree-three part forces k2=0. If also r<=4, its remaining
base-degree-two part forces k1=0. Beyond their respective ranges
these coefficients vanish identically.

The old horizontal partials displayed in Section3 have u-degree at
most two. After the preceding forced vanishings, the complete list of
NONZERO homogeneous components that can be LNDs is therefore:

| Degree | Component | Necessary restrictions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| -1 | h E | none, with h=[u^0]delta(u) |
| 0 | k4 D | k1=k2=k3=0 |
| 4 | -k1 a^2 u^4 D/4 | k2=k3=0 |
| 6 | -k2 a^3 u^6 D/8 | k3=0 |
| 8 | -5 k3 a^4 u^8 D/16 | none |

There are no components of degree below -1 or above8. For degrees
1,2,3,5,7, a component satisfying the required vertical vanishing is
zero. Every field in the table is genuinely locally nilpotent:
D kills a and u, and E kills h. Thus this is both a necessary and
sufficient classification, not merely a vertical-coefficient test.

Since gcd(a,b)=1, D is primitive. Primitive division in the polynomial
ring B makes every nonzero table entry a nonzero constant multiple
of E or D. In particular, admitting lower components, not just the
highest components treated in643, supplies no new primitive direction.

## 5. Closure under recombination of the extracted directions

Put kappa=2ac-b^2 and define

    M=B D+B E=Der_(C[a,kappa])(B).

For the equality, a relative derivation Y has Y(a)=0 and
a Y(c)=b Y(b). Coprimality of a,b gives Y(b)=a g and Y(c)=b g
for some g in B, while Y(u)=h is arbitrary. Thus Y=gD+hE.
The reverse inclusion follows by differentiating a and kappa.

This description supplies the following exact closure properties.

* M is a B-module and a Lie subalgebra, because its members kill a
  and kappa.
* M is saturated among regular derivations: if a nonzero polynomial
  times Y kills a,kappa, the domain property makes Y kill them too.
  Equivalently, a regular rational combination of D,E already has
  polynomial coefficients, by gcd(a,b)=1.
* Every LND in M has flows fixing a,kappa, so conjugation by these
  flows preserves the relative derivation module M.

Consequently any finite sequence starting with the extracted table
entries and using polynomial combinations, Lie brackets, invariant
replicas, primitive division, or conjugation by flows of LNDs already
obtained in M stays in M. Polynomial combinations or brackets are
not claimed automatically to be LNDs. Whenever a flow is used, its
local nilpotence remains an explicit requirement.

For each c0 in C, every member of M preserves

    J_c0=(a,b,c-c0).

Indeed D sends its displayed generators to 0,a,b and E kills them.
Thus all the allowed flows preserve the individual cylinder line
L_c0={a=b=0,c=c0}. At every point of this line, values of fields
in M are vertical. E is among the extracted components, so the orbit
on L_c0 of the generated flows is exactly that line. The procedure
cannot produce a horizontally tangent direction there.

## 6. What this does not obstruct

The full frame remains invertible everywhere, with determinant one.
Its complete fields have horizontal tangent directions missing from
the selected homogeneous LND components. There is an exact safe repair

    T_gamma+G T_U=partial_c.

It is a whole coordinate shear, hence an LND. Its source T_gamma is
not in M; using it is outside the closure statement. The repaired
partial_c is itself a degree-zero homogeneous LND and moves c on the
line a=b=0. Thus G_hom from Section2 is NOT trapped by this example.
The example distinguishes all normalized additive subgroups from the
subgroups found by decomposing one frame's constant directions.

Likewise no inference is made about arbitrary nonlinear reframing,
conjugation by external automorphisms, or another mechanism for finding
homogeneous LNDs. The original base was polynomial throughout.
