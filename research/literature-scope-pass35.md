# Source comparison and remaining hypotheses

The working target remains complex threefold cancellation. The professor's
intended correction and field are unconfirmed. This comparison supports
bounded deductions; it neither establishes novelty nor changes the status
of the general conjecture.

## Unipotent actions on a cylinder

Michel Brion's [*Homogeneous varieties under split solvable algebraic
groups*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.12452), v2, Theorems 1 and 2 and
Section 5.2, supplies an invariant product open whose fibers are orbits.
In the unipotent case its affine-space factor has no punctured-line
factor. Remark 5.2 also identifies the quotient by the unipotent part.
The main reconstruction directly checked these statements and the full
proof of Theorem 2. The paper does not assert that arbitrary polynomial
vector fields generate a finite-dimensional unipotent group.

The additional deduction in
[rank-one-cylinder-pass35.md](rank-one-cylinder-pass35.md) starts with
such a group as a hypothesis. An invariant function cuts out a principal
product open. The slice translation ensures that its denominator belongs
to the original threefold ring. Affine-plane cancellation over the
quotient field then supplies two intrinsic derivations after denominator
clearing. The existence of the group is not supplied by this deduction.

Dasgupta and Gupta's [*An algebraic characterization of the affine three
space*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.00169), v2, Lemmas 2.2 and 2.5 and
Theorem 2.6, explicitly recall the curve, factorial-surface and rigid-base
inputs used in the two-dimensional reconstruction. The main agent
directly checked those statements. The factorial-surface slice statement
is used over the algebraic closure of the quotient field, preserving its
algebraically closed characteristic-zero hypothesis.

Dutta, Gupta and Lahiri's [*On Separable A2 and A3-forms*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.03777),
v2, Theorem 2.8, explicitly states Kambayashi's triviality theorem for
separably algebraic affine-plane forms over a field. That statement was
directly checked. The original 1975 proof was not newly obtained. No
corresponding theorem for an arbitrary threefold form or a ring-valued
base is being substituted.

Alhajjar's [*LND-Filtrations and Semi-Rigid Domains*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.00445),
Proposition 3.5, concerns isomorphisms of semi-rigid domains themselves.
The rank-one author directly checked the statement and nearby proofs.
It does not imply preservation of the intrinsic filtration by every
automorphism of its cylinder. The relative transfer fixing the cylinder
parameter is proved separately in the new note.

## Exceptional curves and modification towers

The geometry investigation directly checked the theorem/conjecture
distinction in Kaliman's
[Seattle survey](https://www.math.miami.edu/~kaliman/library/seattle.paper.pdf),
Section 10. The normalization result in Theorem 10.1 is not a proof
of componentwise contractibility. Remark 10.2 presents contractibility
as a suspected stronger conclusion. Remark 10.4's simple modification
description is restricted to a line or disjoint lines and does not cover
the present nodal component. The initial main retrieval at a different
author URL failed. The working library URL was subsequently obtained,
and the main agent directly read Theorem 10.1 and Remarks 10.2 and 10.4.

In particular, connectedness of the entire plinth support is not a
permissible shortcut. Primitive triangular actions with a multi-root
coefficient already have disconnected plinth support. The exponent-one
obstruction in the new note is proved algebraically from the retained
exact center, rather than importing the unproved contractibility claim.

## Degree-one pencils

Kuroda's [*The automorphism theorem and additive group actions on the
affine plane*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.04070), Theorem 1.1 and its
complete proof on pages 1-3, was directly checked by the main agent.
The statement supplies an invariant coordinate over arbitrary fields
and explicitly credits the characteristic-zero case to Rentschler.
It is the published planar input to the pencil argument.
The new deduction also requires the unit coefficient ideal
of the entire parameterized pencil, not merely local nilpotence of two
endpoint derivations. Its primitive-generator and denominator steps
must be read with their stated polynomial-ring hypotheses.

Bounded searches combined the terms locally nilpotent derivations with
pencil, linear combinations, simultaneous triangularization and fixed
point free. The returned results did not provide a directly matching
statement that settled the exact deduction. This is a limited search
outcome, not evidence of priority or an exhaustive novelty audit.
No secondary index, informal forum, or other AI-generated research page
was used as an independent mathematical justification.

The July 2026 Gaifullin-Petrov preprint
[*Non-cancellative varieties, maximal tori, and the Makar-Limanov invariant*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13593)
was checked at the primary abstract page. It was already recorded in
the earlier investigation. Its specified trinomial-variety results
are not a theorem about every semi-rigid factorial threefold, nor was
a matching classification of the second-kind Koras-Russell cylinder
established here. The full text was not newly read in this pass.
