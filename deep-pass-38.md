# Coordinate frames and exact polynomial integrability

The complex threefold cancellation problem remains unresolved here.
There are two completed restricted arguments: a supplied low-height
coordinate frame forces at least two independent additive directions
on the actual threefold, and the remaining vanishing-divisor branch
of the fixed polynomial integrability model has an exact constructive
criterion. Neither argument constructs the required frame for an
arbitrary cylinder. No general solution or novelty is claimed.

## 1. The original problem and the precise conclusions

The working algebraic problem is
\[
B=A[s]\simeq\mathbb C[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4],\qquad
E=\partial_s,
\qquad A\stackrel{?}{\simeq}\mathbb C^{[3]}.
\tag{1}
\]
This is the complex, dimension-three interpretation of the supplied
conjecture. The supplied conclusion with dimension one needs correction
when the stated dimension parameter is larger than one; neither that
correction nor the complex-field specialization has been confirmed by
the professor. The arguments below explicitly assume (1).

A locally nilpotent derivation, or LND, is a polynomial vector field
whose sufficiently high iterate kills each polynomial. In characteristic
zero it gives an algebraic additive-group action. An intrinsic LND acts
on the ring A itself. An ambient LND acts on B and need not preserve A.
Independence of such fields below means independence over the fraction
field, not a basis of every tangent space.

| Result | Exact extra hypothesis | Conclusion | Unproved extension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whole-frame theorem | All four actual coordinate partials have double commutator zero with E | Two commuting independent intrinsic LNDs on A | Existence of such a coordinate frame |
| Low augmented-rank lemma | E(J)=J² and rank[v\|J] at most two | E fixes an ambient linear coordinate; under (1), A is polynomial | Extending the rank bound to every sliced action |
| Fixed-model integrability | The specified primitive normal form with p(0)=0 | A finite necessary-and-sufficient criterion and polynomial inverse construction | Transferring that normal form to an unknown A |

The complete arguments are in the [whole-frame proof](research/actual-frame-pass38.md)
and [vanishing-divisor proof](research/vanishing-plinth-integrability-pass38.md).
The [recognition note](research/literature-scope-pass38.md) distinguishes
published criteria from unproved existence assertions.

## 2. A whole coordinate frame gives actual intrinsic actions

Choose a genuine ambient polynomial coordinate system in (1), with
partials T_i. Suppose
\[
[E,[E,T_i]]=0\quad(1\le i\le4),\qquad
S_i=[E,T_i].
\tag{2}
\]
This is a simultaneous condition on the entire coordinate frame.
It is stronger than finding one ambient LND of relative degree one.
The coordinate system must have a polynomial inverse; a constant
Jacobian determinant alone does not certify it.

The Jacobi identity applied twice to [T_i,T_j]=0 gives [S_i,S_j]=0.
Each S_i is locally nilpotent by a uniform conjugation argument.
For fixed f, choose N so that T_i^N kills every coefficient of the
finite polynomial exp(-tE)f. Then
\[
(T_i+tS_i)^Nf=\exp(tE)T_i^N\exp(-tE)f=0.
\tag{3}
\]
The coefficient of t^N is S_i^Nf. This argument does not assume
that a limit of arbitrary LNDs is locally nilpotent.

Since [E,S_i]=0, every S_i preserves ker E=A and restricts directly
to an LND on A. Put v=(E(x_i)) and J=Jac(v), and let q be the rank
of the matrix with columns v and J. Its columns are, up to signs,
the fields E,S_1,...,S_4. If q is at least three, choose two S_i
independent modulo E. A relation between their restrictions to A
would make their ambient combination a multiple of E on Frac(A)(s),
contradicting that independence. Their restrictions commute.

Thus the higher-rank branch needs no homogeneous-space theorem or
descent through an auxiliary field extension. It uses the actual
centralizing fields already supplied by (2).

## 3. The low augmented-rank argument

The remaining branch has q at most two. The coordinate calculation
in (2) is exactly
\[
E(J)=J^2,\qquad E^m(J)=m!J^{m+1},\qquad E(v)=Jv.
\tag{4}
\]
Local nilpotence on all entries makes J nilpotent over Frac(B).
If J²v were nonzero, the nilpotent cyclic chain v,Jv,J²v would
contain three independent vectors in the span of [v|J]. Therefore
J²v=0 and E²(v)=0.

If E(v) is nonzero, v and E(v) are independent. The rational map
x to [v(x)] in projective three-space has differential rank q-1,
at most one. Along one actual orbit with independent values, its
image is dense in the projective line generated by
v(a)+tE(v)(a). The irreducible projective image closure therefore
contains that line and, having dimension at most one, equals it.
Hence v takes values
in a fixed two-dimensional complex vector subspace and has
nonzero constant linear annihilators.

If E(v)=0 instead, x+v(x) is a quasi-translation. Choose
d at least max(1,deg v) and introduce one auxiliary variable z.
The homogeneous map
\[
\widetilde v(x,z)=(z^dv(x/z),0)
\tag{5}
\]
is a quasi-translation, as in de Bondt's Proposition 2.5.
Its complete Jacobian, with u=x/z, is
\[
\operatorname{Jac}\widetilde v
=z^{d-1}\begin{pmatrix}J(u)&d\,v(u)-J(u)u\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{6}
\]
Its rank is exactly rank[J|v], not merely rank J. The last column
is essential. The homogeneous rank-one and rank-two results in
de Bondt's Theorem 3.6, attributed there to Gordan and Nöther,
give at least two independent linear invariants in dimension five.
One is z; another has nonzero x-linear part. Setting z=1 gives
a nonzero linear form killed by E.[^1]

In either branch E therefore fixes an ambient coordinate. The kernel
A is regular because A[s] is regular. The fixed-coordinate regular-kernel
theorem of Bhatwadekar, Gupta and Lokhande, Theorem 3.5, gives
\(A\simeq\mathbb C^{[3]}\). This published input retains both its
fixed-coordinate and regularity hypotheses.[^2]

The auxiliary homogenizing variable in (5) is only a proof device.
It does not replace the original one-line cylinder by a different
stable-polynomiality problem.

## 4. What the frame theorem does not establish

Combining the two branches proves that a supplied frame satisfying
(2) forces two commuting independent intrinsic LNDs on A. In
particular, a hypothetical A with only one generic intrinsic
additive direction must fail (2) in every ambient coordinate frame.
There is no argument here that such a frame must exist.

Even the constructed restrictions need not be everywhere nonzero.
For the actual sliced action
\[
E=\partial_w+x^2\partial_y+3x^2y\partial_z,
\quad a=y-x^2w,
\quad b=z-3x^2yw+\tfrac32x^4w^2,
\tag{7}
\]
the invariant ring is \(\mathbb C[x,a,b]\). Two first commutators restrict to
\(-2x\partial_a-6xa\partial_b\) and \(-3x^2\partial_b\). Their determinant
in the a,b directions is 6x³. They commute and are generically
independent, but both vanish on x=0. Generic independence is
therefore not an automatic slice or a tangent-frame certificate.

Nor does (2) force J²=0: example (7) has E(J)=J² nonzero and
q=3. The stronger vanishing was used only in the separate
quasi-translation branch. These distinctions prevent two tempting
but invalid shortcuts to general cancellation.

## 5. Exact integrability at the vanishing divisor

The second result concerns a fixed polynomial ring, not an unspecified
threefold. Let
\[
R=\mathbb C[x,\lambda,b,c],\quad h=x^2c-b^2,\quad
F=p(x)b+Q(x,h),\quad L=F_c\partial_b-F_b\partial_c,
\tag{8}
\]
with L primitive, p=x^eu, e at least one, and u(0) nonzero.
For nonzero g(x,T), define G by G_T=g and G(x,0)=0. Put
\(K=h+\lambda G(x,F)\), \(v=1+\lambda x\), and
\(\mathcal D=x^2\partial_b+2b\partial_c+\lambda g(x,F)L\).

Here p is the factor in \(\mathcal D(F)=x^2p\). It is not asserted
to generate the full plinth ideal of L. A canonical realization
means an actual polynomial change of coordinates fixing \(x,\lambda\),
equal to the identity at \(\lambda=0\), which conjugates \(\mathcal D\)
to \(x^2\partial_w+2v^2w\partial_z\).

Write Q_0=Q(0,T) and g=g_0+xg_1+O(x²). Such a realization exists
if and only if
\[
\operatorname{rad}(p)\mid g\text{ coefficientwise},\qquad
Q_0=q_0+q_1T,\quad q_1\ne0,\qquad
g_1=\frac{2}{3q_1}.
\tag{9}
\]
There is no extra bound on higher x-jets, beyond the stated
primitivity and divisibility assumptions. Every realization has
\(W=b-(2/3)\lambda xb\) modulo \(x^2\).

The [preceding exact criterion](research/q1-integrability-pass36.md)
reduces realizability to a polynomial correction U satisfying
\[
N=F-Q(x,K)+\lambda U(x,\lambda,K)\in pR,\quad W=N/p,
\qquad K+v^2W^2\in\mathbb C[x,\lambda]+x^2R.
\tag{10}
\]
It includes a genuine global inverse argument. Primitivity first
forces Q_0 nonconstant. The even and odd parts of (10) at x=0
then force g_0=0 and W=b there. The nonzero roots of p impose
the other radical factors through the earlier coefficient-ideal
obstruction.

To retain the first jet after dividing by p, invert the polynomial
map \(T+\lambda G(x,Q(x,T))\) modulo \(x^{e+2}\), not merely modulo \(x^e\).
It is the identity modulo x, so finite nilpotent iteration gives
a polynomial inverse psi. The correction
\(U_*=(Q(x,T)-Q(x,\psi(T)))/\lambda\) gives
\(W_*=b-x\lambda b Q_0'(-b^2)g_1(Q_0(-b^2))\) modulo \(x^2\).
The next Taylor error has order at least 2e+1, sufficient even
when e=1, where this lower bound equals the discarded order e+2.

Every other admissible correction differs at first order by
\(x\lambda J(\lambda,-b^2)\). With \(H=G_1\circ Q_0\),
the even part of the remaining congruence is
\[
H(T)+2T H'(T)-2T\in\mathbb C.
\tag{11}
\]
Its coefficient multipliers are 2j+1. Thus H=h_0+(2/3)T in
all degrees, forcing the two linearity and slope conditions in
(9). The odd part kills J. This proves necessity for every
correction, not just a chosen finite ansatz.

For sufficiency, retain \(U_*\) modulo \(x^{e+2}\), and invert the same
curve map modulo u. Radical divisibility makes the coefficients of
its difference from T nilpotent in C[x]/(u), even at repeated roots.
The Chinese remainder
theorem combines the corrections because \(x^{e+2}\) and u are coprime.
The resulting W and Z=(K+C+v²W²)/x² are polynomial, where
\(C=-(2q_0/(3q_1))\lambda x\). The inverse theorem behind (10)
finishes the global construction.

## 6. Nonemptiness and the nonsliced-top obstruction

Every prescribed p=x^eu occurs. For example, choose Q=T and
g=(2/3)x rad(u)(x)/rad(u)(0). For p=x^e there are explicit
closed-form inverse formulas. Set \(t=(2/3)\lambda x\), \(n=e+2\),
\(A_n=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}(-t)^j\), \(B_n=1-tA_n\), and
\(C_n=(-t)^n/x^e\). The last expression is polynomial. Then
\[
W=B_nb+C_nh,\qquad K=tx^eb+(1+t)h,\qquad
Z=(K+v^2W^2)/x^2.
\tag{12}
\]
The matrix on (b,h) has determinant one. Its inverse gives
b=(1+t)w-C_nI and h=-tx^ew+B_nI, where I=x²z-v²w².
The numerator h+b² is divisible by x² because \(3t=2\lambda x\),
so c=(h+b²)/x² is polynomial. Both physical coordinate
directions are verified, not merely their localized Jacobians.

Nevertheless every primitive top in (9) is nonsliced: modulo x,
\(L=2q_1b\partial_c\), so it has fixed points on x=b=0. No polynomial
can have derivative one under L. The classification completes the
vanishing branch of the prescribed normal form; it does not select
a sliced top for an unknown cylinder.

## 7. Current recognition theorems and remaining obligations

A recent exact certificate is available for sparse retractions.
Chakraborty and Pal's August 2026 revision proves that a binomial
retraction of a polynomial ring over a UFD has polynomial image,
Corollary 6.11(ii). Applied to the Taylor retraction pi=exp(-sE),
it recognizes A if every coordinate image has at most two terms.
It does not construct such coordinates.[^3]

For this particular retraction, even one zero or monomial image
is sufficient. A constant image makes s a coordinate by irreducibility;
a nonconstant monomial in ker E forces an occurring ambient
coordinate into ker E by factorial closure. Thus a hypothetical
counterexample must have at least two terms in each image and
at least three in some image, in every coordinate frame.

Another equivalent existence problem is to find a nonzero intrinsic LND
D whose actual kernel inclusion K into A has a retraction.
Chakraborty, Dasgupta, Dutta and Gupta's Corollary 4.3 gives
A=K[t]; their dimension-two retract theorem then makes K
polynomial. This cannot be required of every intrinsic action:
for \(D=x\partial_y+2y\partial_z\) on \(\mathbb C[x,y,z]\), the kernel
C[x,xz-y²] is not a retract, since reduction modulo x would
require a polynomial square to equal minus its indeterminate.[^4]

The remaining central obligation is still existential: construct a
useful actual coordinate frame or intrinsic action from (1), with
no assumed polynomial presentation of A. A bounded next step is
to test a proposed coordinate-preserving reduction of adjoint
height and prove what invariant it decreases. A minimum-degree
frame alone supplies no decreasing move. The completed normal-form
branch and the previously closed rank-two candidate family should
not be reopened without a concrete defect.
